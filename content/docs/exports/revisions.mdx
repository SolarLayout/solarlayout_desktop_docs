---
title: Issuing a revision
description: What issuing a package does - purposes, revision schemes, the record kept in the project, and where the revision prints.
---

Issuing writes the package with a revision and a purpose stamped on every sheet and
page, and keeps a record of that revision in the project file. It is the primary
button in [Export deliverables](/docs/exports/deliverables), **Issue A for review**.
Use it when the package leaves your hands. Use **Export draft package** for anything
else: a draft never uses up a revision.

Issuing depends on three things: a current layout, no run in progress, and a saved
project. **No design check holds it back.** You can issue with checks failed or not yet
checkable, and the package carries no check result. See
[Design checks](/docs/workspace/checks).

## Draft and issue

| | Draft | Issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title block REV | **—** | **A** |
| Title block STATUS | DRAFT | FOR REVIEW, FOR APPROVAL or FOR CONSTRUCTION |
| D0's band | *"Draft · not issued"*, *"Not for construction."* | *"FOR REVIEW"*, *"Issued 5 Nov 2026 by AC."* |
| Design report footer | *"Rev — · DRAFT"* | The revision and purpose |
| Kept in the project | Nothing | The record, and the design as it was |
| Saves the project | No | Yes, before writing and after |

## The Issue card

The card shows on the right of the window while the layout is current.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | **For review** (default), **For approval** or **For construction** |
| **Revision** | Read only: the next revision and today's date, such as *"A · 5 Nov 2026"* |
| **Description** | Optional, at most 120 characters. What this issue is for, in one line: *"Tender set"*, *"Client comments of 12 Oct applied"* |
| **Issued by** | Initials, at most 8 characters. Fills from the company's initials, and prints as Drawn when Drawn is empty. Empty, the window says *"Initials are needed."* |

The primary button follows the purpose. Its **▾** lists *"Issue A for review"*, *"Issue
A for approval"* and *"Issue A for construction"*: choosing one sets the purpose and
issues at once.

## Issue a package

<Steps>
<Step>
### Save the project

The issued revision is kept in the project file, so a project never saved can't issue:
*"Save the project first: the issued revision is kept in it."* See
[Projects](/docs/projects).
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the card

Pick the **Purpose**, add a **Description** if it helps, and check **Issued by**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Click the primary button

The project is saved, the package is written with the revision and purpose, and a
record is added and saved. A failed first save stops here, with **The project could not
be saved**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Send the folder

The banner confirms it: *"A issued for review · 5 Nov 2026 · AC"*. **Open folder** opens
the package.
</Step>
</Steps>

Two failures can follow the write, and each says what stands:

- *"A was written but could not be recorded in the project"*.
- *"A is recorded, but the project could not be saved"*, with the reason and *"Save it
  with File ▸ Save project to keep A."*

## Revision schemes

The company chooses the scheme, in **Revisions** on [Company](/docs/exports/company).

| Scheme | How it counts | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A, B, C…** (default) | One past the newest, whatever the purpose. The first is A and Z is the last | A, B, C |
| **P01 / C01** | Review and approval count P, construction counts C | P01, C01, then P02 on the next review |

Under P01 / C01 the menu reads *"Issue P01 for review"*, *"Issue P01 for approval"* and
*"Issue C01 for construction"*.

If you change the scheme after issuing A, the letters on record stay and the next issue
is P01.

## Where the revision prints

- **Every title block**, in REV and STATUS.
- **D0**, in its status band and revision history.
- **The design report**, on the cover, in the running footer and in section 12.1.
- **The schedules workbook**, in the README's Revision, Status and Issued rows.
- **The BOM workbook**, in its header's Revision and Issued rows.
- **The manifest.**
- **The folder name**, such as `AUD · Rev A · For review · 2026-11-05`.

The revision history has the columns Rev, Date, Purpose, Description, Drawn, Checked and
Approved. The description reaches the history and the manifest, never a folder name.

## What the project keeps

Each issue keeps its revision, purpose, date, initials, description, drawn, checked and
approved, the manifest, and a frozen copy of the design as it was issued. The **Revisions**
card lists them in the window, one line each: *"A · For review · 5 Nov 2026 · AB · Tender
set"*.

Each kept revision grows the project file. It is a record of what you sent: no screen opens
it and nothing writes a package again from it, so keep the folder you sent as well.
