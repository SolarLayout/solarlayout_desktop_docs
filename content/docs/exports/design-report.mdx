---
title: The design report
description: R1, the package's A4 PDF - its twelve sections, what it says where an input is missing, and what it excludes.
---

The design report, R1 in the package, writes the design up as an A4 PDF: a cover
and twelve sections, each with a bookmark. It is the **Design report** row in
[Export deliverables](/docs/exports/deliverables), and it is written from the same
snapshot as the drawings and the schedules.

Every figure in it comes from the design. Where an input is missing, the report
says *Not designed* in words rather than leaving a gap.

## What it contains

| Section | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Cover | The project, its headlines, the status, and the document's number and sign-offs |
| **1** Summary | The headline figures, what was designed, and a key plan |
| **2** Site | 2.1 Location, 2.2 Boundary, 2.3 Land account |
| **3** Design basis | 3.1 Module, 3.2 Inverter, 3.3 Table configuration, 3.4 Pitch, tilt and string sizing: the working, 3.5 Design settings, stated as defaults, 3.6 Design limits and their sources |
| **4** Plant configuration | 4.1 Inverter variants, 4.2 Grouping, 4.3 MV collection and evacuation |
| **5** Block schedule | Every per-block figure |
| **6** Cables and trenches | 6.1 Totals by class, 6.2 Voltage drop, 6.3 Longest feeders or DC trunks |
| **7** Civil and other systems | |
| **8** Earthing | 8.1 Inputs, 8.2 Conductor sizing, 8.3 Earth grid check, 8.4 Quantities |
| **9** Energy | 9.1 Basis, 9.2 Monthly, 9.3 Losses |
| **10** Assumptions, exclusions and open items | What the design assumes, what it leaves out and what remains open |
| **11** Bill of materials | The same lines, quantities and open items as the [BOM workbook](/docs/bom/overview); nothing is recomputed |
| **12** Revision history and drawing register | The revisions, the sheets, and what is in the package |

## Where an input is missing

The report keeps its sections and states what it can't give.

- **No inverter file (OND).** Inverter AC and DC : AC read *"Not designed"*.
- **No energy run.** Section 9 stays and says energy was not calculated for this
  design, and that the section prints once the yield has been run. See
  [Energy yield](/docs/energy/overview).
- **Soil not measured.** Section 8 says the soil value is assumed, and that the
  grid check stands once a soil test confirms it. See
  [Earthing design](/docs/simulation/earthing).

## What it excludes

Section 10.2 lists three fixed exclusions:

- Costs, CAPEX and LCOE.
- A formal yield assessment. The figures in section 9 are indicative.
- Structural design of the mounting structure and the buildings.

## On a central inverter plant

The report uses the plant's own terms: *table to SMB*, *LV, central inverter to
IDT*, section 6.3 *Longest DC trunks*, and section 4.2 *Grouping: SMB → central
inverter → IDT → MV*.

## Draft or issued

A draft's running footer reads *"Rev — · DRAFT"*, and the cover says DRAFT and that
it is not for construction. An issue prints its revision and purpose on the cover
and in the footer, and adds a row to section 12's revision history. See
[Issuing a revision](/docs/exports/revisions).

The report's design checks stay in the application: no check result prints in it.
See [Design checks](/docs/workspace/checks).

The PDF's own properties name it for a document system: the title is *"&lt;project&gt; ·
Design report"*, the subject gives its number, and the author is the company.
