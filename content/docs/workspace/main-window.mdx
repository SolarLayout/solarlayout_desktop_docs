The main window has three parts. A navy **ribbon** runs across the top. The
**left column** holds the pinned **Generate Layout** button and, under it,
every design input on five stage tabs. The **right side** shows the plant —
the drawing, the summary sheet, the energy result, the bill of materials and
the single-line diagram — as five views of the same design, with a row of
plant totals under whichever view is up.

<Screenshot id="main-window-empty" />

Above the ribbon sits a conventional menu bar — **File**, **Edit-Pile** and
**Help** — for projects, the pile editor, the in-app guide and the licence
window. Below everything, a status bar reports what the application is doing.

## The ribbon

<Screenshot id="ribbon" />

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **SolarLayout** | The brand. The window title beside it names the design, for example *SolarLayout  [Fixed Tilt  \|  String Inverter]*. |
| The **design chip** | Reads the design this window holds — *Fixed tilt · String inverter ▾*, *Tracker · Central inverter ▾* and so on. Clicking it opens the New design window and launches a second, independent window for the design you choose there; this window stays open. See [Choosing a design mode](/docs/design-modes). |
| The status mirror | A gold italic line that repeats the status bar's message, so a result is readable without looking down. |
| The **access chip** | **● Active — until \<date>** when this device has access, with *(offline)* appended while a cached check is in use; **● Access expired** or **● Access revoked** in red with a **Contact Us** button; **● Access status unavailable** when the server cannot be reached. With no access at all the chip is hidden and a gold **Get Free Access** button stands in its place. See [Activate your licence](/docs/licence/activate). |
| **Docs** | Opens this documentation site in your browser. |
| **Support** | Opens the **Support / Feedback** window. See [Getting help](/docs/support). |

## The left column

### Generate Layout

The gold **Generate Layout** button (**F5**) is pinned above the tabs, so it
is in the same place whichever tab you are on. Under it a hint line says why
it is disabled — *"Select a boundary file on the Site tab to generate a
layout."* until a boundary file is chosen. Generating needs active access for
this device; without it the License / Subscription window opens instead.

### The five stage tabs

The inputs are split across five tabs, in the order the design is normally
worked through. Every card on a tab is a collapsible group: the ⊖ button in
its header folds it away, and every card opens expanded.

| Tab | Cards, top to bottom | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Site** | **Input Boundary File** · **Site Parameters** · **Structures & Shadow (keep-clear)** · **Topography (slope-aware exclusion)** | The boundary file; roads, arresters, ICR blocks and the transmission line corridor; keep-clear shadows; terrain — [Boundary files](/docs/inputs/boundary-files), [Site parameters](/docs/inputs/site) |
| **Array** | A read-only **MOUNTING** chip · **Module Specifications** · then **MMS-Table Configuration** and **Spacing & Tilt** on a fixed-tilt design, or **Tracker Configuration** on a tracker | The module, the table or tracker geometry, tilt and pitch — [Module](/docs/inputs/module), [Table configuration](/docs/inputs/table), [Tracker](/docs/inputs/tracker) |
| **Electrical** | **String Inverter** (or **SMB – String Monitoring Box** on a central-inverter design) · **Cables** | Strings per inverter, the inverter file, and cable calculation with its allowances — [Inverter](/docs/inputs/inverter), [Cables](/docs/inputs/cables) |
| **Yield** | **Run Energy Calculation** · **Energy Yield** | The energy run, the weather source, site conditions, losses, degradation and exceedance probabilities — [How yield is calculated](/docs/energy/overview) |
| **Tools** | **Obstructions** · **Main Control Room & Objects** · **Studies** | Editing a generated layout: hand-drawn obstructions, the main control room and placed objects, and the three studies — [Obstructions](/docs/layout/obstructions), [Site equipment](/docs/layout/site-equipment), [Sizing from an AC capacity](/docs/simulation/ac-capacity) |

The **MOUNTING** chip on the Array tab reads *Fixed-tilt MMS tables* or
*Single-axis tracker*. It is a reminder, not a control: the mounting was
chosen in the New design window, and a different mounting means another
window.

The **Tools** tab is locked until a layout exists — its tooltip reads
*"Generate a layout first"* and every button on it is disabled. Between the
Obstructions card and the Main Control Room card it carries the reminder
*"Tip: Click and drag a blue ICR to reposition it."*, which is how a control
room is moved: with no navigation tool active, drag it to a valid spot inside
the perimeter road and release.

### The mode banner

Three tools take the whole left column over while they are on: **Sketch**
(hand editing of the drawing), and the **SLD** and **BOM** views. Each replaces
the pinned button with a coloured banner — orange **● Sketch Mode**, blue
**● SLD Preparation**, green **● Bill of Materials** — carrying its own
**Exit** button, and swaps the stage tabs for the tool's palette. Leaving the
mode gives the column back exactly as it was. See
[Sketch Mode](/docs/editing/sketch-mode), [Single-line diagram](/docs/sld/overview)
and [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview).

## The right side

Five tabs across the top — **Layout · Summary · Energy · BOM · SLD** — are
views of one plant, and each takes the whole area under the header. To the
right of the tabs sits the row of tools for the current view: **Save project**
and **Export ▾** always; the drawing's view toggles, the **Layers ▾** popover,
**Sketch**, the navigation icons and the expand button on the Layout view;
**Copy** and **Open in window** on Summary and Energy. Under every view runs a
single row of **plant chips** — the plant totals, and the only place they are
shown.

[Views, layers and tools](/docs/workspace/views) covers each view, every tool
and the Layers rows in full.

## The status bar

| Left | Right |
| --- | --- |
| Progress and result messages — *"Calculating layout…"*, *"Layout ready"* with the notes that apply, *"KMZ exported: …"*, *"Project saved (full): …"* and the like. The ribbon's status mirror repeats them. | The live **cursor position** over the drawing, in UTM metres, and the **application version** (for example *v1.2.0*) — the number to quote when you report a problem. |

The status bar never carries the plant totals; those are in the plant chips.
