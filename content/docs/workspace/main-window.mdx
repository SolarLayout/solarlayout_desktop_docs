The main window has three parts. A navy **ribbon** runs across the top. The
**left column** holds the pinned **Generate Layout** button and, under it,
every design input on five stage tabs. The **right side** shows the plant —
the drawing, the summary sheet, the design checks, the workings, the energy
result, the bill of materials and the single-line diagram — as seven views of
the same design, with a row of plant totals under whichever view is up.

<Screenshot id="main-window-empty" />

Above the ribbon sits a conventional menu bar, and below everything a status
bar reports what the application is doing.

## The menu bar

Four menus: **File · Edit-Pile · Tools · Help**.

| Menu | Items |
| --- | --- |
| **File** | **New Project** (**Ctrl+Shift+N**) · **Move to String / Central Window…** (**Ctrl+N**) · **Save Project…** (**Ctrl+S**) · **Open Project…** (**Ctrl+O**) · **Open Sample Site** · **Company…** · **Advanced settings…** · **Exit** (**Ctrl+Q**) — see [Saving and reopening projects](/docs/projects), [Company](/docs/exports/company) and [Advanced settings](/docs/workspace/advanced-settings) |
| **Edit-Pile** | **Define Pile Layout…** — see [Piles](/docs/editing/piles) |
| **Tools** | **Robot count from DXF…** · **Earthing Design…** |
| **Help** | **How to Use This Tool** (**F1**) · **License / Subscription…** — see [Getting help](/docs/support) |

**Advanced settings…** reads *"Advanced settings… (N changed)"* while the
project has settings off their defaults.

The **Tools** menu repeats two of the studies from the Tools tab, and each item
is enabled when its button is. **Robot count from DXF…** works at any time, with
or without a layout — it reads a plant drawing of its own. **Earthing Design…**
works on the generated layout and is disabled until one exists. Neither has a
shortcut.

## The left column

### Generate Layout

The gold **Generate Layout** button (**F5**) is pinned above the tabs, so it
is in the same place whichever tab you are on. Under it a hint line says why
it is disabled — *"Select a boundary file on the Site tab to generate a
layout."* until a boundary file is chosen.

Once a layout exists, changing any input it was built from makes it out of
date. The button then reads **Regenerate Layout**, with a second line inside
it naming the change — *"Module wattage changed since the last run"*, or
*"2 inputs changed since the last run"* — and keeps its height. The changed
fields turn amber, and a small amber dot follows the name of each stage tab
that holds a change. Change the value back and all of it goes away. See
[Out-of-date results](/docs/workspace/out-of-date-results).

### The five stage tabs

The inputs are split across five tabs, in the order the design is normally
worked through. Every card on a tab is a collapsible group: the ⊖ button in
its header folds it away, and every card opens expanded.

| Tab | Cards, top to bottom | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Site** | **Input Boundary File** · **Site Parameters** · **Structures & Shadow (keep-clear)** · **Topography (slope-aware exclusion)** | The boundary file; roads, arresters, ICR blocks, the line corridor and the area setback; keep-clear shadows; terrain — [Boundary files](/docs/inputs/boundary-files), [Site parameters](/docs/inputs/site) |
| **Array** | A read-only **MOUNTING** chip · **Module Specifications** · then **MMS-Table Configuration** and **Spacing & Tilt** on a fixed-tilt design, or **Tracker Configuration** on a tracker | The module, the table or tracker geometry, tilt and pitch — [Module](/docs/inputs/module), [Table configuration](/docs/inputs/table), [Tracker](/docs/inputs/tracker) |
| **Electrical** | **String Inverter** (or **SMB – String Monitoring Box** on a central-inverter design) · **ACCB & Transformer** (or **Transformer** on a central-inverter design) · **Cables** | Strings per inverter and the inverter file; how inverters group into AC combiner boxes and transformers; cable calculation with its allowances — [Inverter](/docs/inputs/inverter), [ACCBs and transformers](/docs/inputs/accb-and-transformers), [Cables](/docs/inputs/cables) |
| **Yield** | **Run Energy Calculation** · **Energy Yield** | The energy run, the weather source, site conditions, losses, degradation and exceedance probabilities — [How yield is calculated](/docs/energy/overview) |
| **Tools** | **Obstructions** · **Grid connection & Objects** · **Studies** | Editing a generated layout — hand-drawn obstructions, the main control room and placed objects — and the five studies — [Obstructions](/docs/layout/obstructions), [Site equipment](/docs/layout/site-equipment) |

The **MOUNTING** chip on the Array tab reads *Fixed-tilt MMS tables* or
*Single-axis tracker*. It is a reminder, not a control: the mounting was
chosen in the New design window, and a different mounting means another
window.

### The Tools tab

The **Tools** tab opens at any time. Its buttons are greyed out until a layout
exists, except **Robot count from DXF…**, which needs no layout, and, once a
boundary is chosen, **Place Object**.

<Screenshot id="tab-tools-locked" />

Once a run has placed control rooms, the tip *"Tip: In Sketch Mode, drag a blue
ICR to move it."* appears between the **Obstructions** card and the **Grid
connection & Objects** card. The Layout view itself is read-only: outside Sketch
Mode a drag on a building pans the view.

The **Studies** card holds five buttons, in this order:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **🔆 Simulation with AC Capacity** | Sizes the plant from a target AC capacity and DC/AC ratio, then rebuilds the layout at the resulting DC capacity — [Sizing from an AC capacity](/docs/simulation/ac-capacity) |
| **🤖 Robotic Module Cleaning** | Counts the cleaning lines and sizes the cleaning-robot fleet for this layout — [Robotic module cleaning](/docs/simulation/robotic-cleaning) |
| **Robot count from DXF…** | Sizes a cleaning-robot fleet from any plant layout drawing, with no layout needed — [Robot count from DXF](/docs/simulation/robot-count-from-dxf) |
| **🌓 Shadow View (row spacing)** | Opens the row-spacing shadow cross-section — [Row-to-row shading](/docs/energy/shading) |
| **⚡ Earthing Design** | The earthing calculation, plan, report and bill of quantities for this layout — [Earthing design](/docs/simulation/earthing) |

All five are there from the start. The four that work on the layout enable at
the end of a run that produced one; **Robot count from DXF…** is always
enabled.

### The mode banner

Three tools take the whole left column over while they are on: **Sketch**
(hand editing of the drawing), and the **SLD** and **BOM** views. Each replaces
the pinned button with a coloured banner — orange **● Sketch Mode**, blue
**SLD Preparation**, green **Bill of Materials** — carrying its own way out, and
swaps the stage tabs for the tool's own panel. Leaving the mode gives the column
back. See
[Sketch Mode](/docs/editing/sketch-mode), [Single-line diagram](/docs/sld/overview)
and [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview).

## The right side

Seven tabs across the top — **Layout · Summary · Checks · Workings · Energy ·
BOM · SLD** — are views of one plant, and each takes the whole area under the
header. To the right of the tabs sits the row of tools for the current view:
**Save project** and **Export ▾** always; the drawing's view toggles, the
**Layers ▾** popover, **Sketch**, the navigation icons and the expand button on
the Layout view; **Open in window** on Summary and Energy; **Copy** on Energy.
Under every view runs a
single row of **plant chips** — the plant totals, and the only place they are
shown.

[Views, layers and tools](/docs/workspace/views) covers each view, every tool
and the Layers rows in full.

## The status bar

| Left | Right |
| --- | --- |
| Progress and result messages — *"Calculating layout…"*, *"Layout ready"* with the notes that apply, *"Project saved (full): …"* and the like. While the layout is out of date it reads *"Out of date: … Regenerate the layout to update results and exports."* | The live **cursor position** over the drawing, and the **application version** — the number to quote when you report a problem. |

The cursor position is in UTM metres for a Google Earth file or a CAD drawing
located by its own coordinates, and in the drawing's own coordinates for a CAD
drawing or an image placed by hand or loaded as layout only — see
[CAD drawing boundaries](/docs/inputs/cad-boundary). The status bar never
carries the plant totals; those are in the plant chips.
