title: Views, layers and tools
description: The five views of the plant, the tools beside the view tabs, the Layers popover, the plant chips, and the Summary and Energy views.
---

The right side of the main window shows one plant five ways. The **Layout**
view is the drawing; **Summary** is the per-plant figures as a sheet;
**Energy** is the yield result; **BOM** is the bill of materials; **SLD** is
the single-line diagram. They are tabs across the top of the right side, and
each takes the whole area under the header.

<Screenshot id="view-header" />

Switch with a click or with **Ctrl+1** to **Ctrl+5** in tab order. The Energy
tab reads *"Energy •"* when a result landed while another view was up.

## The tools row

The tools sit to the right of the tabs and change with the view.

| Tool | View | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Save project** | every view | Writes the session to a project file — [Saving and reopening projects](/docs/projects) |
| **Export ▾** | every view | The export menu: the Google Earth file, the CAD drawing, the ICR-block drawing set and PDF, the cable schedule, the Word report and the plot as an image. Every item is disabled while the layout is out of date — [The project report](/docs/exports/project-report) |
| **Satellite** | Layout, SLD | An aerial photograph behind the layout, aligned to the site's position. Needs an internet connection; disabled until a layout exists. Refused on a site loaded as layout only, which has no position — *"Satellite imagery needs the site's location."* |
| **Wireframe** | Layout, SLD | Draws tables or tracker units as outlines only |
| **Piles: OFF** / **Piles: ON** | Layout, SLD | The pile overlay. The first use opens the pile editor; disabled until a layout exists — [Piles](/docs/editing/piles) |
| **Layers ▾** | Layout, SLD | The popover of drawing layers, below |
| **Sketch** | Layout, SLD | Switches Sketch Mode on — [Sketch Mode](/docs/editing/sketch-mode). Leaving the Layout view switches it off again |
| **Home**, **Pan**, **Zoom** | Layout, SLD | The three navigation tools. **Home** fits the whole plant |
| **⛶** | Layout, SLD | Opens the plot in the Expanded View window, below |
| **Copy** | Summary, Energy | Puts the sheet on the clipboard as tab-separated text |
| **Open in window** | Summary, Energy | Opens the sheet in its own window, which follows the layout |

### Zooming and the persistent view

The mouse wheel zooms at the cursor in every view but SLD. Whatever framing
you set stays through redraws — a layer toggle, a moved control room, a
drawn obstruction — until you click **Home**, generate a layout, or load a
boundary file. There are no back and forward arrows; the wheel and **Home**
are the way about.

### When the window is narrow

Every tool stays reachable at every width. When **Satellite**, **Wireframe**
and **Piles** no longer fit beside the tabs, they move to the top of the
Layers popover as ON/OFF rows — *Satellite image*, *Wireframe tables*,
*Piles* — and the button reads **Layers ▾ +3**.

### The Expanded View window

**⛶** opens **SolarLayout — Expanded View**: the plot alone in a large,
resizable window with a full toolbar and a **⟵ Return to Main Window** button.
While it is open the main window's plot area reads *"Plot is open in the
Expanded View window. Close that window to return the plot here."*

<Screenshot id="expanded-view" />

## The Layers popover

**Layers ▾** opens a small popover of ON/OFF pills. Rows in order, with the
state each starts in after a layout:

| Row | Default | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plant layout** | ON | The placed tables and equipment. OFF leaves only the boundary and obstacles |
| **Legend** | OFF | The legend — the same switch as the **Legend ▾** chip on the plot |
| **Lightning arresters** | OFF | The arrester rectangles, their labels and their protection circles |
| **ICR blocks** | OFF | Each control room's block — the ICR and the tables wired to it — filled in its own colour and outlined. Enabled once inverters are placed. With it ON, clicking a block, or picking one with the arrow keys and pressing **Enter**, opens that block's summary window — [ICR blocks](/docs/layout/icr-blocks) |
| **DC string cables (Tbl→Inv)** | OFF | Enabled only when cables were calculated |
| **AC cables (Inv→ICR)** — reads **DC cables (SMB→CI)** on a central-inverter design | OFF | Enabled only when cables were calculated |
| **MV cables (ICR→MCR)** | OFF | Needs a main control room placed and cables calculated |
| **Contour / terrain** | OFF | The elevation contours, with an *RL:* readout of the range and the excluded ground |
| **Plant totals** | ON | The plant chip row under the view; OFF hides it |

<Screenshot id="layers-popover" />

A first layout is therefore drawn as the placement alone: cables, arresters
and contours have been computed where you asked for them, but stay off until
you switch them on. The plot carries a units note in its bottom-left corner:
*"UTM 38N · metres"* (with the site's own zone) for a Google Earth file or a CAD
drawing located by its own coordinates, and *"Drawing coordinates · metres"* for
a CAD drawing or an image placed by hand or loaded as layout only — see
[CAD drawing boundaries](/docs/inputs/cad-boundary).

While the layout is out of date the plot dims, and an amber note at its
top-left names what changed, with a **Show 1 change** or **Show 2 changes**
link that opens the list of changes.

### The Legend chip

The legend is folded away by default so it never sits on the drawing. A small
**Legend ▾** chip in the plot's top-right corner (Layout view only) unfolds it;
the chip then reads **Legend ▴**, and the open legend can be dragged.

<Screenshot id="legend-open" />

## The plant chips

One row of chips runs under every view and never wraps. It is the only place
the plant totals are shown — the status bar does not carry them.

<Screenshot id="kpi-strip" />

| Chip | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **DC capacity** | Installed DC, in MWp |
| **Site area** | The gross boundary area, in acres |
| **Plants** | The number of plots — shown only for a multi-plot file |
| **Tables** or **Trackers** | Units placed |
| **Modules** | Modules across the plant |
| **ICR blocks** | Inverter control rooms placed |
| **Lightning arresters** | Shown only once some are placed |
| **Year-1 P50 energy** | Year-one generation in MWh, or *—* before an energy run |

Clicking any chip opens the Summary view; the energy chip opens the Energy
view. When the row is too narrow the lowest-priority chips hide first —
Lightning arresters, Plants, ICR blocks, Site area, Tables, Modules — and DC
capacity and energy go last. Water-body results are left out of the totals.

Two more chips can lead the row. Both open the Summary view when clicked, and
neither is ever hidden for width:

| Chip | When it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Out of date** | Amber, value *Out of date*, caption *figures from the last run* — while any input differs from the last run. Every other chip is greyed meanwhile |
| **Design warnings** | In the warning colour with an exclamation disc; the value is the number of warnings — while the design has any. It follows the **Out of date** chip when both show |

## The Summary view

The Summary view is the per-plant figures as a sheet turned on its side: a
**Metric** column, one column per plant, and a **Total** column when there are
two or more plants. A plant's column header reads *"\<plant\> ›"*, and clicking
it shows that plant on the Layout view.

<Screenshot id="summary-view" />

The rows are grouped:

| Group | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **SITE** | Plant area, boundary length, ICR blocks, lightning arresters, street lights, cleaning robots, piles |
| **ARRAY** | Full and half tables (or trackers), modules, tilt (or max tracking angle), pitch |
| **ELECTRICAL** | DC capacity, inverters or monitoring boxes and their ratings (and central inverters on a central-inverter design), AC capacity, inverter capacity, the DC / AC ratio, then **ACCBs** (string designs only), **Transformers (IDT)** and **Power transformer (MVA)** |
| **CABLES** | The string DC, AC or DC-trunk, and MV conductor lengths, and the three trench lengths |
| **ENERGY** | Year-one energy at each of your three exceedance probabilities, CUF, and the lifetime total |

A trailing `*` on tilt or pitch means the value was auto-calculated (fixed tilt
only). A `—` means not computed — the cable rows before cables are calculated,
the energy rows before a run. The three grouping rows read `—` until inverters
are placed; **Power transformer (MVA)** is the whole plant's figure, shown on the
plot that holds the main control room and in the Total column. Before any layout
the view reads *"Generate a layout to see the per-plant summary here."*

Two banners can sit above the sheet. The **out-of-date banner** lists what
changed since the last run; the sheet is then greyed and **Copy** is disabled.
The **design warnings banner** — *"1 design warning"*, *"2 design warnings"* —
lists each warning under the sentence *"The layout is complete, but … outside
the design rules."* The first is described on
[Out-of-date results](/docs/workspace/out-of-date-results#the-summary-view-and-its-window),
the second on [Design warnings](/docs/reference/design-warnings#the-summary-view).

**Copy** puts the whole sheet on the clipboard as tab-separated text, ready
for a spreadsheet or a document. **Open in window** opens **SolarLayout —
Summary**, a separate window with a **Summary** tab and a **Monthly energy
(Year 1)** tab — the second enabled once energy exists — that follows the
layout as you work. It carries the out-of-date banner too, but not the design
warnings. Every row is defined in
[Summary rows](/docs/reference/summary-columns).

## The Energy view

Before a run the view reads **No energy result yet**, explains what the run
will use, and offers a gold **Calculate energy** button — the same action as
**Calculate Energy** on the Yield tab. On a site loaded as layout only the note
reads *"Energy yield is off: the site's location is unknown (layout only)."*
After a run it shows four tiles — **Year-1 energy**, **Specific yield**,
**Performance ratio**, **CUF** — the monthly table to IEC 61724-1, an
irradiation and performance-ratio chart, and the monthly energy bars.

The result belongs to the layout it was calculated for. While that layout is
out of date a banner sits above the tiles and the figures are greyed; a new
**Generate Layout** clears the result, and the view then reads **Layout
regenerated** until you calculate again. See
[How yield is calculated](/docs/energy/overview).

## The BOM and SLD views

**BOM** shows the editable bill of materials on the right and hands the left
column to the **BOM TOOLS** panel under a green banner; **SLD** shows the
drawing sheet and hands the column to **SLD TOOLS** under a blue banner. Each
banner carries the **Exit** button that gives the column back. See
[Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview) and
[Single-line diagram](/docs/sld/overview).
