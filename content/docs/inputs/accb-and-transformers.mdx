---
title: ACCB and transformers
description: How the ACCB & Transformer card groups each ICR block's inverters onto combiner boxes and transformers, and sizes the power transformer.
---

The **ACCB & Transformer** card, the second card on the **Electrical** tab,
holds the rules that group the plant's AC equipment. Inside every ICR block —
one inverter control room and the tables wired to it — it gathers the string inverters onto **AC combiner boxes** (ACCBs), the combiner
boxes onto **inverter duty transformers** (IDTs), and it sizes the **power
transformer** at the main control room for the plant as a whole. The counts,
the transformer loads and the ratings it produces are the ones the Summary
view, the bill of materials, the single-line diagram, the cable schedule and
the ICR block window all print, so they agree with one another.

The card changes with the inverter topology chosen at launch. In a
central-inverter design it is titled **Transformer**: there are no combiner
boxes, and the central inverters in each control room land on the transformers
directly. See [Choosing a design mode](/docs/design-modes).

## The fields

Top to bottom, as the card shows them. Every row except the last two is yours
to set.

| Field | Default | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inverters per ACCB, max** *(string design)* | 15 | 1–60 | The most string inverters on one AC combiner box. Each ICR block gets its own ACCBs, with the inverters shared evenly between them |
| **ACCBs per transformer, max** *(string design)* | 4 | 1–12 | The most ACCBs on one transformer. Each takes one low-voltage winding |
| **Central inverters per transformer, max** *(central design, in place of the two rows above)* | 4 | 1–12 | The most central inverters on one transformer. Each takes one low-voltage winding |
| **Transformer size limit** | 17.2 MVA | 0.5–100.0 MVA | The largest transformer allowed. A block whose load is bigger gets more transformers. Never above the largest listed rating |
| **Standard transformer ratings (kVA)** | `2500, 3150, 5000, 6300, 8000, 10000, 12500, 16000` | — | The ratings an inverter duty transformer can take. Each transformer takes the smallest one at or above its load |
| **MV voltage** | 33.0 kV | 0.4–400.0 kV | The transformers' medium-voltage side, printed with their ratings (see [MV voltage](#mv-voltage)) |
| **Power transformer margin** | 1.00 × | 1.00–3.00 × | Multiplies the plant's maximum AC output when the power transformer is sized |
| **Power transformer derating** | 1.00 × | 0.50–1.00 × | Divides it. Set below 1.00 for a site above about 40 °C ambient or 1,000 m altitude, so the same load needs a higher rating |
| **Standard power transformer ratings (MVA)** | `10, 12.5, 16, 20, 25, 31.5, 40, 50, 63, 80, 100` | — | The ratings the power transformer can take. Above the largest, as many transformers as the need requires |
| **LV voltage** | *needs the OND file* | read-only | The transformers' low-voltage side, read from the inverter file |
| *(the answer line)* | *Now: Generate a layout first* | read-only | What the rules produce for the current layout |

The margin and the derating move in steps of 0.05. The two rating lists are
text fields, under their captions, across the full width of the card.

### Entering a rating list

A rating list is read when you finish editing it — press **Enter** or leave the
field. Separate the ratings with commas, semicolons or spaces, in any order:
the list is sorted, duplicates are dropped, and it is written back tidily, such
as `2500, 5000`.

A list the application cannot use is not applied. The previous ratings stay in
force, and a hint under the field says why and which ratings are still being
used:

| Entered | Hint |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing | *List at least one rating. Keeping 2500, 5000.* |
| A word or symbol | *“x” is not a number. Keeping …* |
| Zero or a negative figure | *x is not a positive rating. Keeping …* |

The largest rating in the list also caps the transformer size limit — see
[How a block is grouped](#how-a-block-is-grouped).

### The LV voltage line

The low-voltage side is not an input. It reads *needs the OND file* until an
inverter file is loaded on the inverter card, then the file's output voltage,
for example *800 V · from the OND*. See
[Inverter settings](/docs/inputs/inverter).

### The answer line

The last line of the card, prefixed **Now:**, shows what the rules produce for
the layout on screen, and updates as you change them.

| State | What the line reads |
| --- | --- |
| No layout yet | *Now: Generate a layout first* |
| String design, inverter file loaded | *Now: 12 ACCBs · 4 transformers: 4 × 16,000 kVA*, or with mixed ratings *… : 2 × 12,500 + 2 × 16,000 kVA* |
| No inverter file | *Now: 12 ACCBs · 4 transformers · ratings need the OND file* |
| Central design | *Now: 16 central inverters · 4 transformers: …*, or *… · ratings need the OND file* |
| A transformer's load fits no listed rating within the limit | the ratings, then *· 1 with no listed rating* (the count of such transformers) |

The answer line covers the ACCBs and the inverter duty transformers only. The
power transformer's figure is in the Summary view and the bill of materials.

## How a block is grouped

The grouping is made **per ICR block**: one inverter control room and the
tables wired to it through their inverters or string monitoring boxes. No
combiner box and no transformer is shared between two blocks. See
[ICR blocks](/docs/layout/icr-blocks).

For each block:

| Quantity | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **ACCBs** *(string design)* | The block's inverters ÷ **Inverters per ACCB, max**, rounded up. The inverters are shared out in order so that no two ACCBs differ by more than one inverter |
| **Central inverters** *(central design)* | The block's SMBs ÷ **Max SMB per Central Inverter**, rounded up — at least one and at most four per control room. After a [Simulation with AC capacity](/docs/simulation/ac-capacity) they are topped up to the study's unit count |
| **Transformers** | The larger of: the feeders (ACCBs or central inverters) ÷ the per-transformer maximum, rounded up; and, with an inverter file, the block's load ÷ the size limit, rounded up. Never more transformers than feeders. The feeders are shared out in order, and a transformer is added while any one is still over the limit |
| **The limit** | **Transformer size limit**, but never above the largest listed rating. With the defaults that is **16,000 kVA**, not 17.2 MVA |
| **Transformer load** | The sum of the inverter file's maximum AC output over every inverter (or central inverter) on that transformer |
| **Transformer rating** | The smallest listed rating at or above the load and no larger than the limit. When none fits, the transformer has no rating and is counted as *with no listed rating* |
| **Windings** | One low-voltage winding per ACCB or central inverter on the transformer, plus one |
| **Voltages** | Low voltage from the inverter file; medium voltage from **MV voltage** |

### A worked example

A string-design block with 60 inverters, the card at its defaults, and an
inverter file giving 300 kVA maximum output per inverter:

1. **ACCBs:** 60 ÷ 15 = **4 ACCBs** of 15 inverters each.
2. **Transformers by feeders:** 4 ACCBs ÷ 4 per transformer = 1.
3. **Transformers by load:** 60 × 300 kVA = 18,000 kVA, over the 16,000 kVA
   limit, so 18,000 ÷ 16,000 rounded up = **2 transformers**, the larger
   figure.
4. **Each transformer** carries 2 ACCBs — 30 inverters, 9,000 kVA — and takes
   the smallest listed rating at or above that: **10,000 kVA**, with three
   windings.

With 53 inverters instead, the load is 15,900 kVA: one transformer carrying
all four ACCBs, rated 16,000 kVA, with five windings.

### Without an inverter file

With no inverter file loaded the card still groups — the ACCB, central inverter
and transformer **counts** are made — but there is no load, so the transformers
carry no rating and no low-voltage figure. The outputs say so: *rating needs
OND* in the Summary view and the ICR block window, *LV needs OND* on the
single-line diagram. Load the inverter file on the inverter card to fill them
in.

## The power transformer

The power transformer steps the whole plant's output up at the main control
room. It is sized only when three things are true: a plot holds a main control
room, an inverter file is loaded, and the plant has been grouped. Place the
main control room from the Tools tab — see
[Site equipment](/docs/layout/site-equipment).

It is sized from **every** inverter in the plant (every central inverter, in a
central-inverter design), including the plots that feed the main control room
through their own unit substation:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add up the maximum AC output

Every inverter at the inverter file's maximum AC output, in MVA.
</Step>

<Step>
### Apply the margin and the derating

Multiply by **Power transformer margin** and divide by **Power transformer
derating**. The result is the plant's need.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick the ratings

If the need fits under the largest listed rating, one transformer takes the
smallest listed rating at or above it. If not, the need is split across as many
transformers as the largest rating requires, each taking the smallest listed
rating at or above its share.
</Step>
</Steps>

The result reads as a rating in MVA — *63*, or *2 × 80* for two machines. With a
main control room but no inverter file it reads *needs OND*; without a main
control room there is no power transformer and the figure reads `—`. The bill
of materials prints the basis as its remark, for example *Σ PMaxOut 58.1 MVA ×
margin 1 ÷ derating 1 = 58.1 MVA → next listed rating*, which with the default
ratings gives **63**.

The margin ships at 1.00. Rounding up to the next listed rating already leaves
headroom; raise the margin only when your own practice asks for more.

## What changing the card does

The card acts on the layout already on screen. You do not generate again.

- **Every change regroups every plot at once** — each change of a number, and
  each rating list accepted. Nothing moves: no table, inverter or cable is
  placed or routed again. Before the first layout a change simply waits: the
  next **Generate Layout** groups the plant with it.
- **The answer line and the Summary view refresh** straight away.
- **Any open ICR block windows close.** Open the block again to see the new
  grouping.
- **The layout does not go out of date.** The card's fields are not layout
  inputs, so no amber marks appear and every export stays available. Loading an
  inverter file is different: it regroups in the same way, but it does mark the
  layout out of date, because the inverter file is an input to the run. See
  [Out-of-date results](/docs/workspace/out-of-date-results).
- **The bill of materials is rebuilt the next time you open the BOM view.** The
  list is recomputed from the layout at that moment.
- **A single-line diagram already on the sheet is not redrawn.** Choose
  **Automatic** again in the SLD view to rebuild it from the new grouping. See
  [Building a diagram from the plant](/docs/sld/auto-build).

<Callout type="warn">
The rebuild of the bill of materials after a change on this card discards any
edits you made to the list, without asking — added rows, corrected
descriptions, typed remarks and a template put in place of the computed list.
Settle the grouping before you edit the list, and make your edits to the list
last. See [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview).
</Callout>

The card's rules are saved in the project file and come back when it is
reopened; the grouping itself returns with the saved layout, so nothing is
regrouped on opening. See [Projects](/docs/projects).

## Where the grouping appears

Every output reads the same grouping, so a count in one agrees with the count
in the others.

| Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Summary view**, ELECTRICAL group | **ACCBs** (string design only), **Transformers (IDT)** — such as *4 × 16,000 kVA*, *4 · rating needs OND* or *4 · 1 with no listed rating* — and **Power transformer (MVA)**, shown on the plot holding the main control room and in the Total column. All three read `—` before a layout. See [Summary view](/docs/reference/summary-columns) |
| **ICR block window** | **ACCBs** or **Central inverters**, **Transformer** (such as *1 × 16,000 kVA · 4-winding*) and **Transformer load** (such as *13,200 kVA · 33 / 0.8 kV*) for that block; the same rows print in the ICR-block PDF and DXF. See [ICR blocks](/docs/layout/icr-blocks) |
| **Bill of materials** | **ACCB (AC Combiner Box)**, **Inverter Duty Transformer (IDT)** and **Power Transformer**, with remarks that follow the card. See [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview) |
| **Single-line diagram** | The automatic build's ACCB and transformer counts, ratings, windings and voltages. See [Building a diagram from the plant](/docs/sld/auto-build) |
| **Cable schedule** | The collection table: each ACCB or central inverter to its transformer, and on to the main control room. See [The cable schedule](/docs/layout/cables#the-cable-schedule) |

### MV voltage

**MV voltage** is printed wherever a transformer's voltages are: the
single-line diagram's transformer, switchgear and grid labels, the bill of
materials' IDT remark, and the ICR block window's **Transformer load** row. It
does not reach the cable schedule, which sizes and labels its medium-voltage
runs at 33 kV whatever the card says.
