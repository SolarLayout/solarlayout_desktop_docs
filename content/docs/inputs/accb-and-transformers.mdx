---
title: ACCB and transformers
description: How the ACCB & Transformer card groups each ICR block's inverters onto combiner boxes and transformer forms, and sizes the power transformer.
---

The **ACCB & Transformer** card, the second card on the **Electrical** tab,
holds the rules that group the plant's AC equipment. Inside every ICR block —
one inverter control room and the tables wired to it — it gathers the string
inverters onto **AC combiner boxes** (ACCBs) and the combiner boxes onto
**inverter duty transformers** (IDTs), and it sizes the **power transformer** at
the main control room for the plant as a whole. The counts, the transformer
loads and the ratings it produces are the ones the Summary view, the Workings
view, the bill of materials, the single-line diagram and the deliverables all
print, so they agree with one another.

The card changes with the inverter topology chosen at launch. In a
central-inverter design it is titled **Transformer**: there are no combiner
boxes, and the central inverters in each control room land on the transformers
directly. See [Choosing a design mode](/docs/design-modes).

## The fields

Top to bottom, as the card shows them. Every row except the last two is yours
to set.

| Field | Default | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inverters per ACCB, max** *(string design)* | 15 | 1–60 | The most string inverters on one AC combiner box. Each ICR block gets its own ACCBs, with the inverters shared evenly between them |
| **Transformer forms (windings)** | `5, 3, 2` | whole numbers 2–13 | The transformer forms this project allows, by total windings: 5 is one MV winding and four ACCBs or central inverters. Each ICR takes the largest form first, every transformer full |
| **Transformer size limit** | 17.2 MVA | 0.5–100.0 MVA | The largest transformer allowed. A block whose load is bigger gets more transformers. Never above the largest listed rating |
| **Standard transformer ratings (kVA)** | `2,500, 3,150, 5,000, 6,300, 8,000, 10,000, 12,500, 16,000` | positive | The ratings an inverter duty transformer can take. Each transformer takes the smallest one at or above its load |
| **MV voltage** | 33.0 kV | 0.4–400.0 kV | The transformers' medium-voltage side, and the MV cables to the main control room |
| **Power transformer margin** | 1.00 × | 1.00–3.00 × | Multiplies the plant's maximum AC output when the power transformer is sized |
| **Power transformer derating** | 1.00 × | 0.50–1.00 × | Divides it. Set below 1.00 for a site above about 40 °C ambient or 1,000 m altitude, so the same load needs a higher rating |
| **Standard power transformer ratings (MVA)** | `10, 12.5, 16, 20, 25, 31.5, 40, 50, 63, 80, 100` | positive | The ratings the power transformer can take. Above the largest, as many transformers as the need requires |
| **LV voltage** | *needs the OND file* | read-only | The transformers' low-voltage side, read from the inverter file |
| *(the answer line)* | *Now: Generate a layout first* | read-only | What the rules produce for the current layout |

The margin and the derating move in steps of 0.05. At the defaults the line
under **Transformer size limit** reads *Capped at 16,000 kVA, the largest listed
rating*.

### Editing a list

The forms and the two rating lists are lists of chips. Type in the **Add…** box
and press **Enter**; a pasted list goes in at once. Commas, semicolons and spaces
separate values, so *16,000* is one size and *4000,20000* is two. A chip's ×
removes it, except the last one, which stays. A changed list shows a **Reset to
standard** link.

What cannot go in stays as you typed it, with the reason beside it, and the list
keeps what it had:

| Entered | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| A word or symbol | *“x” is not a number.* |
| Zero or a negative figure | *x is not a positive rating.* |
| A form outside the range | *x windings is outside 2–13 windings.* |
| A value the list already holds | *x is already listed.* |

### The LV voltage line

The low-voltage side is not an input. It reads *needs the OND file* until an
inverter file is loaded on the inverter card, then the file's output voltage,
for example *800 V · from the OND*, or *not in the OND file* when the file does
not declare one. See [Inverter settings](/docs/inputs/inverter).

### The answer line

The last line of the card, prefixed **Now:**, shows what the rules produce for
the layout on screen, and updates as you change them.

| State | What the line reads |
| --- | --- |
| No layout yet | *Now: Generate a layout first* |
| String design, inverter file loaded | *Now: 12 ACCBs · 8 transformers: 4 × 5,000 + 1 × 8,000 + 3 × 10,000 kVA* |
| No inverter file | *Now: 12 ACCBs · 4 transformers · ratings need the OND file* |
| Central design | *Now: 16 central inverters · 4 transformers: …*, or *… · ratings need the OND file* |
| A transformer's load fits no listed rating within the limit | the ratings, then *· 1 with no listed rating* (the count of such transformers). The line's tooltip lists each one, such as *ICR-3's transformer load 17,100 kVA fits no listed rating* |

The answer line covers the ACCBs and the inverter duty transformers only. The
power transformer's figure is in the Summary view and the bill of materials.

## How a block is grouped

The grouping is made **per ICR block**: one inverter control room and the
tables wired to it through their inverters or string monitoring boxes (SMBs). No
combiner box and no transformer is shared between two blocks. See
[ICR blocks](/docs/layout/icr-blocks).

For each block:

| Quantity | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **ACCBs** *(string design)* | The block's inverters ÷ **Inverters per ACCB, max**, rounded up. The inverters are shared out in order, so that no two ACCBs differ by more than one inverter |
| **Central inverters** *(central design)* | The block's SMBs ÷ **Max SMB per Central Inverter**, rounded up, at least one, with no limit per control room. After a [Simulation with AC capacity](/docs/simulation/ac-capacity) the count comes from the plant AC instead; see [Inverter settings](/docs/inputs/inverter#maximum-smbs-per-central-inverter) |
| **Transformers** | The feeders (ACCBs or central inverters) go onto the largest form first, **every transformer full**. A count the forms cannot cover exactly backs off larger forms first. With no exact cover, the cover with the fewest spare windings is used, the spare on the last transformer |
| **The limit** | While any transformer's load is over the limit, the largest form is dropped **for that block** and the feeders are filled again. The smallest form is kept whatever the limit |
| **Which limit** | **Transformer size limit**, but never above the largest listed rating. With the defaults that is **16,000 kVA**, not 17.2 MVA |
| **Transformer load** | The inverter file's maximum AC output over every inverter or central inverter on that transformer. A file with no maximum uses its nominal output |
| **Transformer rating** | The smallest listed rating at or above the load and no larger than the limit. When none fits, the transformer has no rating and is counted as *with no listed rating* |
| **Windings** | The form. One winding is MV; the others carry a feeder each, or stand spare |
| **Voltages** | Low voltage from the inverter file; medium voltage from **MV voltage** |

A spare winding is reported by the design check **E-15 Transformer forms**, for
example *ICR 2: 3 ACCBs on a 5-winding transformer, 1 LV winding spare. Add a
4-winding form or change the block.* See
[Design checks](/docs/reference/design-checks).

### How the feeders fill the default forms

With forms 5, 3 and 2:

| Feeders | Transformers (feeders on each, its windings) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 on a 2-winding |
| 2 | 2 on a 3-winding |
| 3 | 2 on a 3-winding, 1 on a 2-winding |
| 4 | 4 on a 5-winding |
| 5 | 4 on a 5-winding, 1 on a 2-winding |
| 6 | 4 on a 5-winding, 2 on a 3-winding |
| 7 | 4 on a 5-winding, 2 on a 3-winding, 1 on a 2-winding |
| 8 | 4 on a 5-winding, twice |
| 10 | 4 on a 5-winding, twice, then 2 on a 3-winding |

The list decides what is possible. With forms 5 and 3, seven feeders take a
5-winding and a 5-winding with three feeders, one winding spare. With forms 5
and 4, six feeders take two 4-winding transformers. With the 5-winding form
alone, three feeders take one 5-winding, with one winding spare.

### A worked example

A string-design block, the card at its defaults, and an inverter file giving
300 kVA maximum output per inverter:

| Inverters | ACCBs | Largest form's load | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60 | 4 of 15 | 60 × 300 = 18,000 kVA on a 5-winding, over 16,000 | The 5-winding is left out. Two 3-winding transformers, 2 ACCBs and 9,000 kVA each, rated **10,000 kVA** |
| 53 | 4 | 53 × 300 = 15,900 kVA on a 5-winding | One 5-winding transformer, rated **16,000 kVA** |
| 41 | 3, of 14, 14 and 13 | No 5-winding: three feeders fill a 3-winding and a 2-winding | A 3-winding carrying 2 ACCBs and 8,400 kVA, rated **10,000 kVA**, and a 2-winding carrying 1 ACCB and 3,900 kVA, rated **5,000 kVA** |

The **Transformers (IDT)** row of the Workings view names the forms, the limit,
the ratings and any form left out, for example *5-winding left out, over 16,000
kVA full*. See [The Workings view](/docs/workspace/workings).

### Without an inverter file

With no inverter file loaded the card still groups — the ACCB, central inverter
and transformer **counts** are made — but there is no load, so the transformers
carry no rating and no low-voltage figure. The Summary view says so, *4 · rating
needs an OND file*, as a link to the file row. Load the inverter file on the
inverter card to fill them in.

## The power transformer

The power transformer steps the whole plant's output up at the main control
room. It is sized only when three things are true: a plot holds a main control
room, an inverter file is loaded, and the plant has been grouped. Place the
main control room from the Tools tab — see
[Site equipment](/docs/layout/site-equipment). With the grid connection
[by others](/docs/layout/grid-connection) it is left out.

It is sized from **every** inverter in the plant (every central inverter, in a
central-inverter design), including the plots that feed the main control room
through their own unit substation:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add up the maximum AC output

Every inverter at the inverter file's maximum AC output, in MVA.
</Step>

<Step>
### Apply the margin and the derating

Multiply by **Power transformer margin** and divide by **Power transformer
derating**. The result is the plant's need.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick the ratings

If the need fits under the largest listed rating, one transformer takes the
smallest listed rating at or above it. If not, the need is split across as many
transformers as the largest rating requires, each taking the smallest listed
rating at or above its share.
</Step>
</Steps>

The Summary view's **Power transformer (MVA)** row carries the result, or the
reason there is none: *needs an OND file*, *needs an MCR on the layout* or *by
others*. The basis reads, for example, *Σ PMaxOut 58.1 MVA × margin 1 ÷ derating
1 = 58.1 MVA → next listed rating*, which with the default ratings gives **63**.

The margin ships at 1.00. Rounding up to the next listed rating already leaves
headroom; raise the margin only when your own practice asks for more.

## What changing the card does

The card acts on the layout already on screen. You do not generate again.

- **Every change regroups every plot at once.** Nothing moves: no table, inverter
  or cable is placed or routed again. Before the first layout a change waits, and
  the next **Generate Layout** groups the plant with it.
- **The answer line and the Summary view refresh** straight away. The earthing
  layer redraws if it is on, and an open Earthing Design window closes.
- **The layout does not go out of date.** The card's fields are not layout
  inputs, so no amber marks appear. Loading an inverter file is different: it
  regroups in the same way, but it does mark the layout out of date, because the
  inverter file is an input to the run. See
  [Out-of-date results](/docs/workspace/out-of-date-results).
- **The bill of materials recounts the next time you open the BOM view.** Your
  edits to it are kept. See [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview).
- **An automatic single-line diagram you have not touched rebuilds at once.** One
  you have edited is kept and marked out of date, with a choice to rebuild it or
  keep it. See [Building a diagram from the plant](/docs/sld/auto-build).

The card's rules are saved in the project file and come back when it is
reopened; the grouping itself returns with the saved layout, so nothing is
regrouped on opening. See [Projects](/docs/projects).

## Where the grouping appears

Every output reads the same grouping, so a count in one agrees with the count
in the others.

| Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Summary view**, Plant sheet | **ACCBs** (string design), **Transformers (IDT)** — such as *4 × 16,000 kVA*, *2 × 12,500 + 2 × 16,000 kVA*, *4 · rating needs an OND file* or *4 · 1 with no listed rating* — and **Power transformer (MVA)**. See [Summary view](/docs/reference/summary-columns) |
| **Summary view**, By block sheet | **ACCBs** or **Central inverters**, **Transformers (IDT)** such as *2 × 12,500 kVA*, and **Transformer load (kVA)** such as *13,200 · 83 %*, for each block |
| **Workings view** | How the transformers and the power transformer were reached |
| **Bill of materials** | **AC combiner box (ACCB)**, **Inverter duty transformer** and **Power transformer**, with remarks that follow the card. See [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview) |
| **Single-line diagram** | The automatic build's ACCB and transformer counts, ratings, windings and voltages. See [Building a diagram from the plant](/docs/sld/auto-build) |
| **Deliverables** | The automatic single-line diagram in the [drawing set](/docs/exports/drawing-set), and the ACCBs, transformers and feeders in the [schedules workbook](/docs/exports/schedules-and-kmz) |

### MV voltage

**MV voltage** is printed wherever a transformer's voltages are, and the MV
cables are sized at it too, so their sizes and drops in the schedules follow this
card.
