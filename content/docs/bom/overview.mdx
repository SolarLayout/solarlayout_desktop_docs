title: Bill of materials
description: The BOM view counts a procurement list from the layout through a profile. How to read it, fill what is open, edit a line and export it.
---

The bill of materials, the BOM, is a procurement list counted from the layout
you have generated. A **profile** decides which items the list can hold and how
each is specified; the design decides which of them apply and how many.

You do not type the list. Everything measurable is counted from the plant. What
the layout cannot know, such as the CCTV system or the wind speed the structure
is designed for, shows as a line that needs your answer. Your answers and edits
sit on top of the count, so a new layout recounts the plant and keeps them.

## Opening the BOM view

Click the **BOM** tab, or press **Ctrl+6**. The left column becomes the BOM
panel under a green banner titled **Bill of Materials**, and the list takes the
place of the plot. The status bar reads *"BOM Mode ON — select a line to see how
it is counted; edits apply as you type."* **Exit BOM** gives the column back to
the stage tabs. See [Views](/docs/workspace/views) for the other views.

<Screenshot id="bom-view" />

The BOM is counted the first time the view opens after a Generate, or when an
export needs it, under the message *"Working out the bill of materials…"*. Before
a layout exists the list reads *"Generate a layout to count the plant."* Water
plots and plots with no tables are not counted.

## The list

Nothing is edited in the list itself. Select a row and the **Line** card on the
left shows how it is counted and what you can change.

Three controls run along the top: **Plant | By block** chooses the table, **All |
Needs attention** filters it, and a search box looks through items,
specifications and makes. **By block** adds a block selector, which starts on
**All blocks**.

### The Plant table

Lines are grouped by category, and each group's heading counts its lines and how
many need attention.

| Column | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Item & specification** | The item's name, then its specification on a second line. The tooltip carries the line's notes and warnings |
| **Makes** | The makes you accept. The PV module and the inverter take theirs from the PAN and OND files |
| **Order qty** | The quantity to order, with the design quantity beneath it and any allowance, such as *3,094 + 5 %*. A dash means there is no quantity yet |
| **Basis** | What the quantity comes from, or what is needed to get one |
| **Source** | **Measured**, **Rule** or **Manual** |
| **Status** | **✓ Ready**, or what the line is waiting for |

| Source | Where the quantity comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Measured** | Counted from the design: modules, tables, cable lengths, earth pits |
| **Rule** | A factor times something counted, such as one mounting stand per string inverter |
| **Manual** | You enter it |

**Order qty** is the design quantity × (1 + allowance %), rounded up to the
unit's precision.

| Unit | Rounded up to |
| --- | --- |
| Nos, set, m | Whole numbers |
| t, m², m³ | 2 decimals |
| LS, MWp | Not rounded |

There is no rate or amount column.

### The By block table

**By block** shows one column for each inverter control room (ICR) block, then
**Shared**, **Total** and **Unit**. A quantity not tied to a block sits under
**Shared**. An empty cell is zero.

<Screenshot id="bom-by-block" />

**Total** here is the design quantity, not the order quantity, so it leaves out
the allowance. Choosing a block in the selector narrows the table to the lines
with a quantity in that block. The block columns and the totals stay those of
the whole plant.

## Getting to ready

A line is **✓ Ready** when it has a quantity and every required part of its
specification. Otherwise it carries one status, the first that applies:

| Status | What the line needs | Where to answer it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **! Needs design input** | Something the design has not provided | The **Go** button, below |
| **! Needs factor** | A factor for a rule whose factor is blank | **Factor** on the **Line** card |
| **! Needs quantity** | A manual quantity | **Quantity** on the **Line** card |
| **! Needs specification** | A required specification field | The field marked with an asterisk on the **Line** card |

A new plant always starts with open lines, because some answers cannot come from
a layout. Dozens of manual lines need a quantity. The ICR cable tray, its canopy
and the equipment-yard light need a factor, as do the earthing cable and lug for
central inverters. **MMS table (supply package)** needs **Basic wind speed**, MMS
being the module mounting structure. The tracker package needs **Basic wind
speed** and **OEM and model**, and the **Power transformer** needs **Voltage
ratio**.

### The Readiness card

The card opens with the verdict, *"✓ All 111 lines ready"* or *"! 71 of 125 lines
ready"*, and filter chips for each status. Under them, each missing design input
has a row saying what to do, where, and how many lines it holds up, with a
**Go** button. **Go** leaves the BOM view and opens the place named.

| Reason on the line | Row reads | **Go** opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *"The AC rating comes from the inverter's OND file."* or *"Transformer ratings come from the inverter's OND file."* | Load an OND file · Electrical tab | The inverter card on the Electrical tab |
| *"The module's Isc comes from its PAN file."* | Load the module's PAN file · Array tab | The module card on the Array tab |
| *"Cable quantities come from the cable calculation, which is off."* | Turn on Calculate cables · Electrical tab | The Cables card |
| *"No listed transformer rating is large enough for its load."* | Add a larger transformer rating · Electrical tab | The grouping card |
| *"Piles are counted from the pile pattern, and none is defined."* | Define a pile pattern · Layout ▸ Piles | The pile window |
| *"No inverters are placed yet."* | Generate the layout · Generate Layout | **Generate Layout** |

## Editing a line

Select a line, and the **Line** card takes its name. It opens with the category,
source, nature and status, such as *"PV modules · Measured · Supply · ✓ Ready"*.
Edits apply as you type. What the card shows depends on how the line is counted.

| Line | What the card shows |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting for a design input | The reason and a button that opens the fix |
| **Measured** | The quantity and its basis, read-only |
| **Rule** | **Factor**, 0 and up, and **Design qty** worked out, such as *"1.0 × 3,144 strings = 3,144 Nos"* |
| **Manual** | **Quantity**, 0 and up |

<Screenshot id="inspector" maxWidth={320} />

Below that, on every line:

- **Allowance**, 0 to 100 %, and the resulting **Order qty**.
- The design values, read-only, each with its origin (*from Array tab*).
- The specification fields, editable, with a list where the item type has set
  choices. Required ones are marked with an asterisk.
- **Makes**: type into **Add a make** to add one, or remove one from its chip. The
  PV module and inverter show *"from the file"* instead.
- **Remarks**.
- **Turn this entry off**, or **Remove this line** on a line you added.

<Screenshot id="inspector-sizes" maxWidth={320} caption="A measured line and its specification fields" />

A rule line and a manual line differ only at the top of the card.

### One size or all of them

An entry can produce several lines, such as one DC solar cable line per size. On
those the card offers **All N sizes | This size only**, and your edit lands on
the whole entry or that one line.

### A number that follows a BOM setting

An allowance that follows a [BOM setting](#bom-settings) shows **This line | All N
lines** and a hint, such as *"BOM setting · Spare for discrete consumables ·
5.0 %"*. **All N lines** changes the setting for this project. **This line** gives
this line a number of its own, and **Use the setting** drops that number again.

### Adding a line

Open the **Add a line** card, or click **+ Add a line** on the empty **Line** card.

| Tab | You give it |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalog** | **Item type** and **Quantity** |
| **Rule** | **Item type**, the design quantity to count from, and **Factor** |
| **New type** | **Name**, **Category**, **Unit**, the design quantity to count from, and **Factor** |

A preview shows the result, such as *"Preview: 40 Nos"*. Click **Add**, and the
new line is selected. An item type you add this way is a supply item. **Add a
line** says *"Generate a layout first."* until a layout exists.

## The Profile card

The top of the panel says what the list is built from.

| Part | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| The profile | Its name, kind, version and entries, with **Change…** to choose another. See [BOM profiles](/docs/bom/profiles) |
| **THIS PROJECT** | How many edits you have made, such as *"54 edits · 5 can go into a profile"*. **Save as profile…** keeps them for other projects |
| **BOM settings** | How many of the ten are changed, with **Edit…** |
| **IN THIS BOM** | How many lines come from how many entries, such as *"125 lines from 118 of the profile's 173 entries"* |

Under **IN THIS BOM**, three counts open to name the entries that have no line.

| Count | Why the entry has no line |
| --- | --- |
| **Not for this design** | It belongs to another design type, such as tracker items on a fixed-tilt plant |
| **Switched off** | It is off by default in the profile, or you turned it off. One that holds an edit of yours reads *"your edit kept"* |
| **None on this layout** | It applies, but the layout has none to count |

A project that carries a structure kit also shows *"This project has a structure
kit. The BOM lists the structure as a package; the kit is kept for later."*

## What survives a new layout

Edits belong to the project and a Generate keeps them. A change to the
electrical grouping or to a BOM setting recounts the list and keeps them too.

When a line or entry leaves the design, the edit on it goes to the card **No
longer in the design**, which opens by itself when a new one arrives. It names the
line and the edit, such as *"make Comet; 3 % allowance"*. **Dismiss** deletes that
edit for good.

While the inputs are [out of date](/docs/workspace/out-of-date-results), the view
shows the last run's plant with no banner of its own, and the BOM's exports are
refused. Inputs changed since the run reach the list after the next Generate.

## BOM settings

BOM settings are the numbers many lines share. They come from the project's
profile, and a change here is for this project. Open them with **Edit…** on the
**Profile** card, or at **File ▸ Advanced settings… ▸ BOM**. **All N lines** on a line
changes one in place. See [Advanced settings](/docs/workspace/advanced-settings).

<Screenshot id="settings-bom" />

| Setting | Default | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Spare for discrete consumables** | 5.0 % | 0–50 | Added to lugs, glands, connectors, bolt sets and the like |
| **Lugs per 3-core cable end** | 3 | 1–6 | One per core, on LV AC and LV-IDT cable ends. IDT is the inverter duty transformer |
| **Bifacial current gain** | 10.0 % | 0–30 | Added to the module's Isc when it is bifacial |
| **String fuse factor** | 1.56 × | 1–3 | Times the design Isc; the fuse is the next size up |
| **DC disconnector factor** | 1.25 × | 1–2 | Times the strings per string monitoring box (SMB) and the design Isc; the next size up |
| **String fuse sizes** | 15, 20, 25, 30, 32, 50, 63 A | each 1–200 | The standard fuse sizes to choose from |
| **DC disconnector sizes** | 250, 320, 400, 500, 630 A | each 50–2000 | The standard disconnector sizes to choose from |
| **MV cable drum length** | 500 m | 50–2000 | A run longer than a drum needs a straight joint per extra drum, per parallel cable |
| **Concrete block spacing** | 0.8 m | 0.3–3 | Cable laid above ground rests on a block every this many metres |
| **Cable tie spacing** | 0.50 m | 0.1–2 | DC cable is tied to the table or tracker every this many metres |

A change recounts the BOM at once, marks the project as changed, and does not put
the rest of the results out of date. To keep a value for other projects, save it
into a profile.

## Remarks from the design checks

A failed [design check](/docs/workspace/checks) that concerns a BOM line shows on
that line as *"Check: &lt;ID&gt; · &lt;the finding&gt;"*, in the item's tooltip and at
the top of the **Line** card. A remark never changes a quantity, and an
accepted check drops off the line. These remarks stay in the application.

| Check finding | Line |
| --- | --- |
| ICR over its rating or not placed | **ICR building** |
| Central inverter overloaded | **Central inverter** |
| Spare winding on a transformer | **Inverter duty transformer** |
| AC feeder over its flag, no single run carries it, or the drop is over budget | **LV AC cable** |
| DC trunk too long | **DC trunk cable** |
| DC string drop | **DC solar cable** |
| A shadow clearance skipped | **PV module** |

## Exporting

Both exports are on **Export ▾** in the view header, and both need active access.
**BOM · Excel workbook…** and **BOM · PDF…** stay greyed out until the BOM can be
written, with *"Generate a layout first"* or *"Inputs changed since the last run.
Regenerate first."* as the reason. The default file name is `BOM - <project> -
<yyyy-mm-dd>`, with the project taken from the project file's name or else the
boundary file's.

An incomplete BOM still exports. Each line goes to one place:

- A line that is not ready goes to **Open items**, and its quantity stays off the
  main list even when the quantity is known.
- A manual quantity typed as 0 is dropped.
- **Mandatory spares** get a section of their own.
- Everything else goes on the main list.

### The Excel workbook

| Sheet | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **BOM** | A heading block, then S.No, Category, Item, Specification, Makes, Nature, Unit, Design qty, Allowance %, Order qty, Source, Basis, Remarks |
| **Block-wise** | Item, Specification, Unit, one column per block, Shared, Total, and Check (*"✓ adds up"* or *"! off by N"*) |
| **Open items** | S.No, Item, Status, What's needed, Where to fix it. Present when something is open |
| **Mandatory spares** | Present when there are any |

The heading block gives the project, the capacity, the design, the profile, each
BOM setting with its source (*"Spare for discrete consumables: 3.0 % (profile
5.0 %, changed in this project)"*), the date and the status (*"! Incomplete · 56 open
items"* or *"✓ Complete · 21 lines"*).

### The PDF

Each page carries the title, the project, the capacity, design and profile, and a
status badge. The body lists the ready lines with S.No, Item & specification, Makes,
Unit, Order qty and Basis, then **Mandatory spares**, **Open items** on a new page,
and **BOM settings** with the value used against the profile's.

<Screenshot id="bom-page" />

Exported files carry the design notes that belong to a line, such as *"Estimated: the
DC strings were routed for a sample of the plot"* or *"No listed rating: …"*. They
carry no design check remarks.

### In the deliverables package

[Export deliverables…](/docs/exports/deliverables) writes the same workbook as S2.
The [design report](/docs/exports/design-report) prints the same pages in its
**Bill of materials** section, with nothing recomputed.

## What the project keeps

A saved project holds the profile as it was used and your decisions: entry and
line edits, entries turned off, added lines and types, and BOM settings. Quantities
are not saved. They are counted again when the project opens. A project file with
inputs only opens with the default profile. See [Projects](/docs/projects).

<Callout type="warn">
Click **Save project** when you finish editing the BOM. Your edits reach the project
file only when it is saved.
</Callout>

## A working order

<Steps>
<Step>
### Finish the design

Load the PAN and OND files and generate with **Calculate cables** on. Place the main
control room and generate again, which routes its MV cables. Define the pile pattern. Cable lengths are usually the largest
quantities on the list. See [Cable routing](/docs/layout/cables).
</Step>

<Step>
### Size the cleaning fleet, if you want one

Run [Robotic Module Cleaning](/docs/simulation/robotic-cleaning) and click **Add to BOM**.
The fleet's lines appear only once it is sized.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the profile

Click **Change…** on the **Profile** card if your company has its own. Starting from
the right profile saves entering its makes and specifications again.
</Step>

<Step>
### Clear the design inputs

Open the **BOM** view and work down the **Go** rows on the **Readiness** card.
</Step>

<Step>
### Answer what is open

Click **Needs attention**, then give each line its quantity, factor or specification.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save and export

Click **Save project**, then export from **Export ▾**.
</Step>
</Steps>
