title: Summary view
description: Every row of the Summary view — the Plant and By block sheets, what stands above them, the reasons in place of a figure, and How?
---

The **Summary** view shows the plant's figures as a sheet, in one of two forms: **By
block**, one column per ICR block, and **Plant**, one column per plant boundary in
your file. It is the second of the view tabs across the top of the right side
(**Ctrl+2**). Before a layout exists it reads *"Generate a layout to see the
per-plant summary here."* See [Views, layers and tools](/docs/workspace/views).

<Screenshot id="summary-with-energy" />

## What stands above the sheet

In order, each only when it applies:

| Item | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| **The out-of-date banner** | Names each change: **Out of date.** *These figures are from the last run, and 2 inputs have changed since: Module wattage 610.0 Wp → 700.0 Wp, Soiling losses 2.0 % → 3.0 %. Regenerate the layout to update them.* The sheet is greyed meanwhile. See [Out-of-date results](/docs/workspace/out-of-date-results) |
| **The failed-checks line** | *"1 design check failed"* or *"N design checks failed"*, then the first three by ID and title, *"E-06 ICR inverter count · …"*, then *"and N more"*, with **Open Checks**. See [Design checks](/docs/workspace/checks#the-chip-and-the-summary-line) |
| **The settings note** | *"Changed in Advanced settings: MV voltage drop 3.0 % (default 2.0 %) · …"* with **Edit…**. See [Advanced settings](/docs/workspace/advanced-settings) |
| **The bar** | The **Plant \| By block** switch, and a line on the right |

The sheet opens on **By block**, in a fresh window and whenever a project opens. Your
choice lasts while the project is open and is not saved.

| Sheet | The line on the right |
| --- | --- |
| By block | *"4 blocks · 11.92 to 15.37 MWp · the smallest is 78 % of the largest"*, or *"1 block · X MWp"* |
| Plant | *"Click a plant's name to show it on the layout"* |
| No blocks | **By block** is greyed, with *"By block needs ICR blocks on the layout"*, and the Plant sheet shows |

## Reading the sheet

**A reason stands where a figure can't be given.** It is a link to the field or card
the figure needs.

| Reason | Means | The link goes to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *needs an OND file* | The figure comes from the inverter file | The OND file row |
| *rating needs an OND file* | The count is known, not the rating: *"8 · rating needs an OND file"* | The OND file row |
| *not calculated yet* | Energy has not been calculated | **Calculate Energy** |
| *no pile pattern* | No pile pattern is defined | The pile window |
| *needs Calculate cables* | **Calculate cables** was off for the run | **Calculate cables** |
| *needs an MCR on the layout* | No main control room is placed | **Place MCR** |
| *by others* | The [grid connection is by others](/docs/layout/grid-connection) | — |
| *laid above ground*, *on cable tray* | That class of cable is not buried, so it has no trench | The laying choice |

### How?

**How?** appears at the right of a row's label when you hover over it or select it, on
any figure that has a working, and opens the [Workings view](/docs/workspace/workings)
at that figure. Plant area, Boundary length, Modules, DC per acre, Inverter AC rating
and the trench rows have none.

**Tilt and pitch are as used.** Whether they were worked out or typed in, the
Workings view says how they were reached.

**Rounding.** Tilt to 1 decimal, pitch and DC capacity to 2, counts to whole numbers,
with thousands separators on large numbers. A feature the design doesn't use leaves its
row out, and a group with no rows disappears.

## The Plant sheet

Columns: *Metric*, one per plant, its head reading *"Demo ›"*, a link that shows that
plant on the Layout view; and *Total* with two or more plants. See
[Multi-plot sites](/docs/layout/multi-plot). One plant on a wide window gets two tables
side by side.

### Capacity

| Row | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **DC capacity (MWp)** | The placed modules multiplied by the wattage |
| **Plant AC capacity (MWac)** | The AC capacity the plant is sized against: the target of a **Simulation with AC Capacity** shared by units when one was run, otherwise the installed inverter capacity |
| **DC / AC ratio** | DC capacity divided by plant AC capacity |
| **Inverter AC capacity (MW)**, **DC / AC on inverters** | The installed inverter capacity and the ratio on it. Shown only when it differs from plant AC |
| **P50 energy, year 1 (MWh)** | Year-one energy at the first exceedance probability |
| **CUF on DC (%)** | Capacity utilisation factor: year-one energy ÷ (kWp × 8760). Once energy exists |
| **CUF on AC (%)** | Year-one energy ÷ (plant AC × 8760). Once energy exists |

### Site

| Row | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Plant area (acres)** | The plant's **gross** boundary area |
| **Boundary length (m)** | The perimeter of the plant boundary |
| **DC per acre (MWp)** | DC capacity per acre of plant area |
| **Street lights** | Poles placed along the perimeter, when the design has some |
| **Cleaning robots** | The fleet the cleaning study sized, when it has been run |

**Plant area is the site's area, not the array's.** It is the area enclosed by the
whole boundary polygon, before the perimeter road setback and before any obstruction or
terrain exclusion, so it does not fall as exclusions grow. Add an obstruction, tighten
the slope limits or widen the perimeter road and the capacity drops while this row
stays where it was.

### Array

| Row | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Full tables** / **Full trackers** | Full units placed |
| **Half tables** / **Half trackers** | Half units placed |
| **Modules** | Modules placed across the plant, full units plus half units |
| **Piles** | Each unit's own piles, summed: the full pattern on every full unit, the half pattern on every half unit. Counted whether or not the pile overlay is on. See [Piles](/docs/editing/piles) |
| **Tilt (°)** / **Max tracking angle (°)** | The fixed tilt the layout used, or the tracker's rotation limit |
| **Pitch (m)** | The row spacing the layout used |
| **GCR** | The ground coverage ratio |

### Electrical

| Row | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **ICR blocks** | Inverter control rooms placed. The number needed is counted per separate piece of land, so it can exceed the plant capacity ÷ **ICR Block**. See [Inverter control rooms](/docs/layout/icr) |
| **SMBs** *(central)* | String monitoring boxes placed |
| **Largest DC load per SMB (kWp)** *(central)* | The most DC capacity on one box |
| **Central inverters** *(central)* | Central inverters. See [Inverter](/docs/inputs/inverter) |
| **String inverters** *(string)* | String inverters placed |
| **Inverter AC rating (kW)** | The AC rating from the inverter file |
| **Largest DC load per inverter (kWp)** *(string)* | The most DC capacity on one inverter |
| **DC limit per central inverter (kWp)** *(central)* | The box capacity multiplied by **Max SMB per Central Inverter** |
| **ACCBs** *(string)* | AC combiner boxes, from the **ACCB & Transformer** card |
| **Transformers (IDT)** | The inverter duty transformers: *"4 × 16,000 kVA"*, *"2 × 12,500 + 2 × 16,000 kVA"*, *"4 · rating needs an OND file"*, *"4 · 1 with no listed rating"* |
| **Power transformer (MVA)** | The whole site's power transformer at the MCR |
| **Lightning arresters** | Arresters placed, with arresters on |

The grouping rows follow the **ACCB & Transformer** card at once, without a Generate.
See [ACCBs and transformers](/docs/inputs/accb-and-transformers).

A string design and a central design have different rows here. To compare them, read
**DC capacity**, **ICR blocks**, **Transformers (IDT)** and the cable group.

### Cables & trenches

The first rows are **conductor** to procure; the trench rows are **trench** to dig.
Mixing them up over-orders cable or under-digs trench.

| Row | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **String DC cable (m)** | Conductor from the tables to the string inverter or the SMB |
| **AC cable to ICR (m)** *(string)* / **DC cable to central inverter (m)** *(central)* | AC from each inverter to its control room, or the DC trunk from each box |
| **MV cable (m)** | Medium-voltage conductor from the control rooms to the MCR or handover point |
| **DC trench (m)** | Trench for the string DC cables |
| **AC trench (m)** *(string)* / **DC trunk trench (m)** *(central)* | Trench for the second leg |
| **MV trench (m)** | Trench for the medium-voltage run |
| **String DC cable per MWp (m)** | String DC cable divided by DC capacity |

A trench total counts a stretch that several cables share once, so the trench figures
are the smaller ones, and only buried classes count as trench. How each route is chosen
is on [Cable routing](/docs/layout/cables).

On a very large plot, the DC trench is measured on a drawn sample of the DC cabling and
scaled to every table, and the figure carries *" (est.)"*.

### Energy

Once energy is calculated, this group holds the other two exceedance rows and the
lifetime total. Every Generate that replaces the layout clears the energy rows. See
[How yield is calculated](/docs/energy/overview).

| Row | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **P75 energy, year 1 (MWh)** | Year-one energy at the second exceedance probability |
| **P90 energy, year 1 (MWh)** | Year-one energy at the third exceedance probability |
| **P50 energy, 25 years (MWh)** | The lifetime total — see below |

**The P rows are named from the exceedance probabilities you set.** On the shipped
defaults of 50 %, 75 % and 90 % they read as above; change any of the three fields under
**Uncertainty & exceedance** and the row name changes with it.

**"P50 energy, 25 years" does not report 25 years.** The figure is the cumulative energy
over years 1 to **Plant lifetime**, after degradation, at the **first** exceedance
probability, and the row's name does not follow either setting. Plant lifetime ships at
30 years, so on shipped settings a row named "25 years" carries a 30-year total.

<Callout type="warn">
Do not quote the lifetime row by its name. Read **Plant lifetime** and the first
exceedance probability off the Yield tab and state both alongside the figure. How the
figures are derived is on [Lifetime and P-values](/docs/energy/lifetime-and-p-values).
</Callout>

## The By block sheet

Columns: one per block, *ICR-1*, or *P1-ICR-1* with several plots; then **Shared** and
**Total**; and a band naming each plant when there are several. A block's head is a link
that fits the Layout view to that block's outline. A block is one ICR and the tables
wired to it. See [ICR blocks](/docs/layout/icr-blocks).

<Screenshot id="summary-by-block" />

| Group | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| **Block size** | **DC capacity (MWp)**, with a bar against the largest block · **Share of plant DC (%)** · **Area (acres)** · **Full tables** and **Half tables** (or trackers) · **Modules** · **Piles**, with a pattern |
| **Equipment** | **String inverters** and **ACCBs**, or **SMBs** and **Central inverters** · **AC capacity (MWac)** · **DC / AC ratio** · **Transformers (IDT)**, *"2 × 12,500 kVA"* · **Transformer load (kVA)**, *"13,200 · 83 %"*, with a bar against the block's rating · **Power transformer (MVA)** · **Lightning arresters** |
| **Cables & trenches** | String DC, the feeder and MV per block; the trench rows in **Shared** and **Total** only |
| **Plant-wide** | **Boundary length (m)** · **Street lights** · **Cleaning robots** · the year-one P row · **CUF on DC** · **CUF on AC**: energy is calculated for the whole plant |

**Total is the Plant sheet's own figure, never a sum of the column.** **Shared** area is
the Total less the blocks as shown.

A note pinned under the sheet says what is shared, for example *"Shared: what belongs to
no single block: 19.73 acres of roads, gaps and the MCR's ground, the trench networks,
the boundary, the power transformer and energy."*, then any rounding difference: *"DC
capacity (MWp): the blocks add up to 54.27; the Total is the plant's own 54.28."*

## Open in window

**Open in window** opens **SolarLayout — Summary**, a separate window for a second
monitor. It follows the layout, shows whichever sheet is chosen, and has two tabs:
**Summary** and **Monthly energy (Year 1)**, the second enabled once energy exists. It
carries the out-of-date banner, not the failed-checks line or the settings note. Use
**How?**, a reason's link or a plant's or block's head in the Summary view itself.

## Where the same figures appear elsewhere

| Destination | What it carries |
| --- | --- |
| The plant chips under every view | Site area, DC capacity, plants (multi-plot only), tables or trackers, modules, ICR blocks, lightning arresters (when placed) and year-one energy, led by **Out of date** and **Design checks** when they apply |
| The Yield tab's status line | After a run: PR, year-one energy, CUF on DC, CUF on AC and the lifetime total |
| The design report | The figures, the design basis and the block schedule. See [The design report](/docs/exports/design-report) |
| The block sheets of the drawing set | One block's figures on each sheet. See [The drawing set](/docs/exports/drawing-set) |
| The schedules workbook and the KMZ | Every circuit and trench; each block's figures in a pop-up. See [Schedules and KMZ](/docs/exports/schedules-and-kmz) |
| The TMY data CSV | The time series behind the energy rows, at the interval you choose. See [Time series](/docs/energy/timeseries) |
| The bill of materials | Quantities computed from the same placed counts and the same grouping. See [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview) |
