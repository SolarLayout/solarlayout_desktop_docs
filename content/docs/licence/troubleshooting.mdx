---
title: Licence problems
description: Every state the access chip and the License / Subscription window can show, what each one means, and the fix — plus the 3-day offline grace.
---

The access chip in the ribbon always shows this device's status at a glance —
**● Active — until &lt;date&gt;**, **● Access expired** or **● Access
revoked** with a **Contact Us** button, or **● Access status unavailable** —
and on a device with no access at all it gives way to a gold **Get Free
Access** button. **Help ▸ License / Subscription…** opens the window with the
same state as a headline, a line explaining it, and the buttons that act on
it.

There is no licence file to find, copy or keep safe. Access belongs to your
account, covers up to three devices, and is checked online. This computer is
identified by its **Device ID** — the Windows registry value also shown under
Settings ▸ System ▸ About ▸ Device ID — which sits behind **Having trouble?**
at the bottom of the window with a **Copy** button.

## The states

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="✓ Active — until <date>">

**What it means.** This device has active access. The line under the headline
reads *"You have access until &lt;date&gt;."* and the buttons are **Access
Details**, **Support cases**, **Refresh** and **Close**.

<Screenshot id="licence-active" />

**Nothing to fix.** **Generate Layout** and the exports work.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Start your free trial">

**What it means.** This device has never had access requested for it, or a
previous request was never completed. The line reads *"All features are
available during your free trial. Activation takes one click in your
browser."* and the buttons are **Get Free Access** and **Contact Us**. This
is the only state in which the window opens by itself at launch.

<Screenshot id="licence-no-access" />

**Fix.** Click **Get Free Access**. The application opens your browser at the
activation page for this device
(`https://solarlayout.app/desktop/solarlayout?device=<DeviceID>`). Complete
activation there, then switch back to the window — it re-checks automatically
and flips to **✓ Active** without a manual **Refresh**. See
[Activate this device](/docs/licence/activate).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="⏳ Waiting for activation">

**What it means.** Shown right after clicking **Get Free Access**, while
activation is being completed in the browser. The line reads *"This will
update automatically when you return here after activating in your browser."*

**Fix.** Finish activating on the page that opened, then switch back to the
window. It checks again on its own; **Refresh** does the same thing if you want
to trigger it directly.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Access expired">

**What it means.** Your account's access has ended, on this device and every
other device on the account. The line reads *"Your free trial or subscription
has ended. Contact us to purchase, or to request a trial extension."* and the
ribbon chip reads **● Access expired** with a **Contact Us** button.

**Fix.** Click **Contact Us**, or write to SolarLayout at
[sales@solarlayout.app](mailto:sales@solarlayout.app), to purchase or to
request an extension. Once it is granted every device picks up the new date;
click **Refresh** to see it at once.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Access revoked">

**What it means.** Access on your account has been withdrawn; it ends on every
device at once. The line reads *"Access on this device has been revoked.
Contact us to restore it."* and the ribbon chip reads **● Access revoked**
with a **Contact Us** button.

**Fix.** Contact SolarLayout to resolve it, then click **Refresh**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Access status unavailable">

**What it means.** The application could not reach the server and has no
usable cached result to fall back on. The line reads *"We couldn't reach the
SolarLayout server. Check your connection and press Refresh."*

A good check is cached, so a brief outage does not stop work: the application
keeps working under its last known status for up to **3 days** without a
connection, and the ribbon chip reads **● Active — until &lt;date&gt;
(offline)** meanwhile. This state only appears once that offline grace has run
out, or on a machine that has never checked in successfully.

**Fix.** Reconnect to the internet and click **Refresh**. If the state
persists on a machine that is definitely online, contact SolarLayout — see
[What to send when you ask for help](#what-to-send-when-you-ask-for-help).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Other situations

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Generate Layout or an export is blocked">

Clicking **Generate Layout** or an export without active access opens the
**License / Subscription** window itself — there is no separate message. Read
the headline and find that state above. The SLD and BOM views are not blocked;
they simply have no layout to work from.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The activation page says You have used all 3 devices">

Your account already uses SolarLayout on three devices, so this one cannot be
added — and nothing frees a place: replacing a computer does not release its
slot. Contact SolarLayout at
[sales@solarlayout.app](mailto:sales@solarlayout.app) with the Device ID of
the new machine.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The activation page says This device is already in use">

This computer was activated under a different account. Sign in with that
account instead, or contact SolarLayout.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I updated or reinstalled the application and access is gone">

It should not be. Access is server-side and belongs to your account, not to
any file on disk, so it survives updates and reinstalls automatically — there
is nothing to re-load.

If the window still reports **Start your free trial** after an update or
reinstall:

1. **Click Refresh** in the License / Subscription window — the application
   may simply not have checked in yet since the update.
2. **Check the machine's internet connection.** The check needs to reach the
   server at least once after install.
3. **Compare the Device ID** under **Having trouble?** against the one listed
   on your account's details page. It should be unchanged after an update; a
   full reinstall of Windows, or a move to a different computer, can change
   it — an application update does not. A changed Device ID is a new device
   on the account: click **Get Free Access** again.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A second Windows user account shows no access">

It should not. The Device ID comes from the machine, not the Windows user
account, so a second account on the same PC reports the same Device ID and the
same access. If the second account shows **Start your free trial**, click
**Refresh**; if that does not resolve it, follow
[What to send when you ask for help](#what-to-send-when-you-ask-for-help)
below.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The Support button will not file a ticket">

Without active access the **Support** button in the ribbon shows *"You'll need
active access to file a support ticket. Start a free trial, or contact us
directly."* with **Get Free Access**, **Contact Us** and **Cancel**. Use
**Contact Us**, or write to
[sales@solarlayout.app](mailto:sales@solarlayout.app).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What to send when you ask for help" id="what-to-send-when-you-ask-for-help">

Send all of this in one message, so access can be checked without a round
trip:

- **The Device ID**, copied with the **Copy** button behind **Having
  trouble?** in the License / Subscription window — not retyped.
- **The exact headline and the line under it**, including any date it names.
- **The email address of your account.**
- **The app version**, shown at the right of the status bar.

Send it to SolarLayout at
[sales@solarlayout.app](mailto:sales@solarlayout.app), or use **Contact Us**
in the License / Subscription window to open the same route. See
[Getting help](/docs/support).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
