---
title: Activate this device
description: Add this device to your account's access from the License / Subscription window or the ribbon, complete it in your browser, and confirm it automatically.
---

Activation adds **this device** to your account's access — there is no file to
send, receive, copy or keep safe. Click **Get Free Access**, complete it in
your browser, and switch back: the application confirms it by itself. Access
belongs to your account and covers up to three devices; see
[How access works](/docs/licence/overview).

On a device that has never been activated the ribbon shows no access chip —
only a gold **Get Free Access** button, which does exactly what the button in
the **License / Subscription** window does. On that device the window also
opens by itself at launch.

Until access is active, **Generate Layout** and every export will not run.
Everything else in the application works, including the SLD and BOM views.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Help ▸ License / Subscription…

The **License / Subscription** window opens. On a device with no access the
headline reads **Start your free trial**, with *"All features are available
during your free trial. Activation takes one click in your browser."* under
it, then the buttons **Get Free Access**, **Contact Us**, **Refresh** and
**Close**. The Device ID is folded behind **Having trouble?** at the bottom;
you do not need it to activate.

<Screenshot id="licence-no-access" />
</Step>

<Step>
### Click Get Free Access

The headline changes at once to **⏳ Waiting for activation** — *"This will
update automatically when you return here after activating in your
browser."* — and your browser opens to the activation page for this device
(`https://solarlayout.app/desktop/solarlayout?device=<DeviceID>`).
</Step>

<Step>
### Complete activation in your browser

Sign in — or create an account — if the page asks, then click **Activate free
access for this device**. On your second or third computer the page says
*"This will be device N of 3 on your account."* and the button reads **Add
this device** instead.

If the page reads **You have used all 3 devices** or **This device is already
in use**, activation stops there — see
[Using more than one device](/docs/licence/overview#using-more-than-one-device)
for what each means and what to do.
</Step>

<Step>
### Return and confirm

Switch back to the **License / Subscription** window. It re-checks
automatically whenever it regains focus — no need to click anything. **Refresh**
checks immediately if you prefer.

A device with active access shows **✓ Active — until &lt;date&gt;**, with
*"You have access until &lt;date&gt;."* under it, and **Access Details**,
**Support cases**, **Refresh** and **Close** in place of the earlier buttons.
The ribbon chip now reads **● Active — until &lt;date&gt;**.

<Screenshot id="licence-active" />

**Generate Layout** now works. Go to
[Your first layout](/docs/first-layout).
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
If the headline reads **Access expired** — *"Your free trial or subscription
has ended. Contact us to purchase, or to request a trial extension."* — or
**Access revoked** — *"Access on this device has been revoked. Contact us to
restore it."* — **Get Free Access** is not offered. Use **Contact Us** in the
window, which opens [solarlayout.app/contact](https://solarlayout.app/contact),
or write to SolarLayout at
[sales@solarlayout.app](mailto:sales@solarlayout.app). See
[Licence problems](/docs/licence/troubleshooting).
</Callout>

## If you try before activating

Clicking **Generate Layout** or any export before access is active opens the
**License / Subscription** window itself, on the headline for this device's
state. There is no separate message to dismiss: act on the window, then try
again.

## Working offline

A successful check is cached, so the application keeps working for up to three
days without an internet connection; the ribbon chip reads
**● Active — until &lt;date&gt; (offline)** meanwhile. If it cannot reach the
server and has no usable cached result, the window reads **Access status
unavailable** — *"We couldn't reach the SolarLayout server. Check your
connection and press Refresh."*

## Updates, reinstalls and other accounts

**Updates and reinstalls do not require reactivating.** Access is checked
against the server and belongs to your account, not to a file on disk, so it
survives reinstalls and updates automatically. See
[Updates and reinstalling](/docs/install/updates).

**Another computer is activated the same way.** Your account covers up to
three devices, and every one of them shows the same end date.

**A second Windows user account on the same PC has the same Device ID.**
Access carries over to that account without doing anything further.
