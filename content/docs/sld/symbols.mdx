The **SYMBOLS** card holds what a **single-line diagram** — SLD — is drawn from. It
appears when you click **Edit diagram** in the SLD view. Placing a symbol takes two
clicks: **click the tile, then click the drawing** where it goes. The symbol becomes
an element you can select, move, rotate, group, copy or delete like any other. The
tools that do all of that are on
[Building the single-line diagram](/docs/sld/overview#editing-the-diagram).

<Screenshot id="sld-tools" maxWidth={360} />

The names below are the panel's own, in its own order, so you can read this page
with the panel open beside it. One group is open at a time, starting with
**Generation**. **Find a symbol** at the top of the card searches by name; with no
match it reads *"No symbol has that name."*

## Placing a symbol

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click the tile

One click arms that symbol. Nothing is placed yet.
</Step>

<Step>
### Click the drawing

The symbol lands where you click, and the tile stays armed, so you can place several.
The hint at the bottom left reads *"Click to place · Esc to stop"*. Press the tile
again or press **Esc** to go back to **Select**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Position and orient it

Drag it into place with **Select**, and use **Rotate 90°** on the **SELECTION** card
if it needs turning.
</Step>

<Step>
### Wire it in

Use **Wire** to connect it to the bus or to the device beside it.
</Step>

<Step>
### Label it

Use **Text** to write the tag and the rating beside the symbol. A symbol carries its
shape and its position; everything printed next to it is text you add.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Generation

The DC end of the plant, and the machine that turns DC into AC.

| Symbol | What it represents |
| --- | --- |
| **PV module** | The photovoltaic modules — drawn once for a string, an array or the whole DC field, depending on how much of the plant the diagram is showing |
| **Y harness** | A DC harness joining two strings into one cable |
| **SMB** | A string monitoring box — the field enclosure that collects strings and passes their combined DC output on to a central inverter without converting it |
| **SMB without fuses** | The same box, drawn with no fuses in it |
| **String inverter** | The inverter standing out in the field in a string-inverter design |
| **Central inverter** | The inverter inside the control room in a central-inverter design |

A string-inverter plant has no string monitoring boxes; a central-inverter plant has
both, with the boxes in the field and the inverters in the control room. See
[Choosing a design mode](/docs/design-modes).

## LV switchgear

Where the converted output is gathered, and the low-voltage devices that isolate and
interrupt it.

| Symbol | What it represents |
| --- | --- |
| **ACCB** | An AC combiner box — the panel a group of string inverters lands on, feeding one low-voltage winding of a transformer |
| **Fuse** | A fuse |
| **MCB** | A miniature circuit breaker |
| **MCCB** | A moulded-case circuit breaker |
| **CB on / off / trip** | Circuit-breaker status indication |
| **Isolator + earth** | An isolator with its earth connection |
| **ISO + earth sw.** | An isolator with an earth switch, drawn as one device |

The two isolator symbols are not the same drawing. Choose per the arrangement your
drawing standard expects, and use one of them consistently across the sheet. The
breaker symbols are distinguished by type, not by rating: a rating is something you
write beside the symbol with **Text**.

## Transformers

Five symbols. The first four differ in how many windings they show. Pick the one that
matches the machine on the drawing rather than the one that looks closest.

| Symbol | What it represents |
| --- | --- |
| **2-winding** | One low-voltage and one medium-voltage winding |
| **3-winding** | Two low-voltage windings and the medium-voltage winding |
| **4-winding** | Three low-voltage windings and the medium-voltage winding |
| **Multi-winding** | Four or more low-voltage windings and the medium-voltage winding |
| **Power transformer** | The power transformer at the main control room |

An inverter duty transformer, IDT, takes one low-voltage winding per combiner box or
central inverter feeding it. How many feeders share a transformer follows the forms
on the [ACCB & Transformer card](/docs/inputs/accb-and-transformers). Since one symbol
draws every machine above four windings, write the count beside it with **Text**.

## MV and protection

The medium-voltage side, and the protection and metering a panel carries.

| Symbol | What it represents |
| --- | --- |
| **MV panel** | A medium-voltage panel, the switchgear the transformer feeds and the point the outgoing line leaves from |
| **VCB** | A vacuum circuit breaker |
| **VT (2 core)** | A voltage transformer with two cores, one for metering and one for protection |
| **VT (open delta)** | A voltage transformer connected in open delta |
| **CT** | A current transformer — stepping line current down to a measurable value for metering and protection |
| **Relay** | A protection relay. Placing it opens the **Protection relay** window, which asks for a **Device number**: pick an ANSI device number or type your own, and it is drawn inside the symbol. **Place relay** arms it |
| **Lightning arr.** | A lightning arrester — LA — the surge-diverting device on the line |
| **MFM** | A multi-function meter, reading voltage, current, power, power factor and energy in one instrument |
| **Voltmeter** | A voltmeter |
| **VSS** | A voltmeter selector switch — the switch that points one voltmeter at each phase pair in turn |
| **R-Y-B phase** | Phase indication for the three phases, labelled R, Y and B |

Instrument transformers and the instruments they feed are drawn as separate symbols,
wired together, because that is how the panel is built: the current transformer sits
on the line and the meter sits on the panel door.

## My symbols

Your own symbols, then **Import image…**, which traces an image into a symbol. A
symbol you trace draws as line art and stays available for later projects. Right-click
one of your symbols to remove it. See
[Importing diagrams and custom symbols](/docs/sld/import-and-custom-symbols).

## Which symbols arrive pre-rotated

The **PV module**, the **String inverter** and **Central inverter**, and the **2-**,
**3-**, **4-winding** and **Multi-winding** transformers are placed **pre-rotated to
270°**, so that they line up with the horizontal bus the rest of the diagram is built
along. This holds in both electrical topologies.

Every other symbol, the **Power transformer** included, is placed as it is drawn on
its tile. If one needs turning — an arrester on a vertical drop, a breaker across the
bus rather than along it — select it and use **Rotate 90°**.

<Callout>
A symbol that looks turned the moment you place it is almost always one of these, and
almost always correct. Check it against the bus before rotating it back.
</Callout>

## The cards under the symbols

The **SELECTION** card rotates, copies, deletes and groups what you have selected, in
90° steps. **More ▸** holds the choices that replace the sheet. Both are on
[Building the single-line diagram](/docs/sld/overview#editing-the-diagram).

## The symbols the automatic build places

The automatic diagram draws its chain from a small part of this palette: the
equipment whose counts and ratings it can read from the plant.

| Topology | Symbols in the chain |
| --- | --- |
| **String inverter** | **PV module**, an inverter, **ACCB**, a transformer, **MV panel**, and the grid |
| **Central inverter** | **PV module**, **SMB**, an inverter, a transformer, **MV panel**, and the grid |

Everything else on this page is yours to add on top of that chain. That is the normal
way round: the automatic build settles the equipment count and the connection order,
and the protection and metering detail is drawing work. See
[Building a diagram from the plant](/docs/sld/auto-build).
