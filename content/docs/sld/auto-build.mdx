---
title: Building a diagram from the plant
description: "Generate: Automatic draws a starter single-line diagram from the layout, the ACCB & Transformer grouping and the inverter file; Full SLD expands it to one row per feeder."
---

**Generate: Automatic** on the SLD TOOLS panel draws a starter **single-line
diagram** — SLD — from the plant you have already laid out. It reads the
layout's equipment counts, the **electrical grouping** — the combiner boxes,
the transformers, their ratings and voltages, as the
[ACCB & Transformer card](/docs/inputs/accb-and-transformers) produces them —
and the inverter file, then places the chain from the modules through to the
grid and annotates each block with how many of that item there are and what it
is rated at. What you get is a correct-by-count skeleton on the drawing sheet,
which you then edit.

It never reads the bill of materials. Editing the materials list, rebuilding it
or loading a template cannot change the diagram.

It is a starting point, not a finished drawing. The chain it places is the
generation-to-grid path; the protection, switching and instrumentation an
issued SLD carries is drawing work you do on top of it.

## Before you run it

The automatic build reads the plant as it stands at the moment you use it, so
the plant has to exist first.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Generate the layout

The equipment counts come from the placed plant — the inverters or string
monitoring boxes, and the control rooms they feed — and the grouping is made
when they are placed. With no layout there is nothing to count, and choosing
**Automatic** only reports *"Generate a layout first, then choose Automatic."*
See [Your first layout](/docs/first-layout).

**No energy calculation is needed** — running the yield calculation first adds
nothing to the diagram.
</Step>

<Step>
### Load the inverter file

The ratings and voltages come from the inverter file loaded on the inverter
card. Without one the diagram is still drawn, by count: the transformers read
*rating needs OND*, the low-voltage side *LV needs OND*, and the inverter's
rating and the plant's MW AC figure are left off. See
[Inverter settings](/docs/inputs/inverter).
</Step>

<Step>
### Open the SLD view and choose Automatic

Click the **SLD** tab (**Ctrl+5**). Nothing is drawn on entry: the view shows
the diagram already stored, if there is one, and the **Generate:** row at the
top of the tools panel offers **Automatic** and **Manual**. Choose
**Automatic**. The chain is drawn on the sheet and the status line reads
*"Auto-built SLD (simple feeder) — edit as needed."*
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
A diagram on the sheet is never redrawn on its own. After you generate the
layout again, change a rule on the **ACCB & Transformer** card or load an
inverter file, choose **Automatic** again so that the chain and the plant
describe each other — and expect the sheet to be rebuilt, not merged with your
edits.
</Callout>

## The chain it draws

Which chain you get follows the inverter choice made at launch, because the
two topologies convert the DC in different places. See
[Choosing a design mode](/docs/design-modes).

<Tabs items={["String inverter", "Central inverter"]}>
<Tab value="String inverter">

```
PV → String Inverter (×N) → ACCB (×n) → Transformer (×n) → MV Panel → Grid
```

| Stage | What it is | What the build writes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PV** | The module field feeding the inverters | The module count and wattage, and *PV Array* with the string count |
| **String Inverter (×N)** | The string inverters placed across the plant. `N` is how many the layout placed | The rating in kVA from the inverter file, and *String Inverter ×N* |
| **ACCB (×n)** | The AC combiner boxes the inverters land on. `n` is the grouping's count: in each ICR block, the block's inverters ÷ **Inverters per ACCB, max**, rounded up | *ACCB ×n* |
| **Transformer** | The inverter duty transformers taking the collected output up to medium voltage | The rating, such as *16,000 kVA* or a range *12,500–16,000 kVA*, the voltages such as *33 kV/800 V*, and *Transformer ×n (k-Wdg)* |
| **MV Panel** | The medium-voltage switchgear the transformers feed | *33 kV Switchgear* — the card's **MV voltage** |
| **Grid** | The outgoing connection | *GRID 33 kV* |

The wires between the blocks carry the multiplicities: the modules and strings
on one inverter, the most inverters on one ACCB, the most ACCBs on one
transformer, and the transformer count.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Central inverter">

```
PV → SMB (×N) → Central Inverter (×n) → Transformer (×n) → MV Panel → Grid
```

| Stage | What it is | What the build writes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PV** | The module field feeding the string monitoring boxes | The module count and wattage, and the string count |
| **SMB (×N)** | The string monitoring boxes placed across the plant, each passing its strings' DC output on without converting it | *SMB ×N* |
| **Central Inverter (×n)** | The central inverters inside the control rooms. `n` is the grouping's count, made per ICR block | *Central Inverter ×n*, with the SMBs per central inverter on its wire |
| **Transformer** | The transformers the central inverters land on | The rating and voltages, the winding count, and the central inverters per transformer on its wire |
| **MV Panel** | The medium-voltage switchgear the transformers feed | The card's **MV voltage** |
| **Grid** | The outgoing connection | The same voltage |

There is no AC leg before the inverter here, because the conversion happens
inside the control room rather than in the field.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

The sheet is headed *SINGLE-LINE DIAGRAM*, with a line giving the plant's MWp
DC, its MW AC (with an inverter file), the topology and the number of ICRs.

Both chains end the same way, and both are drawn along a horizontal bus —
which is why the inverter and every transformer are placed already rotated to
line up with it. See [Building the single-line diagram](/docs/sld/overview).

## Simple feeder or full SLD

The build has two forms, switched by the **Full SLD (per-ICR / per-feeder)**
checkbox on the tools panel. It applies to the automatic build only.

| Full SLD | What is drawn | Paper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Off** — the simple feeder | One annotated feeder, with each block carrying a **×N** multiplicity for how many of it the plant holds | A3 |
| **On** | **One row per feeder** — per ACCB in a string-inverter design, per central inverter in a central-inverter design — across the whole plant, labelled **ACCB-1**, **ACCB-2** … or **C.Inv-1** … and grouped onto their transformers **TX-1**, **TX-2** …, each marked with its own winding count. The rating and voltages are written under **TX-1**; the transformers feed the MV panel and the grid | A3 up to 10 feeders, A1 up to 28, A0 beyond |

<Screenshot id="sld-auto-full" />

Ticking or unticking the box while **Automatic** is chosen **rebuilds the
diagram** in the other form, and
the status line then reads *"Auto-built SLD (full (per-ICR/feeder)) — edit as
needed."* for the full form. Choose the form before you start editing. The
simple feeder is the readable one on a plant with dozens of machines; the full
form shows every feeder and every transformer separately.

### The transformer symbol

The transformer symbol is chosen from the feeders on the transformer, one
low-voltage winding each:

| Feeders on the transformer | Symbol |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 2-winding |
| 2 | 3-winding |
| 3 | 4-winding |
| 4 or more | 5-winding |

The simple feeder uses the most feeders on any one transformer; the full SLD
uses each transformer's own. A transformer's winding count is its feeders plus
one, so if your card allows more than four feeders per transformer, check the
winding count written on the sheet against that and correct it with
**📝 Text** where it differs.

## The grouping it draws

The counts, ratings and voltages on the chain are those of the plant's
electrical grouping, set on the **ACCB & Transformer** card on the Electrical
tab — **Transformer** in a central-inverter design:

| Rule on the card | Default |
| --- | --- |
| **Inverters per ACCB, max** | 15 |
| **ACCBs per transformer, max** *(or central inverters per transformer)* | 4 |
| **Transformer size limit** | 17.2 MVA, but never above the largest listed rating — 16,000 kVA with the default list |
| **Standard transformer ratings (kVA)** | `2500, 3150, 5000, 6300, 8000, 10000, 12500, 16000` |
| **MV voltage** | 33.0 kV |

The grouping is made per ICR block, and a block whose load exceeds the limit
gets more transformers. Each transformer takes the smallest listed rating at or
above its load. The full rules are on the card's own page.

**The diagram, the bill of materials, the Summary view and the cable schedule
all read the same grouping**, so the box and transformer counts agree between
them — there is no reconciliation to do.

<Callout>
If your scheme groups equipment differently — a different number of inverters
per box, or transformer sizes you have already fixed with a supplier — set it on
the card: the inverters per ACCB, the ACCBs per transformer, and your own list
of ratings. Then choose **Automatic** again, and the diagram and the materials
list follow your scheme.
</Callout>

## What the annotations mean

Each block on the chain is annotated with two things:

- **A multiplicity**, written as `×N` — how many of that item the plant
  carries. One inverter symbol carrying a multiplicity stands for every
  inverter in the plant, drawn once. That is normal single-line practice, and
  it is what keeps the sheet readable on a plant with dozens of machines.
- **A rating** — for the transformers, from the grouping; for the inverter, the
  nominal AC power in the inverter file.

Both are put on the sheet by the build rather than typed by you. Where one is
wrong — a rating that does not match the machine you are buying, or a count
you have since revised — correct it on the sheet with the **📝 Text** tool, or
change the card and choose **Automatic** again.

## What to do next

Treat the result as a first draft, in this order:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Check every rating

The ratings come from the grouping and the inverter file. Read each one
against the equipment you are actually procuring, and correct the annotation
where it differs.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add what the build does not cover

The chain carries the generation-to-grid path. Isolators, breakers,
instrument transformers, meters, relays, arresters and phase indication are all
yours to place from the palette and wire in. See [Symbol reference](/docs/sld/symbols).
</Step>

<Step>
### Complete the title block

The legend lists the chain's symbols and the notes carry four standard notes,
but nothing identifies the project — no company name, no project name, no
document number or revision. Write them in with **📝 Text**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fit and export

Use **⤢ Fit** to see the whole sheet, then export it from the tools panel, or
leave it to be carried into the Detailed Project Report as the report's
diagram page. See [Exporting the diagram](/docs/sld/export).
</Step>
</Steps>
