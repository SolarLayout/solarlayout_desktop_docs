title: Building the single-line diagram
description: The SLD view — the diagram built from your layout, how it follows the layout, the printed sheet, and the editing tools for changing it or drawing your own.
---

The **SLD** tab (**Ctrl+7**) holds the plant's **single-line diagram** — SLD,
the one-line electrical schematic that shows how the modules reach the grid.
With a layout on screen the diagram is built as soon as you open the view, and
you review it first: the left column says whether it still matches the layout,
and you open the editing tools only when you want to change something.

The diagram is a drawing, not an electrical study. The automatic build draws the
chain from the plant's own equipment counts and ratings. The protection,
switching and instrumentation an issued SLD carries are yours to add.

## Opening the view

Click the **SLD** tab, or press **Ctrl+7**. The right side shows the diagram.
The left column shows a blue banner **SLD Preparation** with an **Exit SLD**
button, and under it the **DIAGRAM** and **EXPORT** cards and **Edit diagram**.
They replace the stage tabs and the **Generate Layout** button while the view
owns the column; **Exit SLD** hands the column back. The view tabs and the tools
beside them are described on [Views](/docs/workspace/views).

What you find depends on the project:

| When you open the view | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| A layout, and no diagram yet | The automatic diagram, built on entry with no question. See [Building a diagram from the plant](/docs/sld/auto-build) |
| A diagram already in the project | That diagram, with its state on the **DIAGRAM** card |
| No layout and no diagram | *"No layout yet"*, *"Generate a layout and the single line diagram is built from it."* and **Back to Layout**. **Draw by hand** on the left starts a blank sheet instead |

## The DIAGRAM card

The top of the card says what the diagram is and whether it matches the layout.
The status bar carries the same news on entry, such as *"SLD up to date with the
layout."*

| Verdict | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting for a layout** | Nothing to build from yet. **Summary** and **Full** are off, with the note *"Available once a layout exists."* |
| **Up to date with the layout** | The automatic diagram matches the plant. The line under it gives the figures, such as *"Automatic · 54.28 MWp · 159 inverters · 8 transformers"*, and counts your edits once there are any. An edited diagram also offers **Rebuild — discards 1 edit** |
| **Out of date** | The layout changed after this diagram was built. See [When the layout changes](#when-the-layout-changes) |
| **Drawn by hand** | A drawing that doesn't follow the layout. **Replace with the automatic diagram…** asks inline before it replaces your drawing and any traced background; Undo brings both back |
| **Couldn't build the diagram** | The reason follows. A diagram already on screen is kept |

Under the verdict, **Summary | Full** chooses between one chain for the whole
plant and a row for every ACCB or central inverter. It applies to the automatic
diagram only, and on an edited diagram the switch rebuilds and discards your
edits, which you can undo.

A notice under the switch names anything the sheet cannot print yet, with the
button that fixes it:

| Notice | Button | On the sheet meanwhile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *"Transformer ratings need the inverter file (OND)."* | **Load OND…** | *— kVA* and *33 kV / — V* |
| A count of transformers that fit no listed rating | **Electrical tab…** | *— kVA* on those transformers |
| *"The inverter file has no output voltage, so the sheet prints “— V”."* | — | *— V* |

## When the layout changes

The diagram follows the layout in a way that depends on whether you have touched
it.

| The diagram is | When the layout changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Automatic, untouched** | It rebuilds at once, as one undo step. The status bar reads *"SLD rebuilt from the new layout"*, with the capacity, and *"It had no edits."* |
| **Automatic, edited** | It is kept and marked out of date, so that your edits are not lost without your say |
| **Drawn by hand** | Nothing. A hand drawing never follows the layout |

A marked diagram has a banner over the drawing, *"The layout changed after this
diagram was built"*, with the figures for both.

<Screenshot id="sld-out-of-date" />

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rebuild — discards 1 edit** | Builds the diagram again from the layout. Your edits go, and **Undo** in the toast that appears brings them back. The button names how many edits it discards |
| **Keep this diagram** | Leaves the drawing as it is. A chip *"Kept · out of date"* stays on it with both capacities, and a further layout change marks it again. Keep cannot be undone |

The diagram's **PDF…** and **DWG…** wait until you choose one or the other. A
kept diagram exports as it stands, and the **EXPORT** card says so.

## Diagram and Sheet

Two controls at the bottom right of the drawing choose what you look at, and
neither is saved with the project.

| Control | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Diagram** | The drawing alone, framed to fill the view. The view opens here |
| **Sheet** | The sheet as it prints, with its frame, legend, notes and title block |

### What the sheet holds

<Screenshot id="sld-page" caption="The sheet as it prints" />

| Part of the sheet | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Outer border** and **inner frame** | The frame the drawing area sits inside |
| **Zone grid** | Numbers 8 down to 1 across the sheet and letters F down to A along its side, so a comment can name the zone it refers to |
| **LEGENDS** | A panel in the right-hand band, keyed to the symbols on the sheet |
| **NOTES** | Three standard notes: the ratings are indicative and to be confirmed against the final GTP and BOM, cable sizing and protection are to be finalised separately, and quantities marked ×N are per-plant multiplicities |
| **Title block** | The block at the foot of the right-hand band |

### The title block

The title block fills itself from the project's company and project details, so
you do not type into it. Set the company with **File ▸ Company…**; see
[Company details](/docs/exports/company). The project details are edited in the
[Export deliverables](/docs/exports/deliverables) window. A field with nothing to
print shows **—**.

| Field | What it prints |
| --- | --- |
| **COMPANY** | The company's name, with its logo |
| **PROJECT** | The project's name and code, or the file name in muted italic |
| **CLIENT** · **LOCATION** | From the project details |
| **DRAWING TITLE** | *Single line diagram — Summary*, *— Full*, or *Single line diagram* on a hand drawing |
| **DRAWN** | The project's entry, else the company's initials |
| **CHECKED** · **APPROVED** | From the project details |
| **DRAWING NO.** · **REV.** | **—** on this sheet |
| **DATE** | Today |
| **SCALE** · **SHEET** · **PAPER** | *NTS*, *1 of 1* and the paper size |

### Paper size

A diagram with many rows needs more paper, so the paper size follows the number
of rows: **A3** up to 10, **A1** up to 28, **A0** beyond.

## Getting around

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Mouse wheel | Zooms at the pointer, from 25 % to 800 % of the fitted view |
| Plain drag | Pans, while you are reviewing |
| **Space** + drag, or the middle button | Pans while you are editing, where a plain drag belongs to the tool |
| **−** · **+** (keys or buttons; **=** also zooms in) | Zoom out or in by 20 % |
| **Fit**, the percentage button, or **F** | Fits the drawing to the view |

Text keeps its sheet size against the drawing as you zoom.

## Editing the diagram

Click **Edit diagram** — *"Move symbols, add text, or draw your own."* The left
column changes to the editing panel. The status bar reads *"Editing the SLD.
Ctrl+Z undoes."*

<Screenshot id="edit-panel" maxWidth={360} />

The header stays pinned at the top: **Editing diagram**, **Undo**, **Redo**, and
**Done**, with a line that counts your changes. **Done** returns to the review
panel, and any changes are counted in *"The diagram is marked edited."*

### Select, Wire and Text

**Select | Wire | Text** chooses what a press on the drawing does, and a hint at
the bottom left of the drawing repeats it.

| Tool | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Select** | Click to select. Drag a selected element to move it. Drag on empty space for a marquee. **Shift** adds to the selection. Double-click a text or a relay to edit it |
| **Wire** | Press where the run starts and release where it ends |
| **Text** | Click where the label goes. The **SLD text** window takes the text, which can run to several lines, and its **Font**, **Size** (4–96 pt, 8 to begin with) and **Colour** |

A wire is an element in its own right, so **Select** moves it and **Delete**
removes it without disturbing the symbols at either end.

### Symbols

The **SYMBOLS** card holds the palette, in groups with one open at a time. Click
a tile and then click the drawing; the tile stays armed until you press it again
or press **Esc**. See [Symbol reference](/docs/sld/symbols).

### The Selection card

The **SELECTION** card acts on what you have selected. With nothing selected it
reads *"Nothing selected"* and its buttons are off.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rotate 90°** | Turns the selection by a quarter turn |
| **Copy** | Duplicates the selection 8 mm right and 8 mm down on the sheet, selected and ready to drag |
| **Delete** | Removes the selection. The **Delete** and **Backspace** keys do the same |
| **Group** | Binds two or more elements so they move as one |
| **Ungroup** | Releases a group back into its parts |

The case for grouping is repetition. A feeder bay — breaker, current transformer,
voltage transformer, meter, isolator — is the same handful of symbols and wires
every time. Draw it once, group it, and copy the group along the bus.

### More

**More ▸** at the foot of the panel folds away the choices that replace what is on
the sheet.

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Start a blank sheet** | Empties the sheet for a drawing of your own |
| **Trace over a PDF / DXF…** | Brings an existing drawing onto the sheet. See [Importing diagrams and custom symbols](/docs/sld/import-and-custom-symbols) |
| **Remove the traced background** | Takes away a scanned page. It shows only when there is one |
| **Clear the diagram** | Removes every symbol, wire and label |

### Undo

**Undo** holds 100 steps. Every edit and every choice that replaces the drawing
is one step: a rebuild, a replace, a blank sheet, a clear, a switch between
**Summary** and **Full**, the diagram following the layout, a trace, a change of
background. **Ctrl+Z** undoes and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** or **Ctrl+Y** redoes, in the
review panel as well as while editing. After a choice that discards work, a toast
at the top of the drawing offers **Undo** at once.

The undo history is not saved. Opening a project starts it empty.

## Drawing by hand

**Draw by hand** (with no layout) and **Start a blank sheet** (under **More ▸**)
both give you an empty sheet, for a diagram that is not the plant's own chain: a
grid connection scheme, say. *"It won't follow the layout."* The **DIAGRAM** card
then reads **Drawn by hand**, with the number of elements on the sheet.

## Checking a diagram

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the SLD view

Click the **SLD** tab. The automatic diagram is there if a layout is. Clear any
notice on the **DIAGRAM** card, such as **Load OND…**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the form

Choose **Summary** or **Full** before you start editing, because switching
discards edits.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add what the chain does not carry

Click **Edit diagram**, place the protection, switching and instrumentation
symbols your drawing standard calls for, and connect them with **Wire**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check every rating

The automatic build annotates what it places from the electrical grouping and the
inverter file. Read each annotation against the equipment you are buying.
</Step>

<Step>
### Read the sheet

Click **Done**, then **Sheet**, and read the title block as it will print. Set
anything missing with **File ▸ Company…** or in the project details.
</Step>

<Step>
### Export and save

Export with **PDF…** or **DWG…**, and save the project: the diagram is part of it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
The automatic build is a starting point derived from counts and grouping rules,
not a protection design. Read every rating and every device before the drawing
goes to anyone.
</Callout>

## Where the diagram goes

**PDF…** and **DWG…** on the **EXPORT** card write this diagram as you built,
edited or traced it. See [Exporting the diagram](/docs/sld/export).

The [deliverables package](/docs/exports/deliverables) carries a diagram of its
own, drawn automatically from the design. Your edits and tracing do not reach it.

**Save project** keeps the diagram with the rest of the project: its elements,
**Summary** or **Full**, its paper and any traced background, along with what it
was built from, so a reopened project still knows whether it matches the layout.
See [Projects](/docs/projects).
