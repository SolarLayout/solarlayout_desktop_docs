description: Placing the main control room and other structures from the Tools tab, moving them, and the perimeter street lights you enable before generating.
---

Some site equipment is not placed with the layout. The **MCR** — main control
room — and any generic objects are placed by clicking a position on the plot,
from the **Grid connection & Objects** card of the **Tools** tab. Unit
substations are placed the same way on a multi-plot site. Street lights are a
setting on the **Site** tab that you turn on before you generate.

If a solar park or another party provides the main control room and the substation,
see [Grid connection by others](/docs/layout/grid-connection).

The inverter control rooms — ICRs — are different: they are counted from the
plant capacity and sited automatically. See
[Inverter control rooms](/docs/layout/icr).

| Equipment | How it arrives | When | Size set in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MCR** — main control room | You click **Place MCR**, then click the plot | After a layout exists | **Structures & Shadow**, the **MCR** row |
| **Object** — anything else on the site | You click **Place Object**, size it, then click the plot | Once there is a boundary; a layout is not needed | The **Place object** window |
| **USS** — unit substation | You click **Place MCR**, then click each plot | Multi-plot sites only | **Structures & Shadow**, the **USS** row |
| **Street lights** | Placed with the layout | Enabled **before** generating | **Structures & Shadow**, the street light rows |

## The placement card

Until a layout exists the **Tools** tab's tooltip reads *"Obstructions, the MCR and
most studies need a layout; Robot count from DXF works without one"* and the card's
buttons are greyed out, **Place Object** aside. Once a
layout is on screen, the **Grid connection & Objects** card offers **Place
MCR**, **Remove MCR**, **Place Object** and **Remove Objects**.

Placing or moving the main control room, a unit substation or an object starts
work of its own on the plant. One run happens at a time: while it runs this card
is disabled, with the **Obstructions** card beside it, and a change you make
meanwhile waits until it finishes. See
[Cable routing](/docs/layout/cables#one-cable-run-at-a-time).

## Placing the main control room

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click Place MCR

The button changes to **Cancel Placement**, the view switches to Layout, and a bar over
the plot reads *"Click inside the boundary to place the MCR."* A frame goes round the
plot, the cursor becomes a cross, and a ghost at true scale follows the pointer,
labelled *"MCR · 15 × 8 m"*.

<Screenshot id="placing-mcr-ghost" />

Press **Esc**, or click **Cancel · Esc** on the bar or **Cancel Placement** on the card,
to stop without placing anything. **Space** + drag pans while you look for the spot.
</Step>

<Step>
### Click the position on the plot

The main control room is placed at the point you click. A spot outside the plant
boundary is refused, under the pointer as well as on the click, with *"This spot is
outside the boundary. Move inside the plot."* Nothing is placed and the tool stays
armed, so you can click again in the right place.

<Screenshot id="placing-mcr-refused" />
</Step>

<Step>
### Read the result

The bar reports what the building cost, in the form *"MCR placed. n tables removed, DC
capacity a → b MWp."*, and stays for 12 seconds or until you close it. The tables under
the building and under its shadow are gone. The card keeps a line in the form *"MCR placed
in &lt;plot&gt;. n tables removed under it and its shadow."*

The bar also gives the next step:

| The bar says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| *"The cables on this plot were cleared. Generate again to route them."* | The plot had cables, and they must be routed again around the building |
| *"Generate again to route the MV cables."* | **Calculate cables** is ticked, and nothing has been routed yet |
| *"To route its MV cables, tick Calculate cables and generate again."* | **Calculate cables** is off. **Go to Calculate cables** on the bar takes you to it |
</Step>

<Step>
### Generate again to route the medium-voltage cables

Placing the room gives the medium-voltage network its destination; it does not route
anything. Tick **Calculate cables** on the **Cables** card of the Electrical tab if it
is not already on, and click **Generate Layout**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="mcr-placed-regenerate-hint" />

After that run the status line adds *"| MV cable: n m"*, the **MV cables (ICR→MCR)** row
of the Layers popover has something to draw, and the **MV cable (m)** row of the Summary
view is filled in. **Remove MCR** is enabled while a room exists.

<Screenshot id="mcr-placed-hint" />

**Remove MCR** takes the room away again. To put it somewhere else, move it instead:
see [Moving a building](#moving-a-building).

### The footprint

The main control room's size comes from your own fields on the
**Structures & Shadow (keep-clear)** card of the Site tab, not from a fixed
value:

| Field | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MCR** length (east–west) | 15.0 | m |
| **MCR** width (north–south) | 8.0 | m |
| **MCR** height | 5.0 | m |

Length is always the east–west dimension and width the north–south one, so a
building entered as 15 by 8 is wide across the rows, not along them. The height
drives the shadow rather than the plan area. Set these before you place the building;
see [Structures and shadow](/docs/inputs/structures-and-shadow) for the whole card,
including the **Clear tables in shadows** option, which is **on** by default and
removes tables that fall inside the building's year-round shadow.

## Why the main control room comes first

Medium-voltage routing runs **from every inverter control room to the main
control room**. Until the main control room exists there is no destination, so
**no medium-voltage cable is routed at all** — the **MV cable (m)** and **MV trench
(m)** rows of the Summary view read *needs an MCR on the layout*, even on a run with
cable calculation switched on.

<Callout type="warn">
Medium-voltage cables are routed on the **next Generate**, not when the room is
placed. Until you generate again with **Calculate cables** on, the MV row of the
Layers popover has nothing to show and the Summary's **MV cable (m)** has no figure,
however long ago you dropped the room.
</Callout>

The order that works is therefore:

1. Generate the layout.
2. Place the main control room.
3. With **Calculate cables** ticked, generate again.
4. Read the medium-voltage figures.

Place it where the plant's export actually leaves the site — usually near the
point of interconnection — because the trunk network is built to reach it and
its position sets the whole medium-voltage cable length. Moving it changes that
length. See [Cable routing and trenches](/docs/layout/cables) for how the trunk
is formed and what the reported lengths mean.

## Placing generic objects

An object is anything on the site that keeps modules off the ground around it
and that your boundary file does not carry: a tree, a pole, a tank, an existing
shed, a structure the survey missed.

**Place Object** does not wait for a layout: it is enabled once a boundary file is
selected, so you can mark a tree or a tank before the first run and let the layout be
built around it.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click Place Object and size it

The **Place object** window opens. It starts at 5.0 × 5.0 m and 10.0 m tall the first
time, and afterwards at the last size you used in this session.

| Field | First value | Range | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Length (E-W)** | 5.0 | 0–500 | m |
| **Width (N-S)** | 5.0 | 0–500 | m |
| **Height** | 10.0 | 0–200 | m |

**Place on layout** stays greyed out until the length and width are more than 0. A height
of 0 casts no shadow, so only the footprint is kept clear.

<Screenshot id="place-object" maxWidth={400} />
</Step>

<Step>
### Click where the object stands

Click **Place on layout**. The plot shows the same bar, frame and ghost as the main
control room, here *"Click inside the boundary to place the object."* A spot outside the
boundary is refused the same way, and **Esc** stops.

<Screenshot id="placing-object-ghost" />
</Step>

<Step>
### Place the next one, or finish

After each object the window opens again with a line saying what was placed, so you can
size and place another. **Done** ends.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Remove Objects** clears the objects you have placed.

The point of an object is the **shadow**, not the footprint. With **Clear tables in
shadows** on, the year-round shadow of each object across the shadow window clears the
tables it falls on — which is exactly what you want for a tree that no boundary file will
ever describe. The height is what makes that shadow long, so it is the field to get right.

## Unit substations on a multi-plot site

A **USS** — unit substation — exists only on a site whose boundary file contains more than
one plant boundary.

Click **Place MCR** and the bar reads *"Click a plot to place its USS. The last plot gets
the MCR."*, with a count of the plots done. Click inside each plot in turn: every plot you
click receives a unit substation, marked with a tick, and the last one receives the main
control room, with the bar reading *"Click the last plot to place the MCR."* Each plot's
inverter control rooms then route their medium-voltage cables to the building in their own
plot instead of running across country to the main control room.

A plot that already has its building is refused with *"&lt;plot&gt; already has its USS or MCR.
Click a different plot."* **Stop · Esc** on the bar ends the round early.

| Field | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **USS** length (east–west) | 5.0 | m |
| **USS** width (north–south) | 4.0 | m |
| **USS** height | 5.0 | m |

The link from a unit substation onward to the main control room is an overhead
line or a buried cable, and it is **not** part of the automatic medium-voltage
routing — plan and cost it outside the application. A box titled **USS → MCR connection**
says so when the last plot is done. See
[Multi-plot sites](/docs/layout/multi-plot) for the full behaviour, including
how each plot appears in the Summary view.

## Moving a building

Buildings move in [Sketch Mode](/docs/editing/sketch-mode) only. In the Layout view a drag
on a building pans the view. With **Sketch** on, press on the main control room, a unit
substation or an object and drag it to its new place. It is ignored while cables are being
routed.

With **Calculate cables** on, moving the main control room or a unit substation can raise
the **Re-route cables** box if the move would restore automatic trenches you deleted on
that plot.

## Street lights

Street lights are perimeter lighting: a line of poles set close inside the
fence, right around the plant.

They are **off** by default and, unlike the main control room, they are **not**
placed by clicking. Tick **Place street lights** on the **Structures & Shadow**
card of the Site tab **before** you click **Generate Layout**; a layout already
on screen does not gain them.

### What placement does

- Poles are spaced along the plant perimeter at the pole-to-pole span.
- Each pole sits inside the fence, inset by the street light setback.
- Any module table the pole's shadow touches is cleared, so lighting costs a
  little capacity around the edge of the plant.

The number of poles is approximately the **perimeter divided by the span**, so a
long, irregular boundary carries more lights than a compact one of the same
area.

### The street light settings

All of them are under the *— Street Light —* divider of the
**Structures & Shadow** card:

| Field | Default | Range | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pile Ø | 0.30 | 0.0–5.0 | m |
| Height | 6.0 | 0.0–50.0 | m |
| Span | 40.0 | 1.0–500.0 | m |
| Setback | 0.20 | 0.0–50.0 | m |
| **Place street lights** | off | — | — |

What each one changes:

- **Span** sets the pole count. Widen it for fewer poles and less cleared
  ground; narrow it for more of both.
- **Setback** decides how far inside the fence the poles stand. It is small by
  default, which keeps the poles in the perimeter road band rather than out
  among the tables.
- **Height** sets how far the pole's shadow reaches, and therefore how many
  tables it clears.
- **Pile Ø** describes the foundation.

All of them are saved with the project.

### Reading the result

The Summary view reports the pole count in the **Street lights** row, one
column per plant with a Total column on a multi-plot site, once the design has some. The
module and capacity rows already account for the tables the lights cleared.

## If a placement does not do what you expect

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="The buttons on the Tools tab are greyed out">
No layout exists yet. Load a boundary file and click **Generate Layout**; on this card only **Place
Object** works before that.

If a layout exists and the whole card is disabled, cables are being routed
after an edit — the status bar says so. The card comes back when they finish.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Clicking the plot places nothing">
The tool is not armed. While placement is armed the bar is over the plot and the button
reads **Cancel Placement**; if it reads **Place MCR** or **Place Object**, a click on the
plot is an ordinary click. Arm it first. Leaving the Layout view, Generate, Open and Sketch
all stop a placement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="This spot is outside the boundary. Move inside the plot.">
The point you clicked is outside the plant boundary. Nothing is placed and the
tool stays armed — click again inside the boundary.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An object was placed but tables stayed under its shadow">
A height of 0 casts no shadow, and **Clear tables in shadows** must be on for the shadow to
clear tables. Check both.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Street lights did not appear">
They are not placed by clicking. Tick **Place street lights** on the Site tab
and click **Generate Layout** again — an existing layout does not gain them,
and is [out of date](/docs/workspace/out-of-date-results) until you do.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The medium-voltage rows have no figure">
Three things are needed together: the main control room must be placed,
**Calculate cables** must be ticked, and the layout must have been generated
**after** the room was placed. Cable calculation is off by default, and placing
the room routes nothing on its own. The row's own words say which one is missing: *needs an
MCR on the layout* or *needs Calculate cables*.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Where the structures end up in the outputs

- **On the plot.** The main control room, the unit substations, objects and
  street light poles are drawn with the rest of the layout; the main control
  room is violet. **Shadows (keep-clear)** in the Layers popover shows the ground kept clear
  for their shadows.
- **In the drawing set and its DXF twin.** Buildings are drawn with their IDs. See
  [The drawing set](/docs/exports/drawing-set).
- **In the KMZ.** The main control room and unit substations are written as placemarks.
  Street lights are not written. See [Schedules and KMZ](/docs/exports/schedules-and-kmz).
- **In the bill of materials.** The list is computed from the layout, and recounts the next
  time it is opened after you place or remove equipment. See
  [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview).
