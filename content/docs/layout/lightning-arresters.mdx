title: Lightning arresters
description: How arresters are placed on a coverage grid, the ground each mast keeps clear, the settings behind them, and where they show up.
---

An LA — lightning arrester — is a mast that protects a circular area of ground
around it against a direct strike. When you ask for them, the application places
masts across the plant on a grid sized so that every table and building falls
inside at least one protected circle, then removes the tables and trackers the
mast's pile or its shadow touches.

Arresters are **off** by default. Nothing about the layout changes until you tick
them on, and turning them on reduces capacity on a fixed-tilt plant and on a
tracker plant alike, which is why the decision belongs to you rather than to a
default. The rules are the same for both mountings.

## Turning arresters on

Both settings live on the **Site Parameters** card of the **Site** tab. They are
read when you click **Generate Layout**; arresters are placed late in the run,
after the module tables and the inverter control rooms are down and before the
inverters and cables.

| Field | Default | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Place Lightning Arresters** | off | — | Turns arrester placement on for the run. The radius field below stays greyed out until this is ticked. |
| **LA protection radius** | 100.0 | 10.0–500.0 | The radius, in metres, of the circle a single arrester protects. The grid the masts start from is spaced at 1.4 times this. |

<Screenshot id="site-parameters-la-on" />

See [Site parameters](/docs/inputs/site) for the rest of that card. Both the tick
and the radius are kept in a project file.

## What happens when arresters are off

No arresters are placed, no ground is reserved for them, and module tables fill
the whole usable area — the plant boundary shrunk inward by the perimeter road
width, with obstructions and terrain exclusions subtracted. The Summary view has
no **Lightning arresters** row and the **Lightning arresters** chip does not
appear under the view.

The design check **C-02** *Lightning protection designed* fails until arresters
are placed. See [Design checks](/docs/reference/design-checks).

## How the masts are placed

Four steps, in this order, on every run with the option on.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Lay the grid

Grid positions are laid out on a square grid centred on the usable area, with a
spacing of **1.4 times the protection radius**: 140 m at the default 100 m.

The worst-served point in a grid square is its centre. At this spacing that point
is the spacing ÷ √2 from the masts around it, or **0.99 of the radius**, so the
circles just cover the square with a little to spare.
</Step>

<Step>
### Move each position into a gap

A mast stands in the ground between structures, not on one. On a fixed-tilt plant
each position moves north or south to the nearest gap between rows. On a tracker
plant it moves east or west to the nearest gap between columns.

The moved position is kept only if it lies **inside the usable area**, so a mast
is never placed on the perimeter road, in a water body, inside a line corridor or
on excluded ground. Two positions that land on the same spot count once. The
result is regular across the middle of the plant and ragged wherever the boundary
or an obstruction cuts into it.
</Step>

<Step>
### Complete the coverage

Dropping positions at the edges would leave outlying equipment unprotected, so a
second pass measures every table, ICR, main control room and unit substation
against the masts already placed. Anything whose centre is **farther than the
radius from every mast** gets one more, in the nearest gap.

On a long, thin or awkwardly notched site this pass adds the masts the regular
grid could not reach, and those are the ones you will see off the grid lines.
</Step>

<Step>
### Clear the keep-clear

Every table or tracker unit touched by a mast's pile, or by its shadow, is removed.
The next section covers what that means.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="arrester-coverage" />

## The keep-clear, and what it costs

The circle is the protection. The keep-clear is the ground the mast itself takes
from the plant, and it has two parts:

- **The pile.** A square of the **LA** pile Ø, and never less than 0.2 m.
- **The shadow.** The mast's shadow swept over the **Shadow Window** on every day
  of the year. It reaches further the taller the mast and the wider the window.

A table or a tracker unit touched by either is removed whole. The plant's module
count and DC capacity are recomputed from what survives, and the land account
books the ground as arrester keep-clear. Without a known latitude, or with the
height at 0, only the pile clears.

<Screenshot id="arrester-keep-clear" />

To see the figure for your own site, generate once without arresters and once with
them and compare the **DC capacity (MWp)** row of the Summary view. On the bundled
sample site at the defaults, a fixed-tilt plant of 54.28 MWp and 1,589 tables takes
26 masts, 17 from the grid and 9 from the coverage pass, and they remove 89 tables,
or 3.04 MWp. The **Lightning arresters** row of the [Workings](/docs/workspace/workings)
view gives the count and capacity removed on your own plant.

On a tracker plant the masts stand in the gaps between columns, and the same rule
removes any unit a pile or shadow touches.

Arresters clear tables, so a capacity comparison between two designs is not
like-for-like unless arresters are set the same way in both runs.
</Callout>

## The settings behind the keep-clear

Two further arrester fields sit on the **Structures & Shadow (keep-clear)** card of
the Site tab, alongside the building footprints:

| Field | Default | Range | Unit | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LA** height | 9.0 | 0.0–100.0 | m | Sets the mast's shadow, and prints in the arrester's line of the bill of materials. At 0 the mast casts no shadow |
| **LA** pile Ø | 0.30 | 0.0–5.0 | m | Sets the mast's footprint on the ground |

The **Shadow Window** on the same card, 9.0 to 15.0 solar hours by default, sets the
span of each day the shadow is swept over. Changing any of these fields
[marks the layout out of date](/docs/workspace/out-of-date-results) until you
generate again. See [Structures and shadow](/docs/inputs/structures-and-shadow).

## Raising or lowering the protection radius

The radius sizes the protected circle and spaces the grid, so changing it changes
the mast count more than you might expect.

| Change | Masts placed | Tables removed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Raise** the radius | Fewer | Fewer |
| **Lower** the radius | More | More |

Two things to hold in mind:

- The grid spacing follows the radius in both directions, so the number of positions
  changes with the *area* of a grid square, not with its side. Doubling the radius
  gives about a quarter of the grid positions.
- The radius is a claim about the mast you will build. Raising it to thin out the
  grid only reduces cost if the installed arrester genuinely protects that far. The
  application takes the number you give it and does not check it against a
  protection standard.

## Reading arresters on the plot and in the outputs

- **On the plot.** The masts and their *LA-n* labels draw with the **Plant layout**
  row of the **Layers ▾** popover. **Arrester coverage**, off by default, adds the
  protection circles in orange-red. **Shadows (keep-clear)**, off by default, shows
  the ground kept clear. See [Views](/docs/workspace/views) for the popover.
- **In the Summary view.** The **Lightning arresters** row is in the Electrical group
  when the design has arresters, with a Total column on a multi-plot site, and the
  **Lightning arresters** chip under every view appears once some are placed. Because
  arresters are placed after the tables, the module and capacity rows already account
  for the tables they removed.
- **In the design checks.** **C-02** *Lightning protection designed* reads whether
  they are placed, and **E-09** *Tables inside arrester cover* counts any table left
  outside a circle against a limit of 0 tables, which you can change in
  [Advanced settings](/docs/workspace/advanced-settings).
- **In the bill of materials.** The arrester's line carries the mast height. See
  [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview).
- **In the KMZ.** Arresters are labelled points in a folder of their own, with the
  protection radii hidden. See
  [Schedules and KMZ](/docs/exports/schedules-and-kmz).

## Arresters and cleaning robots

A mast's keep-clear interrupts the run of tables a cleaning robot drives along. The
robot sizing tool lists every gap too wide for a standard bridge between tables, and
separates the gaps at equipment from the others, so you can decide which to bridge.
See [Robotic module cleaning](/docs/simulation/robotic-cleaning).

## Earthing the arresters

The earth pits under each arrester are sized in **Earthing Design** (on the **Tools**
menu and in the **Studies** card), not here. It gives every arrester a ring of at
least **Pits per LA** earth pits, and adds pits until the ring meets the **LA earth
pit** target, 10 Ω by default, following IEC 62305-3 (type A arrangement). Its bill of
quantities carries a test joint for each arrester. See
[Earthing design](/docs/simulation/earthing).

## An arrester placed by hand

Sketch Mode's **➕ LA** tool, in the **PLANT OBJECTS** group, stands a mast at the point
you click, not moved to a gap, and removes the tables under its pile and shadow. See
[Sketch Mode](/docs/editing/sketch-mode).

The plant's arrester design comes from **Place Lightning Arresters** and **Generate
Layout**: the grid, the coverage pass and the checks above all work from it. To change
how many masts the plant carries and where they fall, change the radius and generate
again.
