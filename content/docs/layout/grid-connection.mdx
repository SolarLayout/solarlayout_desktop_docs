---
title: Grid connection by others
description: Designing a plant with no main control room or substation of its own, as in a solar park, with or without a handover point for the medium-voltage cable.
---

A plant in a solar park usually has no main control room (MCR) or pooling substation
of its own: the park provides them. Choose **By others** under **Grid connection** and
SolarLayout Desktop designs the plant up to where your scope ends, and every output says
the rest belongs to someone else.

The choice is on the **Grid connection & Objects** card of the **Tools** tab, once a
layout is on screen. **On this site** is the default: the plant has its own main control
room, as described in [Site equipment](/docs/layout/site-equipment).

<Screenshot id="grid-connection-by-others" />

## Decide where your scope ends

The question to settle is who runs the medium-voltage (MV) cable from the inverter
control rooms (ICRs) to the park's substation.

| Your scope | What to do | What the design holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You run the MV cable to the park's terminal | Choose **By others** and place a **handover point** | MV cable, fibre, termination kits and joints, routed to the point |
| The park runs it from your ICRs | Choose **By others** and place nothing | Everything up to each ICR's outgoing breaker panel |

## Place a handover point

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click Place handover point

The plot shows *"Click the plot, its fence or just outside it to place the handover
point."*
</Step>

<Step>
### Click where the cable hands over

The point can stand inside the plot, on its fence, or outside the fence up to the
**Handover point reach**: 200 m unless you change it under **File ▸ Advanced settings**,
in the **Layout** group. Further out, the plot says how far is allowed and nothing is
placed.
</Step>

<Step>
### Read the result

With **Calculate cables** on, the cables route straight away and the plot reports
*"MV cable and fibre routed to it: n m."* With it off, the plot tells you to tick
**Calculate cables** and generate again.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="grid-connection-point-placed" />

A handover point is a position, not a building. It removes no tables, casts no shadow
and takes no earth pits, and the DC and AC cables stay as they are. It is named **HP-1**
on the plan, in the drawings and in the schedules. To move it, turn on **Sketch** and drag
it; **Remove point** takes it away.

<Screenshot id="handover-point-mv-cables" />

On a site with several plots, each plot can have its own point, numbered in the order you
place them, and no unit substations are placed. A plot with no point stops at its ICRs'
breaker panels, so one project can hold both scopes.

## What changes in the bill of materials

| Line | On this site | By others, handover point | By others, no point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MCR building, furniture and air conditioning, water tank | Yes | No | No |
| Power transformer, its body earth pits and its nitrogen fire protection | Yes | No | No |
| HT panel, HT feeder panel, 50 kVA auxiliary transformer, the MCR's earth pits | Yes | No | No |
| MV cable, joint kits, optical fibre and its conduit, MV trench earthing strip | Yes | Yes | No |
| MV termination kit at the ICR | Yes | Yes | No |
| MV termination kit at the far end | Indoor, at the MCR | Outdoor by default, at the handover point | No |
| Breaker panel at each ICR, IDTs, ICR buildings | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Weather station and its RS485 cable | At the MCR | On site, at the first ICR | On site, at the first ICR |

Lines you enter by hand stay in the list whichever you choose, because the application
can't tell who provides them: the metering yard, modem, CCTV, CAT-6 cable, borewell and RO
plant, security cabin and main gate. Switch off any the park covers.

## What the outputs say

- **Design report:** with a point, *"The MV collector runs to HP-1. The pooling substation
  and the main control room are by others."* With no point, the MV cable from each ICR's
  outgoing breaker is listed as by others too.
- **Single-line diagram:** with a point, the feeders run to it. With no point, each ICR's
  panel ends with *"MV and grid: by others"*.
- **Schedules workbook:** the **Feeds** column of the **Blocks** sheet names the point or
  reads *"By others"*.
- **KMZ and DXF:** the point is drawn and named; with no point there is nothing to draw.
- **Design checks:** *MV collection designed* passes with a point, and does not apply with
  no point.
- **Summary and Workings:** the power transformer reads *by others*, and so does the MV
  cable when there is no point.

## Changing your mind

Choosing **By others** on a plant that holds a main control room or unit substations removes
them at once, and the tables they had cleared return to the layout. The DC and AC cables
were routed around the old layout, so generate again to route them. Choosing **On this site**
removes any handover points and leaves the other cables as they are. Neither change brings
back what the other choice removed: place it again.

Saved projects keep the choice. A project saved before the choice existed opens as
**On this site**.

If the card reads **On this site** and nothing is placed, the outputs treat the main control
room as missing and say so, because it may simply not have been placed yet. Choose
**By others** when it is genuinely someone else's.
