---
title: ICR blocks
description: What an ICR block is, how to see the blocks on the plot, and where each block's figures are.
---

An **ICR block** is one inverter control room (ICR) and the tables wired to it.
A table belongs to the block of the control room that its string inverter — or
its string monitoring box (SMB), in a central-inverter design — feeds. The
block is therefore an electrical grouping, and it is the unit a block
transformer, a cable schedule and a construction package are usually organised
around.

Every output that talks about blocks uses that definition: the **ICR blocks**
layer, the Summary view's **By block** sheet, the block sheets of the drawing set
and the KMZ. The blocks are known once the inverters are placed, which happens on
every **Generate Layout**; before that a block has no tables.

## Blocks and the placement groups

The control rooms are placed first, each at the centre of a group of tables of
near-equal capacity. See [Inverter control rooms](/docs/layout/icr). The
inverters or SMBs are then assigned to the rooms, each starting on the room
nearest to it along the plant and moving to a neighbouring room only when that
room is full. So a block — the tables wired to a room — is close to the group
the room was placed for, but not identical to it: along the edge between two
groups a few tables can end up wired to the neighbouring room. How devices are
assigned is on [Cable routing](/docs/layout/cables).

## Seeing the blocks

Open **Layers ▾** in the Layout view's tools and switch **ICR blocks** on. Each
block is filled in its own colour and outlined, and labelled with its name.
The row is off by default and greyed out until the inverters are placed; it
stays available while the layout is out of date. The layer only colours: a click
on a block does nothing.

<Screenshot id="icr-blocks" />

- **Names.** Blocks are named **ICR-1**, **ICR-2** and so on, after the number of
  their control room in the plant.
- **Outlines.** A block's outline hugs its tables, closes the gaps inside the
  block, keeps off the other blocks and is clipped to the plant boundary. A
  block split by an obstruction or a water body can therefore have several
  pieces.
- **Colours.** The colours cycle through a fixed set, so distant blocks can share one.

The layer is the quickest way to see how the plant is wired: which tables feed
which building, and whether a block reaches across a corridor or a pond to
pick up tables the neighbouring room would serve more directly.

## Each block's figures

Open the **Summary** view on **By block**, where it opens by default. Each block has a
column, followed by **Shared** and **Total**. The rows give its DC capacity and share of
the plant, area, tables, modules and piles; its inverters and ACCBs, or SMBs and central
inverters; its AC capacity and DC / AC ratio; its transformers and their load; its
arresters; and its cable lengths. Clicking a block's head fits the Layout view to its
outline. Every row is on
[Summary view](/docs/reference/summary-columns#the-by-block-sheet).

<Screenshot id="summary-by-block" />

Energy is not split per block: yield is calculated for the whole plant, in the
[Energy view](/docs/energy/overview). The **Share of plant DC (%)** row is the figure
to scale by if you need a block's share.

On a site where more than one plot has control rooms, block names carry the plot's
number, *P1-ICR-1*, *P2-ICR-1* and so on, so that blocks from different plots stay
apart. See [Multi-plot sites](/docs/layout/multi-plot).

## Blocks in the deliverables

The package works block by block:

- **The drawing set** has block sheets at one scale, with match lines between tiles,
  then the pile layout and earthing sheets on the same tiles. See
  [The drawing set](/docs/exports/drawing-set).
- **The design report** has a block schedule. See
  [The design report](/docs/exports/design-report).
- **The KMZ** has one folder per block, with its outline, its tables, its devices and its
  ICR, and the figures in the outline's pop-up. See
  [Schedules and KMZ](/docs/exports/schedules-and-kmz).
- **The BOM** has a **By block** view. See [Bill of materials](/docs/bom/overview).
