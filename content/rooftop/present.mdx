**Present** shows the open design on the whole screen as a guided tour: the
3D roof of the Studio without the app's bars, played in six scenes from the
place on the map to the energy of the year, then again from the start. It is
made for a meeting with a client, on a tablet or a computer.

<Screenshot id="rooftop-present-day" />

## Start the tour

<Steps>
<Step>

### Let the design run

Open the design in the Studio and let it run. The tour needs the layout,
the sun of the day and a stringing option. Choose the option you want to
show in the Strings lens or in [Performance](/docs/rooftop/performance): the
tour shows the chosen one.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set the ground

The ground under the roof is the one the Studio's **Layers** shows. Turn on
**Satellite imagery** there to show the roof on its satellite image; leave
it off for the plain grid.

</Step>
<Step>

### Press Present

**Present**, with its play icon, stands in the top bar. The browser's full
screen is asked for where the browser allows it, and the tour begins.

</Step>
</Steps>

**Present** is offered when the top bar is at least 820 px wide: on a tablet
or a computer, not on a phone. On a tablet the button shows its play icon
alone; on a computer the word stands beside it.

## The six scenes

One loop lasts 40 seconds. A panel names each scene and counts its figures.

| Scene | What plays | Time | The panel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · The place | "The satellite from above tilts into 3D" | 4 s | **Roof area**, latitude and longitude |
| 2 · The roof | "The building and the obstacles rise" | 4 s | **Obstacles**, the roof's size or corners, the parapet |
| 3 · The array | "The modules fill row by row" | 6 s | **Positions**, the rows, the tilt |
| 4 · The sun | "The day plays with its shadows", with the sun's path | 12 s | **Solar time**, the sun's height and bearing, on the date of the day |
| 5 · The strings | "The strings light up by MPPT" | 4 s | **DC capacity**, the strings and modules in series, the inverters |
| 6 · The energy | "The energy counts up" | 10 s | **Energy to grid, year 1**, the specific yield, PR |

In scene 5 each string is coloured by its MPPT, the inverter input that
tracks its own best operating point; a string is a chain of modules wired in
series. PR, the performance ratio, is the share of the energy the modules
would give at their rating that reaches the grid.

The camera starts straight down on the roof, as on the map, and tilts into
the 3D view over the first scene; it then holds that view to the end of the
loop. The day of scene 4 is the day the Sun lens shows: the design day,
unless you chose another there. In scene 6 the energy counts up over six
seconds, then holds at the year's figure until the loop begins again.

## What stands on the screen

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| Bottom left | The design's name, its DC capacity and its energy of a year, for example "Pune demo roof · 357.28 kWp · 617.8 MWh a year", and under it the words of the scene |
| Bottom right | "Press any key or touch the screen to return", which fades after the first loop |
| The panel | The scene's number and name, and its figures counting up |

## End the tour

Press any key, click, or touch the screen. The tour stops, the full screen
is left, and the Studio opens at the lens you used last.

## When the tour waits

Until the design has run with an option, Present shows the design's name
and waits, saying why:

| It says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Updating energy** | The run is on its way; the tour begins when it lands. |
| The reason the run has no options, such as **No string length fits this inverter** or **Too few positions for a string** | Leave Present, act on the reason in the Studio (the [Strings lens](/docs/rooftop/strings) for the strings, the Roof lens for the roof), then present again. |

<Callout type="info">
Present works from the design kept on this device. Free, no account needed.
Your designs are kept on this device, so present from the device that holds
the design, or open its project file on the one you present from.
</Callout>

## Reduced motion

When the device asks for reduced motion, each scene is a still cut held for
its time, and the day of scene 4 passes in 12 cuts.

## When 3D cannot be drawn

On a browser that cannot draw in 3D, or that stops drawing in 3D, a flat
plan of the roof stands in for the scene, with "This browser cannot draw the
roof in 3D." or "The browser stopped drawing the roof in 3D."
[Getting started](/docs/rooftop/getting-started#what-the-browser-needs) says
what the browser needs.
