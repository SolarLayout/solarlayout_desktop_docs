---
title: What SolarLayout Rooftop does
description: Rooftop PV design in the browser, from the roof on the map to the layout, shading, stringing, the hourly energy yield and the handover files.
---

SolarLayout Rooftop designs a rooftop PV system in the browser, on a phone, a
tablet or a computer. You find the roof on the map and outline it, and the app
lays out the modules, works out the shading, sizes the strings and simulates
the energy of every hour of a year, on one live model of the roof in 3D.
Free, no account needed. Your designs are kept on this device.

## From a roof to a design

| Step | Where | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find the roof | [**Find**](/docs/rooftop/find) | Search an address, coordinates or a maps link, then outline the roof on the satellite image: by its corners, as a rectangle, from a detected footprint, or by typing its size. Add obstacles with their heights. A KMZ, KML or DXF drawing can be imported instead. |
| Shape the roof | **Studio**, **Roof** lens | The roof type (flat with tilted rows, flat east-west, or sloped with the modules flush), the edges and the access around them. |
| Lay out the array | **Studio**, **Array** lens | The module, from the app's library of PAN files or your own; the mounting; the spacing between rows. |
| Read the sun | **Studio**, **Sun** lens | The sun's path, the shadows at any hour, the shade map and the shading over the year. |
| Size the strings | **Studio**, **Strings** lens | The system voltage, the inverter, from a library of OND files or your own, the modules in series, and the stringing options, each simulated for a year. |
| Read the energy | **Performance** | The hourly simulation on PVGIS or NASA POWER weather, or your own CSV: the losses from sunlight to the grid, P50, P75 and P90, and 25 years. |
| Hand it over | **Handover** | A design report (PDF), a single-line diagram, the bill of materials, the DC cable schedule, the CAD layout (DXF), a Google Earth model (KMZ), the hourly data (CSV) and the project file, one at a time or all together. |
| Show it | **Present** | A guided full-screen tour of the design for a meeting with a client. |

A change to the roof, the equipment or the settings runs the design again: the
layout, the shading, the options and the energy follow the roof as you change it.

## What you need

A browser that can draw in 3D. A design starts from a place on the map, and
the app's equipment libraries and the demo let you begin without files of
your own.
