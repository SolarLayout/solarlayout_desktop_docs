Help comes from three places, in this order: the help inside the app, these
pages, and SolarLayout. The first two are immediate, and the third goes
faster when you arrive with the right details.

## The help inside the app

An orange **?** beside a section heading explains that section: hover over
it for a summary, press it to open the topic. On Find, the tools have
how-tos with pictures and short clips, and a tip's **Show me how** opens the
how-to of the move it suggests. The open topic is part of the page's
address, so you can send a colleague a link to it.

## Contacting SolarLayout

Write to SolarLayout through [solarlayout.app/contact](https://solarlayout.app/contact).
A report is acted on fastest with:

- what you did, and what you expected;
- the message the app showed, word for word;
- the page's address from the browser's address bar;
- the browser and the device, phone, tablet or computer;
- the design's project file: **Save a copy** in the project switcher.

<Callout type="info">
Your designs are kept on this device, so SolarLayout cannot see them. The
project file is how a design reaches anyone else, SolarLayout included.
</Callout>
