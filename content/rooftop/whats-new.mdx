SolarLayout Rooftop updates in the browser: the next time you open it, you
have the latest. Each update that changes what you meet has an entry here,
newest first.

## 10 October 2026

- **Each string's DC cable is sized.** The DC cable schedule gives each
  string the smallest of 4, 6 and 10 mm² copper that carries 1.25 times the
  module's Isc and drops no more than 1 % of its voltage, with a **Drop (%)**
  column. The bill of materials gives the metres of each size.
- **Far shading takes the sky the horizon hides.** The diffuse light the
  distant horizon hides now counts as far shading, not near shading, in the
  Sun lens and in Performance. The year's energy is the same.
- **The inverter in the CAD layout at its size.** The DXF draws each inverter
  at the width and depth its OND file gives; where the file gives none, a 2 ×
  1.2 m block named a symbol, not to scale.
- **Add the obstacle.** In the Roof lens a new obstacle joins the design by
  **Add the …** or Enter, once its height is typed; leaving the height field
  does not add it.
- **One button for a detected footprint.** On Find, the callout of a
  detected building has **Use this outline**; its corners drag as any
  outline's.

## 9 October 2026

- **Where your designs are kept, in plain words.** Home's **On this device**
  says: "Your designs are kept on this device."
- **Sloped from Find.** A roof built on Find as **Sloped, flush** gets the
  roof Sloped gives in the Roof lens: no parapet, and a pitch of 10° where it
  had none.
- **PVGIS weather without its horizon says so.** When PVGIS sends a site's
  weather without its horizon, the Sun lens reads "Far shading: not counted,
  PVGIS sent no horizon for this site", and Performance notes the same. The
  next time the weather loads, PVGIS is asked again.
- **The east-west ridge line.** On an east-west roof, the Array lens says
  "The ridges run square to the roof edge closest to the equator".
- **Your own option on a small roof.** When a roof holds too few positions
  for the run's options but one string fits, **Add your own** stands under
  the message in the Strings lens, set to that string. **Add** builds it and
  chooses it.
- **A weather CSV is a whole year.** A file of other than 8,760 hours (8,784
  in a leap year) is refused: the energy of a year needs every hour of it. A
  blank or unreadable global irradiance cell is refused with its row, and so
  is one of beam or diffuse when the file gives both. Irradiance below zero, taken as 0, and gaps in the air
  temperature, filled from the hours on either side, are noted.
- **Sloped roofs face their slope.** Modules on a sloped, flush roof face its
  **Faces**, whatever azimuth the array kept from a flat roof.
- **A row pitch under a table's depth says so.** One entered under a table's
  depth on plan plus 0.3 m is raised to it, with a note to review.
- **The Voc coefficient that sizes the strings.** Under **Sizing
  temperatures**, the field gives the one-diode model's coefficient the strings
  are sized with, unless you enter one.
- **A module file without a voltage rating.** It is taken as 1000 V, and the
  Strings lens says the limit was taken, not given by the module.
