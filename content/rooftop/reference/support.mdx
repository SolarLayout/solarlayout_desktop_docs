Help for SolarLayout Rooftop comes from three places. The help inside the app
answers "what do I put here?" beside the control. These pages go deeper: the
walkthroughs, how the app works things out, and what to do when something goes
wrong. SolarLayout answers what neither does.

## The help in the app

The help is on Find and in the Studio: on the Roof, Array and Strings steps,
and in the Sun view. It covers Find's tools and sections, and the sections of
the work column that carry a **?**.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Find the ? disc

A quiet **?** stands beside a section's name, and beside Find's tools and
sections. On a computer, rest the pointer on it, or move the keyboard's focus
to it: its tooltip gives the topic's summary in one sentence, then "Click for
more".
</Step>
<Step>
### Open the topic

Press the **?**. The help sheet opens: from the right on a computer or a
tablet, from the bottom on a phone. Its head says where you are, such as
"Section · Roof step" or "Tool · Find".
</Step>
<Step>
### Read it, then follow it further

Most section topics say what to set, what it changes and when to change it.
A tool's topic gives numbered steps, most with pictures and some with a short
clip.
Under **Related**, open a related topic, or the row "&lt;section&gt;, in the
docs" to open these pages in a new tab.
</Step>
</Steps>

Close the sheet with Escape, its ×, or a press beside it. **Back** returns from
a topic you reached by a link.

The open topic is part of the page's address. To show a colleague the same
topic, copy the address from the browser's address bar and send it.

The app explains its own words where you meet them. On a computer, each
step's name has a one-line tooltip, and the first time you meet a step or the
Sun view, a line under its title says what it is. See
[Help in the app](/docs/rooftop/start/steps#help-in-the-app).

Where something fails, the app says what happened and what to do, with a button
for the remedy, such as **Try again**. Some problems add **Details**: open it
for the words to quote in a report.

## Contacting SolarLayout

Write to SolarLayout at
[sales@solarlayout.app](mailto:sales@solarlayout.app) with a question, a
report of something that went wrong, or a request.

Free, no account needed. Your designs are kept on this device. A design on one
device is not on another: the project file carries it across. To show
SolarLayout a design, send its project file.

A report is acted on fastest when it has:

- what you did, step by step, and what you expected;
- the message the app showed, word for word, and what **Details** says, if the
  message has it;
- the page's address, from the browser's address bar;
- the browser, and the device: a phone, a tablet or a computer;
- the design's project file.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Download the project file

Press the design's name in the top bar, then **Download the project file**. On
Home, a card's **⋯** menu has the same item.
</Step>
<Step>
### Attach it to your mail

The file ends in `.rtd` and is named after the design, such as
`Pune-demo-roof.rtd`. It holds the whole design, your own module, inverter and
weather files included, so SolarLayout can open it and see what you saw.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
A project file holds the design's place: the roof's outline with its latitude
and longitude. Send it only to those who may know where the roof is.
</Callout>

If the problem is with a file of Handover, say which file and attach it beside
the project file. Your designs and the project file lists what the project
file holds.
