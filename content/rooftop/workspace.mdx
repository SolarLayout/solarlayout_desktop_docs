SolarLayout Rooftop has six areas, each with its own address, so a link or a
bookmark opens the same place again. Studio, Performance and Handover are the
three workspaces: they share one design and one top bar.

| Area | Address | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | `/` | The search that starts a design, **Import a drawing**, **Open a project**, the demo, and the designs kept on this device |
| **Find** | `/find` | The roof on the map: its place, its outline, its obstacles |
| **Studio** | `/studio/roof`, `/studio/array`, `/studio/sun`, `/studio/strings` | The design on a live 3D roof, in four lenses: **Roof**, **Array**, **Sun**, **Strings** |
| **Performance** | `/performance` | The energy of the year and its losses |
| **Handover** | `/handover` | The report, the drawings, the tables and the project file |
| **Present** | `/present` | The guided tour of the design on the whole screen |

## Moving between areas

| To | How |
| --- | --- |
| A workspace | On a tablet or a computer, the **Studio**, **Performance** and **Handover** switch in the top bar. On a phone, the tab bar at the bottom. The Studio opens at the lens you used last. |
| Home | The logo at the top left, on every size, or **Home** in the project switcher. |
| Handover | **Export** in the top bar. |
| Present | **Present** in the top bar, on a tablet or a computer. It is not offered on a phone. |
| Another design | The design's name in the top bar opens the project switcher: **New design**, **Open a project**, **Import a drawing**, **Save a copy** and **Home**. |

Home and Find stand outside the workspaces: they have no top bar and no tab
bar.

## The Studio on each size

| Size | The lenses | The settings of a lens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Computer | A rail of icons with labels at the left | A panel at the right |
| Tablet | A row of buttons floating at the top left of the 3D view | Under the 3D view and the figures |
| Phone | A four-way switch at the top of the bottom sheet | In the bottom sheet, which opens at half height |

<Screenshot id="rooftop-studio-phone" />

At the top right of the 3D view, **3D** and **Plan** switch between a
perspective from the south-west and a view straight down with north up.
**Layers** has a switch for the satellite image under the roof, off at
first. On a tablet or a computer, **Fit** frames the whole roof again and the
north control turns the view north up.

## Help where you work

An orange **?** beside a section heading explains that section. Pressing it
opens the help beside the page on a tablet or a computer, or from the bottom
on a phone. The open topic is part of the page's address, so a link to it
opens the same topic.

## Saving

There is nothing to save by hand. Every change is kept on this device a
moment after you make it. A mark before the design's name in the top bar
shows the state; point at the name, or open the project switcher, to read it
in words: **Saved on this device**.
[Your designs and the project file](/docs/rooftop/projects) says what that
means and how a design moves to another device.
