---
title: Roof
description: Check the roof built on Find, then set its type, its edges and access, its obstacles and its outline on the Roof step.
---

Roof is the first of the five steps in SolarLayout Rooftop. Its first part is
[Find](/docs/rooftop/roof/find), where you outline the roof on the map. After
**Build this roof** the app opens the Roof step with the array already laid on
the roof, and you check what was built: the parapet, the strips kept free of
modules, what stands on the roof and where its corners are.

The step's work column holds four sections, top to bottom: **Roof type**,
**Edges and access**, **Obstacles** and **Outline**. Each is a summary row
that opens; none is open the first time. [The work
column](/docs/rooftop/start/steps#the-work-column) explains rows, fields and the
primary button. A change to any value on this step lays the roof out again,
then runs the design. The roof albedo is the exception: it is set in
Performance and runs the design alone.

## Check the roof

The step's head says **Check the roof**, and under it:

> Built from your outline. The app set the edges: check the parapet, it shades
> the first row.

That is a roof outlined on the map. A roof read from a KMZ or DXF drawing
says "Built from your file", and a roof entered by hand, such as the demo,
"Built from your corners".

On the design's first open, while no other step of it has been opened on this
device, a status of two lines stands under the head:

| Line | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A tick, **The array is on the roof**, then `<n> modules · <kWp> kWp` once the run has an option (`<n> positions` before that) |
| 2, while the run works | A ring, **Computing the energy**, "Shading the modules over the year. A few seconds." |
| 2, when it is done | A tick, `<MWh> MWh in year 1`, "The energy follows each change you make." |

Energy is in MWh to one decimal, DC capacity in kWp to two decimals. On the
demo the status reads 638 modules, 357.28 kWp and 617.8 MWh in year 1. When
the run fails or finds no option, line 1 stands alone, and [the
result](/docs/rooftop/start/steps#the-result) in the top bar says what went wrong.

Work through the roof before going on:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Measure the parapet

Open **Edges and access** and type the **Parapet height** measured on site.
Its shadow keeps part of the roof free of modules and costs energy over the
year. If the roof has no parapet, type 0.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the roof type

Open **Roof type**. Find asked for it; change it here if the roof is pitched,
or to compare east-west units with tilted rows.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the obstacles and their heights

Open **Obstacles**. Each obstacle needs its height above the roof surface,
measured on site: the imagery cannot give one. Add any not drawn on Find.
</Step>
<Step>
### Compare the edges with the site

The stage labels each roof edge with its length. Where a corner is off, edit
it in Plan from **Outline**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Go on to Array

Press **Continue to Array** at the column's foot. Until Array has been opened
once for this design, the line above it reads "Next: Array, the modules on the
roof, how they are mounted and spaced." (not on a phone).
</Step>
</Steps>

### The roof on the stage

On a computer the stage's title is **Roof**, and the line under it gives the
roof's area, the area inside the edge margin and the obstacles, as
`<area> m² · <area> m² inside the edge margin · <n> obstacles`. On the demo:
`3,600 m² · 3,344 m² inside the edge margin · 6 obstacles`.

On the Roof step each edge carries its length, `<n.n> m`, at its middle. Of two
parallel edges of the same length, only one is labelled (the one further
south and west). The kept-clear areas, where a shadow keeps modules out, are
drawn on each step outside the Sun view.

### The view controls

The view controls stand at the stage's top right.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **3D** / **Plan** | 3D looks at the roof in perspective from the south-west, above it. Plan looks straight down, north up; it pans and zooms but does not turn. Plan is where the outline's corners are edited |
| **Fit** | Frames the roof again, as the 3D view from the south-west. Pressed in Plan, it leaves Plan |
| North control | Its needle turns with the view. Press it to turn the view north-up, at the same distance and height |
| **Sun** | Turns on the Sun view on the step in hand: shadows at any hour, and the shade on each module over the year. See [The Sun view](/docs/rooftop/steps/sun) |
| **Imagery** | Shows the satellite imagery as the ground; press again for the plain grid. Each load starts on the grid, and the choice is not part of the design. The imagery's credit stands at the stage's bottom left while it is drawn |
| **Show panel** | On a computer, while the work column is hidden. **Hide panel** at the column's top right, or `⌘ \` or `Ctrl \`, hides it; the step's primary then floats at the stage's foot |

On a phone the controls are 3D, Plan, Sun and Imagery; Fit and the north
control are on a tablet or a computer. On touch, in 3D one finger orbits and
two pan and pinch; in Plan one finger pans. An edit never moves the camera.

If the browser cannot draw 3D, a flat plan, north up, stands in with "This
browser cannot draw the roof in 3D.", and the view controls are not shown.

### Selecting on the roof

Press the roof, an obstacle or a kept-clear area to select it; a callout names
it. A press is a pointer that moves 4 px or less, so an orbit that ends on
something selects nothing. A press on the ground clears the selection, and so
does Escape (outside a field or a dialog). On a computer, selecting something
shows a hidden work column again.

| Selected | Callout |
| --- | --- |
| The roof | **Roof · selected**, then `<a> × <b> m · <area> m²` for a rectangle, `<n> corners · <area> m²` otherwise |
| An obstacle | **&lt;name&gt; · selected**, its size and height, and "Drag to move it." (box, tank, tree) or "Move it by its points in the list." (wall, building) |
| A kept-clear area | **Kept clear · &lt;n&gt; positions**, then "&lt;cause&gt; shades this area between &lt;start&gt; and &lt;end&gt;", the shade-free hours. See [How the layout is placed](/docs/rooftop/steps/array#how-the-layout-is-placed) |

## Outline

**Outline** is the last section. Closed, it reads the roof's latitude and
longitude to two decimals, its area and **Edit it in Plan**. On the demo:
`18.52° N, 73.86° E · 3,600 m² · Edit it in Plan`. It carries no who-set-it
word.

Open, its card says:

| Line | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Place | Latitude and longitude to five decimals, with N, S, E or W |
| How it was made | `Outlined on satellite`, `By hand` or `From a file` |
| Corners and obstacles | `<n> corners · <n> obstacles` (no obstacle part when there are none) |
| Size and facing | A rectangle (4 corners, right angles within 1°) as `<long> × <short> m`, any other outline as `<area> m²`; then `long side faces <deg>° <compass>`, the outward side of the longest edge nearer the equator |
| File | The drawing's file name, for a roof read from a KMZ or DXF |

To move a corner:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Press Edit it in Plan

The stage turns to Plan and selects the roof, and each corner of the roof is a
handle. Pressing the roof in Plan on the Roof step selects it too.
</Step>
<Step>
### Drag the corner

While it moves, a label gives the lengths of the two edges it joins, as
`<a> m · <b> m`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Let go

The corner snaps to the nearest 0.1 m. The move is one edit: the app lays the
roof out again and runs the design. ⌘ Z or Ctrl Z undoes it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="rooftop-roof-plan" />

An outline that would cross itself, or close to under 1 m², is refused with
"The outline cannot cross itself. The roof stays as it was."

A roof covers at most 30,000 m², about 3 MWp. To design another roof, choose
**New design** in the project menu, which opens Find.

## Roof type

**Roof type** is the first section. Find asked for the type, so its word is
always **Yours**. Three buttons set it: **Tilted rows**, **East-west** and
**Sloped**. The section's closed row gives the long name.

| Button | Long name | What the app lays out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tilted rows** | Flat, rows facing south (north south of the equator) | Rows of tables at the array's **Tilt**, facing the array's direction. The rows are spaced to keep clear of each other's shade in the shade-free hours, unless a row spacing is typed. A walkway crosses the rows after a set number of modules |
| **East-west** | Flat, rows facing east and west · back to back, low tilt | Units of two sides back to back at the **East-west tilt**, one side turned east and the other west of the array's direction, one module a side. A gap between units only where the shade-free hours need one. A walkway after a set number of units |
| **Sloped** | Sloped · modules follow the roof pitch | Modules flush on the roof at its **Roof pitch**, facing its **Direction (azimuth)**, both set in Edges and access. Rows follow each other with the module gap alone, no gap for shade. The modules' lower edge is 0.1 m above the roof |

Choose **Sloped** for a pitched roof. The app then sets the parapet to 0, and
the roof pitch to 10° if it was 0: type the real pitch and direction. Choosing
**Tilted rows** or **East-west** changes the type alone. Choose **East-west**
to compare more modules at a low tilt with the energy of tilted rows.

<Screenshot id="rooftop-roof-sloped" />

<Screenshot id="rooftop-roof-east-west" />

The type also sets the defaults of two temperatures and the rear gain:

| Default | Tilted rows | East-west | Sloped |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Heat loss factor (Uc), in Performance | 29 | 20 | 15 |
| Cell rise for sizing the strings, °C | 25 | 25 | 35 |
| Rear gain of a bifacial module | Yes | Yes | None |

[Mounting](/docs/rooftop/steps/array#mounting) shows the fields of each type.

## Edges and access

**Edges and access** sets where modules may not go: the strip at the edges,
the walkways and the space around obstacles. Closed, it reads
`Parapet <h> m · edge margin <s> m`, or `No parapet · edge margin <s> m`. On
the demo: `Parapet 0.90 m · edge margin 1.00 m`, **Default**.

The fields stand in groups: **At the edges**, **Access**, **Around
obstacles**, and on a sloped roof **Sloped roof**.

| Field | Default | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Parapet height** (At the edges) | 0.90 m; 0 on a sloped roof. Hint "Default · measure it on site" | 0–5 m | A wall 0.2 m thick along the inside of each edge. It casts shadows like an obstacle: the area they cross in the shade-free hours is kept clear, and its shade counts in the energy. 0: no parapet |
| **Edge margin (setback)** (At the edges) | 1.00 m. Hint "Default · usually 0.5 to 1.5 m" | 0–10 m | The roof is shrunk inward by it, sharp corners kept. If that splits the roof, the largest piece is used. The fire code may set it |
| **Walkway width** (Access) | 0.60 m. Hint "Default · room to clean and repair the modules" | 0–3 m | The width of the path |
| **Walkway every** (Access), Tilted rows | 24 modules. Hint "Default · counted in modules along a row" | 0–500 modules | After that many modules along each row, an aisle of the walkway's width crosses the rows. 0: none |
| **Walkway every** (Access), East-west and Sloped | 4 rows. Hint "Default · counted in rows" | 0–500 rows | After that many rows (sloped) or units (east-west), the walkway's width is added. 0: none |
| **Clearance** (Around obstacles) | 0.50 m. Hint "Default · kept free all round" | 0–5 m | Each obstacle's footprint grows by it, rounded at the corners. No module stands in it |
| **Roof pitch** (Sloped roof) | 10° | 0–60°, whole degrees | The slope of the roof, and the modules' tilt |
| **Direction (azimuth)** (Sloped roof) | 180° | 0–359.9° | The way the slope faces, clockwise from north. The modules face it |

Metres take two decimals. Under the fields, a line reads
`Roof albedo 0.20 · in Performance, Assumptions` with an arrow. Roof albedo is
how much light the roof reflects. The line opens Performance; set
the albedo under [Assumptions](/docs/rooftop/steps/performance#assumptions).

<Screenshot id="rooftop-roof-edges" />

The hint under each field says who set it. Change a value and the hint reads
`Yours · default <value> <unit>` with **Reset**, which gives the app's value
back as one edit; the section's word turns to **Yours**.

<Screenshot id="rooftop-roof-yours" />

Change these when the site has no parapet, when its code asks for a wider
margin, or when the roof needs a walkway more often.

## Obstacles

An obstacle is anything on the roof that takes room and casts shade: a box
such as a stair room or a plant room, a tank, a wall, a tree, or a building
beside the roof. Each one counts three times:

- No module stands on it or within its **Clearance**.
- The area its shadow crosses in the shade-free hours of the design day is
  kept free of modules, unless **Allow shaded positions** is on in
  [Spacing](/docs/rooftop/steps/array#spacing).
- Its shadow counts, hour by hour over the year, in the shade of each module
  it falls on.

The section reads **Obstacles · &lt;n&gt;**. Closed, it lists the names in
order, those of one group counted once: `Stair room, water tank, 4 AC units`
on the demo, `None on the roof` with none. Its word is **Yours** once there is
one.

Open, each obstacle has a row: its kind's icon, its name and
`<Kind> · <size> · <h> m high`. Obstacles of one kind, size and height drawn
together share one row (`AC units`, `4 boxes · 1.5 × 1.0 m · 1.2 m high`) until
one of them is selected. Press a row to select it. While the parapet is over
0, the list ends with `Parapet, all edges` and its height, set in Edges and
access.

### Add an obstacle

Obstacles drawn on Find are already here; see [Add the
obstacles](/docs/rooftop/roof/find#add-the-obstacles). To add one on the Roof step:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose its kind

Under "Add one: choose its kind, then place it on the roof.", press **Box**,
**Tank**, **Wall**, **Tree** or **Building**. A draft named `<Kind> <n>` stands
at the roof's centre, with no height.
</Step>
<Step>
### Place it

Press the roof where it stands. The draft moves there.
</Step>
<Step>
### Type its height

The draft's card opens with the focus in **Height above the roof**. Type the
height above the roof surface, never above the ground.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add it

Press **Add the box** (or tank, wall, tree, building), or Enter. The obstacle
is added and selected. **Add** does nothing while there is no height;
**Cancel** drops the draft.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Its fields

A selected obstacle's card has its name (type to rename it), its kind, a bin
to remove it, and its fields. By kind, in the card's order:

| Kind | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Box | Length, Width, Height above the roof, Rotation, East, North |
| Tank | Diameter, Height above the roof, East, North |
| Tree | Crown diameter, Height above the roof, Crown starts at, East, North |
| Wall | Height above the roof, Thickness, its points |
| Building | Height above the roof, its points |

| Field | Default | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Length**, **Width** | 2 m each | 0.1–500 m | The box's sides |
| **Diameter** | 2 m | 0.1–100 m | The tank's diameter |
| **Crown diameter** | 4 m | 0.1–100 m | The tree's crown. The tree's footprint and its kept-clear space are the whole crown |
| **Height above the roof** | None: type it | 0.05–200 m | Its top, above the roof surface |
| **Crown starts at** | 2 m | 0–100 m | The trunk, 0.3 m across, casts shade up to here; the crown casts it from here to the height |
| **Thickness** | 0.2 m | 0.05–5 m | The wall's thickness |
| **Rotation** | 0° | −180 to 180°, whole degrees | The box's turn, counter-clockwise |
| **East**, **North** | Where it was placed | −5,000 to 5,000 m | Its position, in metres east and north |

A wall's and a building's points are a table, **Point**, **East**,
**North**, with **Add a point** and a bin on each point: "Type each point in
metres east and north of the roof's centre, in order." A wall keeps at least
2 points, a building 3. A draft wall is 6 m long, east to west; a draft
building 10 × 10 m, from 3 to 13 m east of the centre.

A building beside the roof takes room on it only where it, with its
clearance, overlaps the roof. Its shade counts all the same.

### Move or resize it on the roof

Select a box, a tank or a tree and drag it. A label gives
`East <x> m · North <y> m`; the move snaps to 0.1 m and stays inside the
roof's bounding box, and the drop is one edit. Walls and buildings move by
their points in the list.

A selected box has handles: one at each top corner resizes it with the
opposite corner held; a turn handle on a stem off its north edge turns it,
snapping to a right angle within 3°; a height handle on a stem up from its
middle sets its height. Sizes snap to 0.1 m. A tank or a tree has one handle
at the east of its edge, which sets its diameter.

### Heights to review

A drawing may give no height for some obstacles. A note at the top of the
column then says "The file has no height for these obstacles. The app uses
1.0 m.", with `<n> to review` and **Review**, which selects the first. Its row
carries a note mark, "Height to review", and its card says "Not in the file.
Enter the real height." with **Next**. A typed height takes the obstacle off
the list. [Import a drawing](/docs/rooftop/roof/find#import-a-drawing) covers how
heights are read from a file.

## When something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "The outline cannot cross itself. The roof stays as it was." | A corner was dragged so that the outline crosses itself, or closes to under 1 m² | Drag the corner along the roof's edge, in order around it |
| "This outline covers &lt;area&gt; m². SolarLayout designs one roof at a time, up to 30,000 m² (about 3 MWp). For a larger site, outline each building as its own design." | The roof is over the limit | Outline each building as a design of its own |
| "The roof outline crosses itself. Give the corners in order around the roof." | The layout cannot use the outline | Edit the corners in Plan, in order around the roof |
| "The roof outline encloses no area. Give at least three corners that are not on one line." | The outline has no area | Give the roof at least 3 corners not on one line |
| **Energy not computed**, "The roof has no room for a module inside the edge margin and clear of shade. Check the outline, the edge margin and the obstacles.", **Open Roof** | No module fits | Lower the edge margin or the clearance, check obstacle heights, or turn on **Allow shaded positions** in Spacing |
| **Layout not updated**, the cause, **Try again** | A layout failed; the last positions stay on the stage | Press **Try again** |
| "The file has no height for these obstacles. The app uses 1.0 m." | An imported drawing gave no height | Press **Review** and type each real height |
| "The highest is &lt;max&gt; &lt;unit&gt;." or "The lowest is &lt;min&gt; &lt;unit&gt;." | A typed value was out of range; the field is set to the end of its range | Type a value inside the range |
| "This browser cannot draw the roof in 3D." | The browser cannot draw 3D; a flat plan stands in | Use another browser for the 3D view |

More cases are in [Troubleshooting](/docs/rooftop/reference/troubleshooting#roof-and-array).
