SolarLayout Rooftop runs in the browser, on a phone, a tablet or a computer.
There is nothing to set up: open its address and start a design, or open the
demo to look around first.

Free, no account needed. Your designs are kept on this device. A design on one
device is not on another: the project file carries it across.

## Open Rooftop

Open [rooftop.solarlayout.app](https://rooftop.solarlayout.app) in the browser.
Home opens: the title **Rooftop PV design**, the line "Outline a roof on the
map. The app lays out the modules, the shade, the strings and the energy of a
year.", and under it the search, the largest thing on the page.

<Screenshot id="rooftop-home" />

The search is a field, **Address or coordinates**, with **Find the roof** beside
it. Under the search stand the other ways to start a design:

| Way in | What it does | See |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Find the roof** | Searches the address typed, or reads the coordinates or the maps link typed, and opens the map at the place. With nothing typed it opens the map at its first step | [Find the building](/docs/rooftop/find#find-the-building) |
| **Use my location** | Opens the map where this device is, when you are at the building. The browser must allow the app your location | [Find the roof](/docs/rooftop/find) |
| **Enter the roof by hand** | Opens the roof's place and shape as fields, with no map | [Enter the roof by hand](/docs/rooftop/find#enter-the-roof-by-hand) |
| **Import a drawing** | Opens a KMZ, KML or DXF drawing of the roof as a design | [Import a drawing](/docs/rooftop/find#import-a-drawing) |
| **Open a project file** | Opens a design downloaded from the app as one file (`.rtd`) | [Open a project file](/docs/rooftop/projects#open-a-project-file) |

**Docs** at the top right of Home opens these pages in a new tab, on each size.

### Start from an address

<Steps>
<Step>
### Type the address

Type an address or a place in **Address or coordinates**. Coordinates in
decimals, latitude first, or a link from a maps site work too.
</Step>
<Step>
### Search for it

Press **Find the roof**, or Enter. The app lists up to 5 places it found, each
with its kind. Coordinates and a maps link need no search: the list reads them
back as one row, **Coordinates**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the place

Press the place. The map opens on it, close enough to outline the roof, and the
column says **Outline the roof**.
</Step>
</Steps>

A place found by its address or its name names the design. The design takes
the place's whole label, such as "Pune, Maharashtra, India", and the part before
the first comma, "Pune", is its place name, which Present shows. Coordinates, a
maps link and **Use my location** give no name: the design is called "Untitled
roof" until you rename it.

On a phone, **Find the roof** stands under the field, as wide as it.

<Screenshot id="rooftop-home-phone" maxWidth={390} />

Home is laid out for three sizes: a phone under 640 px wide, a tablet from 640
to 1100 px, and a computer from 1101 px. So is each screen of the app. See
[On a phone or a tablet](/docs/rooftop/steps#on-a-phone-or-a-tablet).

## Add it to the home screen

Rooftop can stand on the home screen of a phone or a tablet like an app. Its
icon is named **Rooftop**, and it opens without the browser's bars, on the
whole screen, clear of the clock, the notch and the home bar.

The app does not ask to be added. Use the browser's own command for it: in
Safari on an iPhone or an iPad, press **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Other
browsers have the command in their menu.

## Try the demo

The last card on Home is **Try the demo**: "A finished design on a 90 × 40 m
roof in Pune, to look around before you start your own." Press
**Open the demo**. It opens in the Studio, on the Roof step at first, and
runs.

| Item | The demo |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Pune demo roof, place name Pune |
| Place | 18.52° N, 73.86° E |
| Roof | Flat, rows facing south: a 90 × 40 m rectangle, its long side facing south |
| Obstacles | A stair room (5 × 4 m, 3 m high), a water tank (2.5 m across, 2 m high), and four AC units (1.5 × 1 m, 1.2 m high) |
| Array | Two modules in each row's height; the rest at the app's defaults |
| Module | INA-144MHC-TF-560, 560 Wp |
| Inverter | WP-330KTL-H1, 275 kW, 6 MPPT |
| Weather | A typical year from PVGIS, carried with the demo |
| System voltage | 1500 V, set as your choice |

The first time it opens, the Roof step says **The array is on the roof** with
"638 modules · 357.28 kWp", then **617.8 MWh in year 1** with "The energy
follows each change you make."

Change anything in the demo to see what it does. The demo is not kept on the
device until its first change; from then on it is a design like any other,
under **Your designs · on this device** on Home. The Studio, Performance and
Handover, opened with no design remembered on this device, open the demo.

## Where your designs are kept

Free, no account needed. Your designs are kept on this device: in this
browser, on this phone, tablet or computer. Nothing is kept on a server.

- **Saved by itself.** A design is saved 600 ms after your last change, after
  an undo or a redo, and at once when you leave the page or close the tab.
  There is no Save button.
- **The saved mark.** Beside the design's name in the top bar a mark says the
  state: a green dot, **Saved on this device**; a small ring, **Saving**; a
  hollow dot, **Not saved**, for the demo or a design before its first change,
  and for one deleted from Home while it is open. A note mark with **Not saved** means the browser refused
  to keep the design: see [Notes to review](/docs/rooftop/steps#notes-to-review).
- **The results are not kept.** A design opened again runs again: the layout,
  the shade, the options and the energy are worked out afresh.
- **After a reload**, the design that was open opens again.
- **On another device or in another browser** your designs are not there. To
  move one, download its project file on the first device and open it on the
  second.

<Callout type="info">
A design is kept in this browser only. To keep a copy anywhere else, or to send
it to a colleague, download its project file: **Download the project file** in
the menu under the design's name. See
[Your designs and the project file](/docs/rooftop/projects).
</Callout>

### What leaves the device

The design is kept on the device, but the app's server does the computing. Each
change sends the design to it, and the server sends back the layout, the
options and the energy. The server keeps no designs and has no accounts. It
holds recent weather, shading results and report pages in its memory, never on
disk, until it stops, and it logs one line per request:
the address asked, the answer's status and the time taken, kept 30 days.

| Goes to | When | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The app's server | Each change, the Sun view, each Handover file, opening a drawing or a project file | The design: its name, the roof and its obstacles with their latitude and longitude, the array, the losses, your own equipment and weather files, the system voltage |
| The map's imagery and address search (Esri) | Find's map; the Studio's ground while **Imagery** is on; words typed in a search | The area on the map; the words typed |
| Building outlines (Overture Maps) | Only while **Detect building** is the tool on Find | The area on the map |
| PVGIS, or NASA POWER (asked by the app's server) | When the weather of a site is loaded | The latitude and longitude |
| The browser's location | Only when you press **Use my location** | Read by the browser; the place goes with the design built there |

The app has no analytics and writes no cookies.

## What the browser needs

The app asks three things of the browser:

**To draw in 3D (WebGL).** The roof, the Sun view and Present are drawn in 3D,
and Find's map needs it too. Without it:

| Where | The app says | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Studio and Present | "This browser cannot draw the roof in 3D." | A plan of the roof, north up, stands in for the 3D view |
| The Studio and Present, when the browser stops drawing | "The browser stopped drawing the roof in 3D." | The same plan stands in |
| Find | "This browser cannot draw the map" / "Try another browser, or enter the roof by hand." | The column opens on **Enter the roof by hand** |

**To keep data on this device.** Designs are kept in the browser's own store.
A browser that does not allow it cannot keep designs: the saved mark shows the
note and **Not saved**, and the note says "The design could not be saved on
this device: this browser cannot save designs." The app also remembers a few choices on the device, such as the
steps you have opened and the work column hidden; a browser that refuses only
forgets them.

**A connection.** The app's server lays out the roof and runs the energy. With
no connection the result says "The server could not be reached." and "Check
your connection, then try again.", with **Try again**. When a result came
before, it stays on the screen, marked "Showing the last result", and the
cause reads "The server could not be reached after your last change. The
result shown is from before the change."

For a message the app shows and what to do, see
[Troubleshooting](/docs/rooftop/troubleshooting#anywhere-in-the-app).
