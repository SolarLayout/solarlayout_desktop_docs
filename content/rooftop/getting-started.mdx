SolarLayout Rooftop runs in the browser on a phone, a tablet or a computer.
Free, no account needed. Your designs are kept on this device.

## Open Rooftop

<Steps>
<Step>

### Go to the app

Open [rooftop.solarlayout.app](https://rooftop.solarlayout.app) in any
browser. Home opens: **Find your roof. Design it in 3D.**

</Step>
<Step>

### Start a design

Type an address, paste coordinates or a maps link into the search, and
click **Find my roof**. Find opens at that place, ready for you to outline
the roof.

</Step>
<Step>

### Or start from a file

**Import a drawing** reads a KMZ, KML or DXF with the roof and its
obstacles. **Open a project** reads an `.rtd` file saved by Rooftop, from
this device or a colleague.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="rooftop-home" />

## Add it to the home screen

In the browser's menu, choose **Add to Home Screen**. Rooftop then opens
from its own icon, named **Rooftop**, without the browser's bars, and uses
the whole screen on an iPhone or an iPad.

## Try the demo

**Open the demo** on Home opens the *Pune demo roof*: a 90 × 40 m flat roof
with tilted rows, a stair room, a water tank and four AC units, with its
module, inverter and weather already chosen. The demo's capacity and energy
appear on its card after it has run once on this device.

## Where your designs are kept

Every design is kept in this browser, on this device. To take one to another
device or a colleague, save its project file.

| What | How it works |
|---|---|
| Saving | Automatic, a moment after each edit. The mark before the design's name in the top bar says **Saved on this device** when you point at it. |
| The demo, a new design | Kept from their first edit. |
| Results | Not kept. A design you open runs again. |
| Another device or browser | Not there. Use **Save a copy** to download the `.rtd` project file, then **Open a project** on the other device. |
| Deleting | From the design's card on Home: **Delete**. It is kept on this device alone, so it is gone. |

<Callout type="info">
A colleague opens the same `.rtd` file with **Open a project**. It opens as
a new design on their device and runs again there.
</Callout>

## What the browser needs

| Need | Without it |
|---|---|
| 3D drawing (WebGL) | "This browser cannot draw the roof in 3D." The roof is shown as a plan instead. |
| Room to keep data for the site | Designs cannot be kept. The top bar shows **Not saved**, and the notes give the browser's reason. |
