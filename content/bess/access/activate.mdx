---
title: Activate this device
description: Add this device to your account's access from the License window, complete it in your browser, and confirm it with Refresh.
---

Activation adds **this device** to your account's access — there is no file
to send or receive. Open the License window, copy this device's Device ID,
request access, and complete it in your browser. Coming back and clicking
**Refresh** confirms it. Access belongs to your account and covers up to
three devices; see [How access works](/docs/bess/access/overview).

You can also see this at a glance without opening the License window: the
header banner always shows an access-status chip. On a device that has never
been activated it reads **●  No active access**, with a **Get Free Access**
button right beside it that does exactly what the License window's button
does.

Until then, **▶  Simulate**, **⚙  Optimise** and every export won't run — the
app offers to open the License window instead. Everything else in the
application works. See [How access works](/docs/bess/access/overview) for the
full list of what stays free.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Help ▸ License…

The License window opens. On a device with no active access, the status
headline reads **✗  No access**, with the message *"No active access for this
device. Click 'Get Free Access' to get started."* beneath it.

Below that: the read-only **Your Device ID (identifies this computer to
SolarLayout:)** field with its **Copy** button, then **Get Free Access**,
**Contact Us**, **Refresh** and **Close**.

<Screenshot id="bess-access-license-window" />
</Step>

<Step>
### Copy the Device ID

Click **Copy** beside the Device ID field rather than reading the value off
the screen. It goes straight to the clipboard.
</Step>

<Step>
### Click Get Free Access

The status headline changes at once to **⏳  Waiting for activation**, and a
page opens in your browser, addressed to this device's Device ID.
</Step>

<Step>
### Sign in and activate in the browser

Sign in — or create an account — if the page asks, then click **Activate free
access for this device**. On your second or third computer the page says
*"This will be device 2 of 3 on your account."* and the button reads **Add
this device** instead.

If the page reads **You have used all 3 devices** or **This device is already
in use**, activation stops there — see
[How access works](/docs/bess/access/overview) for what each means and what
to do.

<Screenshot id="bess-access-web-activation" />
</Step>

<Step>
### Return and click Refresh

Back in the License window, click **Refresh**. It re-checks straight away,
bypassing any cached result, so a change made moments ago on the server
side shows up immediately. The window also checks again on its own whenever
it regains focus.

A device with active access shows **✓  Active**, with **Access Details**,
**Refresh** and **Close** in place of the earlier buttons.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
If the status reads **✗  No access** with a message about access having
ended or been revoked, **Get Free Access** will not help — see
[Access problems](/docs/bess/access/troubleshooting) or contact SolarLayout at
[sales@solarlayout.app](mailto:sales@solarlayout.app).
</Callout>
