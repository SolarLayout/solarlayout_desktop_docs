---
title: Symbols and custom symbols
description: Opening the symbol library on the Single Line Diagram tab, building your own symbol from a selection, and deleting one you've added.
---

Three controls on the **Single Line Diagram** tab manage the symbol palette
used by [Generate SLD](/bess/plant/generate-sld) and by hand-drawing:
opening the full library, turning a selection into a new reusable symbol,
and deleting a symbol you've added.

## Opening the symbol library

**🧩  Open Symbol Library…** opens a window showing a scrollable, 4-column
grid of symbol thumbnails. Clicking a thumbnail arms it — the status line
confirms which symbol is armed and prompts a click on the canvas. A click on
the **Single Line Diagram** canvas then places it there.

## Making a symbol from a selection

**➕  Make Symbol from Selection** — available both on the toolbar and inside
the Symbol Library window — turns the currently selected drawn objects (line,
rectangle, circle, polygon or text) into a new reusable symbol. The
selection is flattened, centred and scaled uniformly into a standard-size
box, then you're prompted for a name before it's saved into the library.

## Deleting a custom symbol

**🗑  Delete Custom**, inside the Symbol Library window, deletes the
currently selected symbol — but only if it's one you saved or imported
yourself. Selecting a built-in symbol and clicking **🗑  Delete Custom**
instead shows **Click an imported symbol first, then Delete.**
