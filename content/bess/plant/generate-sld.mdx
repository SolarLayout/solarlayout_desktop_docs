---
title: Generating the SLD
description: What Generate SLD draws — the same plant topology as Generate Layout, redrawn as IEC schematic symbols.
---

**⚙  Generate SLD**, on the **Single Line Diagram** tab, re-runs the same
[equipment auto-placement](/docs/bess/plant/generate-layout) as **⚙  Generate
Layout**, then draws the result again — not as scaled rectangles this time,
but as IEC schematic symbols on the same connections.

<Screenshot id="bess-plant-generate-sld-result" />

## From rectangle to symbol

Each equipment type is redrawn as its own symbol:

| Equipment | Drawn as |
| --- | --- |
| Containers | A battery symbol |
| PCS | A PCS symbol |
| Transformer | A two-winding, three-winding or five-winding transformer symbol, matching the selected **Transformer Type** |
| LV panels | A rectangle at its real footprint size |
| MV panels | A rectangle at its real footprint size |

Containers, PCS and the transformer are each drawn as a fixed 3 m × 3 m
symbol, regardless of the equipment's real footprint on the **Plant Layout**
tab. LV and MV panels are not converted to symbols — they stay rectangles at
their actual length and width.

## Why the wires still reach

A symbol is smaller than the full-size rectangle its connections were
originally routed to. To keep the wires from stopping short at the edge of
that larger, no-longer-drawn rectangle, any connection that lands on a
symbol's top or bottom edge is re-snapped to the symbol's own top or bottom
terminal.
