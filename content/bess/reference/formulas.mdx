These are the formulas behind the DFR-shortfall penalty, the CUF cap, grid
charging and working-capital costs, and the return metrics on the
**💹 Financials** tab — worded the way Help ▸ **User Guide / Formulas…**
states them inside the application itself. Every figure is derived from the
15-minute dispatch simulation, rolled up into an annual cash-flow model. For
a one-line definition of each return metric instead of its formula, see
[Return metrics](/bess/financials/metrics).

## Return and value metrics

**IRR** (Internal Rate of Return) — the discount rate at which project NPV
equals zero.

**Equity IRR** — the IRR of the equity cash-flow stream:

```
equity cash flow = EBITDA − interest − principal − tax (when levered) − reinvestment
```

In the final year this also adds terminal value and any DSRA release, net
of remaining debt. Computed only when **Enable debt (levered analysis)** is
on.

**NPV** (Net Present Value) —

```
NPV = sum, over every year, of (that year's cash flow ÷ (1 + discount rate) ^ year)
```

at the **Discount Rate (NPV)**.

**Payback** — the year cumulative project cash flow first turns
non-negative, linearly interpolated within that year. **Discounted
Payback** applies the same rule to the discounted-cumulative cash flow
instead.

**DSCR** (Debt-Service Coverage Ratio) —

```
DSCR = (EBITDA − tax (when levered) − reinvestment) ÷ (interest + principal)
```

for the year, reported as a minimum and an average across the debt tenor.

**DSRA** (Debt-Service Reserve Account) —

```
DSRA = (DSRA months ÷ 12) × (first year's interest + principal)
```

**MoIC** (Multiple on Invested Capital) —

```
MoIC = (sum of positive cash inflows) ÷ initial outlay
```

Initial outlay is Equity plus DSRA on a levered project, Total CAPEX
otherwise.

**PI** (Profitability Index) —

```
PI = (NPV + CAPEX) ÷ CAPEX
```

A PI above 1 is value-accretive.

**WACC** (Weighted Average Cost of Capital) —

```
WACC = (equity share of funding × cost of equity) + (debt share of funding × interest rate × (1 − tax rate))
```

Cost of equity is taken as the **Discount Rate (NPV)**. Computed only for
levered projects.

<Callout type="info">
The application shows two different IRR figures and two different NPV
figures, from two different cash-flow streams — the headline figures carry
no debt, tax or salvage value, while the report's own figures do. See
[Return metrics](/bess/financials/metrics) for which figure appears where.
</Callout>

## Cost per unit of energy

**LCOE** (Levelised Cost of Energy) —

```
LCOE = (Total CAPEX + sum of discounted (OPEX + reinvestment) across years) ÷ sum of discounted (energy × 1000) across years
```

in Rs/kWh, on the energy basis chosen by **LCOE Energy Basis** on the
**🏦 Finance** tab:

| Basis | Energy in the denominator |
| --- | --- |
| **Energy delivered to load (default)** | Energy served to the Contracted Capacity |
| **Total generation (solar+wind)** | All generation, including exported energy |
| **Delivered + exported energy** | All useful energy sold |

**LCOS** (Levelised Cost of Storage) — the same shape as LCOE, but over
BESS-only CAPEX, OPEX and reinvestment, divided by discounted
battery-discharge throughput instead of delivered energy — the cost of
every kWh that actually passes through the battery.

## Revenue, penalty and cost formulas

**DFR-shortfall penalty** — per month, for each of the Peak, Off-Peak,
Overall and 15-minute buckets:

```
shortfall (MWh) = max(target DFR × required MWh − delivered MWh, 0)
penalty = Penalty Multiplier × PPA Tariff × shortfall × 1000
```

That month's penalty is the greatest of (Peak penalty + Off-Peak penalty),
the Overall penalty, or the 15-minute penalty.

**Annual CUF cap** — when **Apply Annual CUF Cap** is on:

```
cap (MWh) = cap % × Contracted Capacity × 8760
```

Energy up to the cap is paid at the **PPA Tariff**; energy above it is paid
at the Export Price instead.

**Grid-charging cost** —

```
grid-charging cost = (sum, across the year, of grid-import MWh × Grid Charging Price × 1000) × an escalation factor
```

Applies always on a Standalone project; only as worst-case backup on a
generation-backed one.

**Payment-delay / working-capital carrying cost** —

```
receivables = annual revenue × (Receivable Lag ÷ 365)
working-capital carrying cost = receivables × (Working-Capital Rate + WC Facility Fee − Late-Payment Surcharge)
```

Deductible, so it reduces EBITDA — and tax — each year. Zero when
**Receivable Lag** is 0.
