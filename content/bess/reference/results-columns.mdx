---
title: Result table columns
description: Every column of the DFR Table tab and the Financials tab's per-year table — the exact header, in order, and what each one reports.
---

Two result tables carry the application's per-period detail: the
**📋 DFR Table** tab reports one row per calendar month of Year 1, and the
**💹 Financials** tab's per-year table reports one row per project year.
Both are fixed order, left to right — the order below matches the columns
on screen.

## DFR Table columns

One row per calendar month of Year 1. In depth:
[The DFR table](/docs/bess/results/dfr-table).

| Column | What it reports |
| --- | --- |
| **Month** | The calendar month, Year 1 |
| **15min%** | That month's Delivery Fulfilment Ratio (DFR) at the 15-minute granularity |
| **Total%** | That month's Overall DFR |
| **Peak%** | That month's DFR during Peak hours |
| **Off-Pk%** | That month's DFR during Off-Peak hours |
| **15m OK** | Whether the 15-minute DFR met target that month |
| **Pk OK** | Whether the Peak DFR met target that month |
| **OffPk OK** | Whether the Off-Peak DFR met target that month |
| **Mon OK** | Whether the Overall Monthly DFR met target that month |
| **ShortPk MWh** | The MWh shortfall at Peak granularity, on a month that missed |
| **ShortOpk MWh** | The MWh shortfall at Off-Peak granularity, on a month that missed |
| **Short15m MWh** | The MWh shortfall at 15-minute granularity, on a month that missed |
| **Penalty Lac** | That month's DFR-shortfall penalty, in Rs Lakhs |
| **Pen%Rev** | That month's penalty as a share of revenue |
| **Export MWh** | Exported energy for the month |
| **Export Rev Lac** | Export revenue for the month, in Rs Lakhs |

That's 16 columns, in a fixed order. A row is shaded green when Peak,
Off-Peak, Overall and 15-minute DFR all met target that month, and red when
any one of the four missed.

## Financials per-year columns

One row per project year. In depth:
[The financials table](/docs/bess/results/financials).

| Column | What it reports |
| --- | --- |
| **Yr** | The project year |
| **Deg%** | That year's Solar/Wind generation degradation |
| **SOH%** | That year's battery state of health |
| **BESS MWh** | Energy the battery handled that year |
| **Sched MWh** | Energy scheduled — delivered against the Contracted Capacity — that year |
| **Export MWh** | Energy exported that year |
| **Export Rev Lac** | Export revenue for the year, in Rs Lakhs |
| **Gross Rev Cr** | Gross revenue for the year, in Rs Crore |
| **Pen Lac** | That year's DFR-shortfall penalty, in Rs Lakhs |
| **Pen%Rev** | That year's penalty as a share of revenue |
| **OPEX Lac** | That year's operating cost, in Rs Lakhs |
| **Grid MWh** | Grid energy used to charge the battery that year |
| **Grid %Exp** | That year's grid-charging energy as a share of export |
| **Grid Chg Cr** | The cost of that year's grid charging, in Rs Crore |
| **WC/Delay Cr** | That year's payment-delay working-capital cost, in Rs Crore |
| **Repl/Aug Cr** | That year's battery replacement or augmentation cost, in Rs Crore |
| **EBITDA Cr** | EBITDA for the year, in Rs Crore |
| **Interest Cr** | Interest for the year, in Rs Crore |
| **Tax Cr** | Tax for the year, in Rs Crore |
| **NCF Cr** | Net cash flow for the year, in Rs Crore |
| **Equity CF Cr** | Equity cash flow for the year, in Rs Crore |
| **DSCR** | Debt-Service Coverage Ratio for the year |

That's 22 columns, in a fixed order. A year's row is shaded light red when
its net cash flow is negative, and yellow when it's a replacement or
augmentation year — a negative-NCF year takes the red shading even when
it's also a replacement/augmentation year. Independently, a row's own
figures are painted red wherever any of Gross Revenue, EBITDA, NCF, Equity
Cash Flow, Interest, Tax, Penalty, OPEX, Export Revenue or DSCR is negative
that year.
