---
title: Generation degradation
description: The Year-1 and Year-2-onward loss applied to Solar and Wind generation over the project life.
---

The **📉 Degrad.** tab sets how much Solar and Wind generation itself
declines over the project life — separate from the battery's own state-of-health
degradation on the **🔋 BESS** tab.

<Screenshot id="bess-inputs-degradation" />

## Fields

| Field | Default | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Generation Loss Year-1` | 1.0% | 0–5% | Solar/Wind generation loss in Year 1 |
| `Generation Loss Year-2+` | 0.4%/yr | 0–2%/yr | Annual generation loss from Year 2 onward |

The tab's own caption describes the two figures as compounding — each
year's generation factor is (1 minus the Year-1 loss) multiplied by (1 minus
the Year-2+ loss) raised to the power of (year minus 1) — and captions the
shipped defaults as "1% Year-1, 0.4%/yr thereafter (IEC 61215 standard module
degradation)".
