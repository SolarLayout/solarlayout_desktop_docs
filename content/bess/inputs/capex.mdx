---
title: CAPEX
description: Capital cost per installed MW of Solar and Wind and per installed MWh of BESS.
---

The **💰 CAPEX** tab sets the capital cost the financial model charges for
each source, in Crore per unit installed — 1 Crore is Rs 1,00,00,000.

<Screenshot id="bess-inputs-capex" />

## Fields

| Field | Default | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Solar CAPEX** | 5.0 Cr/MW | ≥ 0.1 Cr/MW | Solar capital cost per installed MW |
| **Wind CAPEX** | 7.0 Cr/MW | ≥ 0.1 Cr/MW | Wind capital cost per installed MW |
| **BESS CAPEX** | 1.5 Cr/MWh | ≥ 0.1 Cr/MWh | BESS capital cost per installed MWh |

<Callout type="warn">
The tab's own caption reads "Defaults: Solar 5 Cr/MW, Wind 7 Cr/MW, BESS 1
Cr/MWh" — but the **BESS CAPEX** field itself shows **1.5 Cr/MWh** on a fresh
launch. Treat 1.5 Cr/MWh as the real default; the caption's "1" is stale.
</Callout>
