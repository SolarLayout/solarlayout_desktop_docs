---

The **📈 Dashboard** tab draws a single chart: a header strip of headline
figures above six panels, covering generation, the battery, DFR performance
and the cash flow the run produced.

## The header strip

A navy bar above the panels reports the run's sizing on the left — Solar,
Wind and BESS — and two headline results on the right — IRR and Contracted
Capacity.

## The six panels

<Screenshot id="bess-results-dashboard" />

1. **Total Generation vs Contracted Capacity  ─  7-day scrollable window** — a stacked solar/wind generation fill, a total-generation line against the Contracted Capacity line, a Scheduled/Delivered line, and an exported-surplus fill.
2. **Battery Charge / Discharge & State of Charge  ─  7-day scrollable window** — charge, discharge and grid-charge fills on the left axis, state of charge (SOC, in MWh) on a twin right axis. Any day with grid charging is shaded purple on both this panel and the one above it.
3. **Monthly DFR Performance** — grouped Overall, Peak and Off-Peak DFR percentage bars, one group per month, against their target lines.
4. **Monthly DFR Penalty  (Red = Target Missed)** — that month's penalty in Rs Lakhs, as a bar: green when Peak, Off-Peak and Overall all passed that month, red otherwise.
5. **Annual Revenue vs OPEX** — Gross Revenue, OPEX and Penalty bars, plus a net cash-flow line, one point per project year.
6. **Cumulative Cashflow  ─  IRR = …** — the "…" is that run's own IRR figure, filled into the title. The panel draws annual and cumulative cashflow bars and a line, a payback-year marker, and an IRR badge.

<Callout type="info">
The IRR badge on panel 6 is coloured green at 15% or above and red below
it — a fixed benchmark, not the **Project Target IRR** field on the
**📊 OPEX** tab. The two only agree when that field is left at its own
default.
</Callout>

## The 7-day scroll

Panels 1 and 2 default to a 7-day window rather than the full year. A slider
labelled **Day**, beneath them, scrolls both panels together across all 365
days, with an instruction beneath it reminding you to drag it.

## Maximize

**⛶  Maximize**, next to the chart's toolbar, opens the same six panels in an
enlarged window of their own — the most comfortable way to read the detail
in each one. Press Esc, or close that window, to bring the chart back into
the tab.
