---
title: The DFR table
description: The DFR Table tab's column groups — delivery, met/missed, shortfalls, penalty and export — and its green/red row shading.
---

The **📋 DFR Table** tab reports one row per calendar month of Year 1, once a
run finishes. Each row carries that month's
[Delivery Fulfilment Ratio](/bess/concepts/dfr) (DFR) at every granularity the
application tracks, whether each target was met, the shortfall on the months
that missed, that month's penalty, and its export figures.

<Screenshot id="bess-results-dfr-table" />

## The column groups

Every row starts with the calendar **Month**, then five groups of columns,
left to right:

| Group | Columns | What it reports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery % | **15min%**, **Total%**, **Peak%**, **Off-Pk%** | That month's DFR at the 15-minute, Overall, Peak and Off-Peak granularities |
| Met/missed flags | **15m OK**, **Pk OK**, **OffPk OK**, **Mon OK** | Whether each of those four granularities met its target that month |
| Shortfalls | **ShortPk MWh**, **ShortOpk MWh**, **Short15m MWh** | The MWh gap, at Peak, Off-Peak and 15-minute granularity, on a month that missed |
| Penalty | **Penalty Lac**, **Pen%Rev** | That month's DFR shortfall penalty, in Rs Lakhs, and its share of revenue |
| Export | **Export MWh**, **Export Rev Lac** | Exported energy and its revenue for the month |

That's sixteen columns in total, in a fixed order. The exhaustive column
reference — the exact header for each one — lives in the reference section of
this documentation rather than being repeated here.

## Row shading

A row is shaded green when that month's Peak, Off-Peak, Overall **and**
15-minute DFR all met target, and red when any one of the four missed. The
colour down the tab is a faster read than the individual OK flags — scan for
red first, then check which flag in that row explains it.

Hovering over any column heading shows a short tooltip explaining what it
reports.
