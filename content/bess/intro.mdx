---
title: What BESS Desktop does
description: A Windows application that sizes and financially models hybrid renewable-energy and battery-storage projects, from a dispatch simulation to a report.
---

BESS Desktop is a Windows application for sizing and financially modelling
hybrid renewable-energy and battery-storage projects — solar, wind, or both,
paired with a battery energy storage system. You give it how much the plant
generates and what it is contracted to deliver. It runs a 15-minute annual
dispatch simulation of the battery, builds a year-by-year financial model from
the result, and produces a financial report and the plant's own drawings.

It opens fully usable, with no start-up window and no activation check before
the input tabs. Only running an analysis, or exporting from it, needs the
device activated — see [Getting started](/bess/getting-started).

## What you give it

| Tab | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **📂 Data** | The project type — which sources feed the battery — and, unless you choose the grid-only project type, a generation profile as a CSV |
| **⏰ Peak** | The hours of the day the plant is on peak, and the hours it is off peak |
| **📐 Sizing** | The Contracted Capacity the plant must serve, and the delivery targets it is measured against |
| **🔋 BESS**, **📉 Degrad.** | The battery's efficiency, depth of discharge, end-of-life and degradation assumptions |
| **💰 CAPEX**, **📊 OPEX**, **🏦 Finance** | Capital cost, operating cost, tariffs, and financing terms |

Every one of these ships with a working default — see
[Your first analysis](/bess/first-analysis).

## What it produces

- **A sized plant.** Solar, Wind and BESS capacities — either the ones you
  entered, or the mix an automated search finds for you.
- **Delivery performance.** How closely the dispatch meets the Contracted
  Capacity, read from the **📈 Dashboard** and **📋 DFR Table** tabs.
- **A financial model.** IRR, NPV and the full cash flow, re-run year by year
  across the project's life, on the **💹 Financials** tab.
- **Reports.** A multi-page PDF report, a Word document, and supporting CSVs.
- **Plant drawings.** A plant layout and single-line diagram, opened from the
  **🏗 Plant Layout & SLD** button on the banner.
